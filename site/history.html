A quarter century of global fits

Where the six oscillation parameters stood at each step since 2000, for the three groups that have published global analyses continuously — and how much the precision improved along the way.

## What this page will contain For each parameter — δm², Δm², sin²θ₁₂, sin²θ₁₃, sin²θ₂₃ and δ — a timeline of best-fit values and allowed ranges as published by: - the **Bari group**, from 2000 to the present; - the **Valencia group** (de Salas, Forero, Tórtola, Valle and predecessors); - **NuFit** (Esteban, González-García, Maltoni, Schwetz and predecessors). Two things make this harder than plotting numbers, and both are the reason the page is worth building: 1. **The conventions differ.** We use Δm² = m₃² − ½(m₁² + m₂²); the other groups quote Δm²₃₁ or Δm²₂₃. A timeline that overlays them without saying so would be quietly wrong. Every point here will carry the convention it was published in, and every conversion will be stated. 2. **The data behind each point differs.** A 2008 range and a 2025 range are not the same measurement made twice; they are different datasets. The page will say what entered each analysis. ## How the numbers get here From the published papers, and from nowhere else. For each entry: the arXiv identifier, the table it came from, and the convention it uses, recorded in a plain, checkable file (`site-src/data/history.yaml`) that anyone can read against the sources. The χ² files of the older analyses no longer exist, so there is no shortcut: the values are read from the paper tables one by one. A point that cannot be verified against its source is left out — never interpolated, never reconstructed from a figure.

Status

Awaiting the list of papers. The list of Bari analyses to include is being settled first, together with the corresponding Valencia and NuFit releases; only then does the transcription start. Nothing is shown on this page until it has a source behind it.
