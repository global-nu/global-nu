Selection and ranking are deterministic: arXiv's API is queried for the configured categories, every record that names the field is admitted, and those are scored against the fixed keyword list below. No model is involved in choosing what appears here. The split between the two streams is by the preprint's primary arXiv category: hep-ex, nucl-ex, physics.ins-det are read as experimental, everything else as theory, so a phenomenology paper cross-listed to an experimental category still appears under theory.

The 36 words that decide this page

Admitted only if it says one of these — neutrino, antineutrino, lepton, neutrinoless, double beta, seesaw, leptogenesis, majorana, pmns, flavon, pontecorvo

This is a gate, not a weight: a preprint that never names the field is left out however well it scores, and these words count for nothing in the ranking itself. A longer word starting with one of them counts (neutrinophilic, neutrino-argon).

Strong terms, counted double — neutrino, oscillation, neutrinoless, double beta, leptogenesis, seesaw, sterile, mass ordering, mass hierarchy

Supporting terms — flavour symmetry, flavor symmetry, lepton mixing, lepton flavour, lepton flavor, fermion mass, mass matrix, flavon, modular, discrete symmetry, grand unification, leptonic CP, non-standard interaction, reactor, long-baseline, supernova

Among those admitted, a term found in the title counts 3 to the 1 it counts in the abstract, a strong term counts double a supporting one, and the scores add.
