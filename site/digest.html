Ranking is deterministic: arXiv's RSS feeds are queried for the configured categories, and each record is scored against the fixed keyword list below. No model is involved in choosing what appears here. The split between the two streams is by the preprint's primary arXiv category: hep-ex, nucl-ex, physics.ins-det are read as experimental, everything else as theory, so a phenomenology paper cross-listed to an experimental category still appears under theory.

The 19 words the score is computed from

Strong terms, counted double — neutrino, neutrinos, oscillation, neutrinoless, double beta, leptogenesis, seesaw, sterile, mass ordering, mass hierarchy

Supporting terms — flavour symmetry, flavor symmetry, modular, grand unification, leptonic CP, non-standard interaction, reactor, long-baseline, supernova

A term found in the title counts 3 to the 1 it counts in the abstract, a strong term counts double a supporting one, and the scores add.
