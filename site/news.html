Experiments and results

DUNE data-analysis AI

DUNE researchers are developing AI systems to sift through the experiment's data, aiming to identify neutrino interactions, flag signs of a stellar explosion, and spot problems inside the detector as it prepares for data-taking. The collaboration draws on decades of machine-learning work at Fermilab in building these tools.

IceCube probes Earth's interior

IceCube is using atmospheric neutrinos, produced in cosmic-ray-induced air showers, to investigate the structure of the Earth's interior, complementing indirect seismic and gravitational measurements.

IceCube's WavePID tool

IceCube has described WavePID, a tool that identifies the particles produced when a neutrino interacts with the ice from the shape of the resulting light pattern, working at the nanosecond level.

NOvA hydrogen cross section

The NOvA collaboration measured the muon-antineutrino charged-current quasi-elastic cross section on hydrogen using the near detector, drawing on a 1.2×10^21 proton-on-target exposure in the NuMI beam. The analysis selected 35,509 signal events in the hydrogen-rich (10.8%) detector, the highest-statistics sample of (anti)neutrino-hydrogen interactions measured to date, with backgrounds from heavier nuclei constrained using dedicated control samples.

Theory highlights

recently published, with the links each record carries

  • Dey and Dutta study how well NOvA and DUNE, individually and combined, could constrain a scenario in which the third neutrino mass state decays invisibly into a sterile state under the normal mass hierarchy. They find that the assumed true value of the mixing angle θ23 significantly affects the resulting constraint on the decay parameter.

    Constraining invisible neutrino decay at NOνA and DUNE — INSPIRE · DOI

  • Shickele, Jokiniemi, Belley and collaborators combine results from multiple neutrinoless double-beta decay experiments with nuclear matrix elements computed ab initio, via chiral effective field theory and the in-medium similarity renormalization group, to derive global Bayesian limits on the Majorana neutrino mass. In contrast to more phenomenological nuclear models, their ab initio results suggest the current generation of 0νββ experiments has likely not yet reached the sensitivity needed to probe the mass regime of interest.

    Global ab initio neutrino mass limits from neutrinoless double-beta decay — arXiv · INSPIRE · DOI

  • Zaizen reviews the pairwise nature and quasi-steady states of collective neutrino flavor conversion in supernovae, using quantum kinetic equation simulations to show the neutrino system evolves toward asymptotic states through turbulence-like cascades and collisional processes with background matter. This bears directly on modeling flavor conversion in core-collapse supernovae and binary neutron-star mergers.

    Pairwise neutrino flavor conversion in supernovae: quantum kinetics and astrophysical … — INSPIRE · DOI

  • Di Mauro presents a secluded dark-matter model with an extra U(1)_X gauge symmetry containing a Dirac dark-matter particle, three heavy neutrinos, and a singlet scalar that mixes with the Higgs. Because the Higgs-portal mixing arises only at one loop from the same Yukawa structures that generate active neutrino masses, the mixing angle is tied directly to the neutrino mass scale and the heavy-neutrino masses.

    Neutrino mass and secluded dark matter puzzles solved together — INSPIRE · DOI

  • Li, Xue, Wang and collaborators derive an analytical expression for the maximum neutrino flux attainable in the standard one-zone leptohadronic blazar jet model as a function of the observed X-ray flux, addressing the tension between neutrino production and X-ray observational constraints. Applied to a sample of neutrino candidate blazars, their analytical results agree with full numerical modelling.

    On the Maximum Neutrino Flux of Blazars in the One-zone Leptohadronic Model — arXiv · INSPIRE · DOI

  • Majhi, Pal, Ghosh and collaborators compare two formalisms for implementing quantum decoherence in neutrino oscillations, one defined in the matter mass eigenstate basis and one defined in the vacuum basis and rotated into matter, showing the two give different oscillation probabilities and different sensitivity to the decoherence parameter at the future DUNE and P2SO long-baseline experiments.
