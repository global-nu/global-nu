IceCube Nobel Prize

The Royal Swedish Academy of Science announced on 6 October that the 2026 Nobel Prize in Physics goes to Francis Halzen, IceCube principal investigator, for contributions to the IceCube Neutrino Observatory and the discovery of high-energy neutrinos of astrophysical origin. The KM3NeT Collaboration sent its congratulations. The whole South Pole station gathered at the IceCube Lab for a group photo after hearing the news.

JUNO-TAO first data

The Taishan Antineutrino Observatory is a satellite of JUNO, with a 2.8-ton liquid-scintillator detector at 43.2 m from a reactor core. Installation was completed in 2025 and physics data taking began on 10 February 2026. The JUNO Collaboration reports stable cryogenic operation at -50 degrees and about 90% of the readout channels retained, based on commissioning data and the first physics runs.

TeV neutrino cross section

A new analysis of IceCube data determines the muon-neutrino-nucleon charged-current cross section across the TeV scale. The authors state that earlier collider, atmospheric and astrophysical measurements leave uncertainties of about 20-40%, and that no single measurement covers sub-TeV to tens of TeV precisely. The excerpt does not give the new result.

KM3NeT-ANTARES and gravitational waves

The ANTARES and KM3NeT collaborations present two analyses combining LIGO-Virgo gravitational-wave data with ANTARES and ORCA neutrino data from the third observing run. One uses high-energy neutrino candidates as triggers to search for gravitational-wave candidates. The other searches for spatial and temporal coincidences with subthreshold events.

P-ONE photomultipliers

P-ONE is a planned cubic-kilometre-scale neutrino telescope for the Pacific Ocean, using Ocean Networks Canada infrastructure. Its first detector line will carry 320 Hamamatsu R14374-10 3-inch photomultiplier tubes. A new paper reports the characterisation of all tubes for that line before deployment, to check them against the detector acceptance criteria.

Baikal-GVD

Baikal-GVD is under construction in Lake Baikal and is now 70% complete. A search for point-like sources in five years of track-like events, from 2019 to 2024, found no significant neutrino source. T2K near detector

SuperFGD, a key part of the ND280 upgrade, is made of about 2 million optically isolated 1 cm^3 cubes read out by three orthogonal fibres. It provides 3D images of neutrino interactions and detects protons down to about 300 MeV/c. The records also describe a selection of muon-neutrino charged-current interactions with single positive pion production in the upgraded ND280, which the authors present as a step towards reducing T2K's dominant systematic uncertainties.

Neutrino Emissivity in Δ -Direct-Urca Processes Inside Neutron Stars

The paper calculates the neutrino emissivity of Δ-direct-Urca processes in neutron-star matter, a contribution the authors say had not been explicitly treated. Urca processes are leading cooling channels, so the result bears on how neutron-star thermal evolution depends on the presence of Δ isobars.

Constraining ultralight vector bosons via solar neutrino oscillations: The gauged L μ - L τ model and prospects for JUNO and XLZD

The paper uses a gauged U(1) L_mu-L_tau model with an ultralight vector boson that kinetically mixes with the photon. This produces an effective matter potential in the Sun that modifies MSW oscillations. It is constrained with Borexino, SNO and Super-Kamiokande solar data, and prospects for JUNO and XLZD are discussed.

Thermal leptogenesis in the Babu-Nandi-Tavartkiladze model of neutrino mass

The paper studies neutrino mass and thermal leptogenesis in the Babu-Nandi-Tavartkiladze model, where masses arise from a dimension-7 operator at tree level and a dimension-5 operator at one loop. The model accommodates sub-eV neutrino masses with a new-physics scale of order TeV. The authors examine whether thermal leptogenesis works here, which differs from canonical seesaw leptogenesis.

Direct calculation of the two-neutrino double-beta decay rate including the full lepton-energy dependence

The authors compute two-neutrino double-beta decay rates without decoupling nuclear structure from the lepton kinematics, in a single unified formula. They compare the resulting rates and electron spectra with closure, non-closure and Taylor-expansion approximations for 82Se and 136Xe. The comparison matters for anyone using such approximations in double-beta decay analyses.

Paleodetectors for neutrino signals from diverse Galactic stellar collapses

The paper asks whether nuclear recoil tracks in ancient minerals could record neutrinos from past Galactic core-collapse supernovae. It varies neutron-star masses and includes failed supernovae that form black holes, as well as the nuclear equation of state. Sensitivity is found to increase for models with larger neutrino emission.

Magnetorotational Supernova Neutrino Spectra Versus Mass Ordering

The paper considers magnetic and oscillation effects on neutrinos emitted by a protoneutron star in a magnetorotational supernova. The authors find that oscillations in the outer stellar layers harden the electron antineutrino spectrum, most clearly for the inverted mass ordering. They describe this as favourable for ground-based detection of supernova neutrinos.
