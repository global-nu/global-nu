Experiments and results

JUNO oscillation update

The collaboration reported an improved measurement of oscillation parameters and an indication of the neutrino mass ordering. The analysis uses 207.2 live days and 8,294 inverse beta-decay candidates at an average reactor baseline of 52.5 km. It reaches a precision of approximately 1% on the atmospheric mass splitting, with Δm²31 = +2.509 (+0.027/−0.025) × 10⁻³ eV² for normal ordering and −2.482 (+0.025/−0.026) × 10⁻³ eV² for inverted ordering, using the Daya Bay constraint in the fit. The record excerpt does not give a significance for the ordering indication.

DUNE far-site construction

Crews at the Sanford Underground Research Facility have moved about 39% of the I-beam steel needed to construct the cryostat. The steel moved so far, nearly 2 million pounds, completely fills the south cavern. Separately, the DUNE Collaboration met at Unicamp to discuss the progress of the project, which is led by Fermilab.

Baikal-GVD point sources

Baikal-GVD is a cubic-kilometre-scale detector under construction in Lake Baikal and is now 70% complete. A search for point-like astrophysical sources used a 5-year track-like dataset from 2019-2024. No significant source was found, and upper limits were computed for a selection of candidate objects. The authors compare these with published results from IceCube, ANTARES and KM3NeT.

Search for Point-Like Neutrino Sources with Baikal-GVD — INSPIRE · DOI
MicroBooNE proton cross sections

MicroBooNE presented the first double-differential cross sections for mesonless muon-neutrino charged-current interactions on argon as a function of proton multiplicity and kinematics. The analysis uses the full Booster Neutrino Beam dataset, corresponding to 1.30 × 10²¹ protons on target. Cross sections were measured for up to the fourth-leading proton, among other variables.

MINERvA superscaling

MINERvA reported the first measurement of the superscaling variable reconstructed from visible final-state kinematics in neutrino-nucleus scattering. The sample is charged-current quasielastic-like muon-neutrino interactions on carbon in the medium-energy NuMI beam. The variable is used as an externally calibrated quantity whose peak position is sensitive to the invisible nuclear missing energy.

T2K near detector upgrade

The upgrade of the ND280 near detector was completed in 2024, which has significantly enhanced its tracking capabilities. One paper presents a new selection of muon-neutrino charged-current events with single positively charged pion production in the new modules. Another proposes a neural network to distinguish electrons, photons, muons, pions and protons in the SuperFGD scintillator target. Both aim to constrain neutrino-interaction modelling, which dominates the systematic uncertainties of T2K's oscillation analysis.

EXO-200 public data

A subset of EXO-200 calibration data has been released publicly for AI and machine-learning applications. It comprises approximately one million events from ²²⁸Th calibration campaigns taken between 26 February and 2 March 2018, near the end of Phase-II operations. Each event includes raw waveforms from the U-wire, V-wire and avalanche photodiode channels, together with reconstructed quantities such as charge and light energy.

ANTARES solar WIMPs

The collaboration searched for neutrinos from WIMP annihilation in the Sun using the full ANTARES dataset. WIMPs could be captured gravitationally in the Sun and annihilate into Standard Model particles that emit neutrinos. The telescope is sensitive to neutrino energies from a few tens of GeV to the TeV scale, which suits WIMP masses below a few TeV. The excerpt does not state the result.

Theory highlights

recently published, with the links each record carries

  • This paper presents what it describes as the first combined oscillation analysis of recent atmospheric datasets from Super-Kamiokande, IceCube-DeepCore and KM3NeT/ORCA, together with Daya Bay reactor data. Such combinations were long considered infeasible outside the collaborations. A unified model fits all datasets without significant parameter tensions and prefers normal over inverted ordering at 3σ.

    Atmospheric neutrino oscillations: The full picture — arXiv · INSPIRE · DOI

  • The paper derives global Bayesian limits on Majorana neutrino masses from neutrinoless double-beta decay searches, using nuclear matrix elements computed ab initio from chiral effective field theory. In contrast to nuclear models, the ab initio results indicate that current-generation experiments have likely not yet reached the sensitivities required to probe the mass region it considers. This matters for how existing 0νββ limits are translated into neutrino-mass constraints.

    Global ab initio neutrino mass limits from neutrinoless double-beta decay — arXiv · INSPIRE · DOI

  • The authors calculate two-neutrino double-beta decay rates without the usual approximations that decouple nuclear structure from lepton kinematics. They compare the resulting rates and electron spectra with closure, non-closure and Taylor-expansion methods for ⁸²Se and ¹³⁶Xe. A more realistic treatment of the 2νββ spectrum is relevant to the background modelling of searches for the neutrinoless mode.

    Direct calculation of the two-neutrino double-beta decay rate including the full … — arXiv · INSPIRE · DOI

  • The paper asks whether nuclear recoil tracks in ancient minerals could record neutrinos from past Galactic core-collapse supernovae. It varies neutron-star masses, includes failed supernovae that form black holes, and considers the role of the nuclear equation of state. Sensitivity is enhanced for models with larger neutrino emission, so paleodetectors depend on the collapse outcome.

    Paleodetectors for neutrino signals from diverse Galactic stellar collapses — arXiv · INSPIRE · DOI

  • The authors study thermal leptogenesis in a model where neutrino masses arise from a dimension-7 operator at tree level and a dimension-5 operator at one loop. The model accommodates sub-eV neutrino masses with new physics at the TeV scale, and the paper explores whether leptogenesis is viable there. This differs from canonical seesaw-based leptogenesis because of the scalar quadruplet in the model.

    Thermal leptogenesis in the Babu-Nandi-Tavartkiladze model of neutrino mass — arXiv · INSPIRE · DOI

  • The paper studies composite neutrino models in which heavy neutrinos are bound states of a near-conformal strongly coupled sector, mixing with Standard Model neutrinos through an inverse seesaw. Matching the conformal dynamics yields transition dipole couplings of order 10⁻⁶ to 10⁻⁸ GeV⁻¹, larger than loop-level predictions of minimal Dirac or Majorana models. The authors carry out an event-level simulation of the production-and-decay signatures.

    Neutrino dipole moments and radiative signatures from partial compositeness — arXiv · INSPIRE · DOI
