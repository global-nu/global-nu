This week's neutrino-physics news is dominated by IceCube: a renewed five-year NSF operating contract, new Upgrade strings deployed at the Pole, and fresh source searches using DeepCore data, alongside commissioning progress on T2K's ND280 upgrade and a first MicroBooNE cross-section measurement on argon. Experiments and results

IceCube funding renewed

The US National Science Foundation has renewed its cooperative agreement with the University of Wisconsin–Madison for IceCube operation and maintenance. The five-year, $53 million award covers continued running of the observatory for the period 2026-2031.

The U.S. National Science Foundation renews NSF IceCube operation and maintenance contract — Read it
IceCube Upgrade deployment

During the austral summer of 2025/26, five new strings carrying new photosensor designs were deployed as a dense infill at the centre of the existing IceCube array. A companion study projects the sensitivity this Upgrade will bring to the ongoing hunt for the sources of high-energy neutrinos, building on evidence that active galaxies are among the likely sites of hadronic particle acceleration.

IceCube Upgrade status and perspectives — arXivProjected sensitivities of the IceCube Upgrade — Read it
IceCube source searches

The collaboration has published a new search for steady-state neutrino emission using DeepCore data, revisiting NGC 1068, the active galaxy for which IceCube reported evidence of high-energy neutrino emission four years ago. A separate search targets neutrinos from tidal disruption events, transient phenomena on timescales of weeks to months; IceCube has so far identified two galaxies as neutrino sources and observed neutrinos from the Milky Way.

IceCube and extra dimensions

IceCube has also searched for signatures of extra spatial dimensions, one proposed explanation for the hierarchy problem, the puzzle of why the weak force is so much stronger than gravity.

Searching for extra dimensions with IceCube — Read it
T2K ND280 upgrade

T2K has commissioned the Time-of-Flight detector of its ND280 upgrade, six scintillator panels totalling 118 EJ-200 plastic-scintillator bars read out by silicon photomultipliers around the SuperFGD and the High-Angle Time Projection Chambers. The detector provides timing and directional information for particle identification and background rejection, part of the effort to reduce systematic uncertainties on oscillation parameters including the CP-violating phase.

MicroBooNE cross sections

MicroBooNE has reported the first measurement of flux-integrated cross sections for electron-neutrino interactions on argon as a function of kinematic imbalance variables, using its full dataset of 1.3×10^21 protons on target from Fermilab's Booster Neutrino Beam. These variables are sensitive to nuclear effects such as Fermi motion, multinucleon processes and final-state interactions.

Solar neutrinos outlook

A review argues that reactor antineutrino measurements have now pinned down the oscillation parameters governing solar-neutrino flavour conversion so precisely that the propagation-related uncertainty is becoming negligible, opening the way to use solar neutrinos to probe the Sun itself. It notes that solar-neutrino detection still falls short, by roughly an order of magnitude, of the precision the Standard Solar Model already predicts for the dominant pp flux.

After 60 years, here comes the Sun — arXiv
SHiP/NA67 detector design

The SHiP/NA67 collaboration described the detector systems planned for its experiment at CERN's HI-ECN3 beam facility, which will search for feebly interacting GeV-scale particles and perform all-flavour neutrino-physics measurements. The collaboration is currently optimising the initial configuration ahead of commissioning and first physics runs in 2032-2033.

Theory highlights

recently published, with the links each record carries

  • A combined oscillation analysis unifies atmospheric-neutrino datasets from Super-Kamiokande, IceCube-DeepCore and KM3NeT/ORCA with reactor data from Daya Bay, fitting 839,048 events across 1536 bins with 91 parameters. The single physics model describes all datasets with no significant tensions and prefers the normal over the inverted mass ordering at 3σ significance, a combination previously considered infeasible outside individual collaborations.

    Atmospheric neutrino oscillations: The full picture — arXiv · INSPIRE · DOI

  • By combining likelihood functions from a suite of neutrinoless double-beta decay experiments with nuclear matrix elements computed ab initio in the in-medium similarity renormalization group approach, this work derives global Bayesian limits on the Majorana neutrino mass. In contrast to results based on other nuclear models, the ab initio calculation indicates that current-generation 0νββ-decay experiments have likely not yet reached the necessary sensitivity.

    Global ab initio neutrino mass limits from neutrinoless double-beta decay — arXiv · INSPIRE · DOI

  • This paper lays out the physics case for TAMBO, the Tau Air-shower Mountain-Based Observatory, a planned mountain-based detector aimed at mapping the high-energy neutrino sky more completely than existing instruments have managed. The authors argue the approach could also turn up clues to new physics.

    Measuring the high-energy neutrino sky using the deep-valley neutrino observatory TAMBO — INSPIRE · DOI

  • This paper proposes a minimal dark SU(2) gauge symmetry that forces a Dirac neutrino mass matrix to have rank two, leaving one neutrino mass exactly zero while remaining consistent with oscillation and cosmological data. Anomaly cancellation requires a second fermionic dark doublet, and a discrete symmetry that forbids Majorana masses lets the two dark doublets pair up vectorlike, giving a predictive origin for a strictly massless neutrino.

    Minimal dark S U ( 2 ) origin of a massless Dirac neutrino — arXiv · INSPIRE · DOI

  • The authors build a minimal Standard Model extension in which a single U(1)PQ symmetry both drives an inverse seesaw mechanism and stabilises a fermionic dark matter candidate, generating light neutrino masses at the TeV scale without invoking very high energy scales. The resulting axion-like mediator predicts a correlation between the effective Majorana mass and the CP-violating phase, enhancing testability in future experiments.

    Fermionic dark matter and neutrino masses in a minimal U(1)PQ inverse seesaw framework — INSPIRE · DOI

  • This study asks whether ancient minerals could register nuclear-recoil tracks left by neutrinos from past Galactic core-collapse supernovae, extending the paleodetector concept to cover variations in neutron-star mass and failed supernovae that instead form black holes. Sensitivity depends on the nuclear equation of state, which sets neutron-star radii and maximum masses, and is enhanced for more compact remnants that release more gravitational energy as neutrinos.

    Paleodetectors for neutrino signals from diverse Galactic stellar collapses — arXiv · INSPIRE · DOI
