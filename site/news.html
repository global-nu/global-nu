NOvA hydrogen cross section

A measurement of the total cross section for muon antineutrino charged-current quasi-elastic scattering on hydrogen was made using the near detector, with a proton-on-target exposure of 1.2×10^21 in the NuMI beam. A topological and kinematic selection yielded 35,509 signal events in the hydrogen-rich (10.8%) detector, the highest-statistics sample of such interactions measured to date, with backgrounds from heavier nuclei constrained by dedicated control samples.

Theory highlights

recently published, with the links each record carries

  • TAMBO, a mountain-based neutrino observatory under development, aims to help map the high-energy neutrino sky, a goal that has proved difficult despite a decade of progress in neutrino astronomy. The paper, from Argüelles, Bazo, Briceño and collaborators, also frames the observatory as a route to searching for signs of new physics.

    Measuring the high-energy neutrino sky using the deep-valley neutrino observatory TAMBO — INSPIRE · DOI

  • Shickele, Jokiniemi, Belley and collaborators combined results from multiple neutrinoless double-beta decay searches with nuclear matrix elements computed ab initio, using chiral effective field theory and the in-medium similarity renormalization group, to set global Bayesian limits on the Majorana neutrino mass. In contrast to conventional nuclear models, their ab initio results indicate that the current generation of double-beta decay experiments has likely not yet reached the sensitivity needed to probe the mass region motivated by these calculations.

    Global ab initio neutrino mass limits from neutrinoless double-beta decay — arXiv · INSPIRE · DOI

  • Di Mauro proposed a secluded dark-matter framework built on an extra U(1) gauge symmetry, in which a Dirac dark-matter particle, three heavy neutrinos and a singlet scalar generate both the observed dark matter and the active neutrino masses through a shared one-loop portal to the Higgs. The model ties the dark-sector mixing angle directly to the heavy-neutrino masses and the light-neutrino mass sum, linking the two puzzles to a common origin.

    Neutrino mass and secluded dark matter puzzles solved together — INSPIRE · DOI

  • Dey and Dutta examined how well NOvA and DUNE, in combination, could constrain invisible decay of the third neutrino mass state into a sterile state under the normal mass ordering. They found that the true value of theta_23 strongly affects the achievable constraint, with a value in the higher octant giving a notably tighter bound on the decay parameter.

    Constraining invisible neutrino decay at NOνA and DUNE — INSPIRE · DOI

  • Liu, Gardiner and Dytman used the GENIE event generator to test a range of models for pionless neutrino-argon scattering against recent MicroBooNE charged-current measurements, comparing established and newer choices for nucleon form factors, quasielastic cross sections and nuclear ground-state treatments. They report strong separation between competing models in some cases, offering clear guidance for the simulations that upcoming neutrino experiments will rely on.

    Benchmarking state-of-the-art theory and empirical models of pionless neutrino-argon … — arXiv · INSPIRE · DOI

  • Sakai, Inoue and Owen revisited accretion-disk winds from active galactic nuclei as a possible contributor to both the cosmic neutrino background and the cosmic gamma-ray background, building a lepto-hadronic wind model calibrated against radio and GeV gamma-ray data from nearby Seyfert galaxies detected by Fermi-LAT.

    Revisiting Disk Winds in Active Galactic Nuclei as an Origin of Cosmic Gamma-Ray and … — arXiv · INSPIRE · DOI
