Experiments and results

DUNE AI triggers

DUNE researchers are developing AI systems to process the experiment's large data volumes, sort neutrino interactions, flag possible signs of a stellar explosion and catch problems inside the detector automatically. The effort draws on decades of machine-learning work at Fermilab and is aimed at readying the experiment for full-scale data analysis.

DUNE detector stress test

A prototype DUNE detector module was subjected to an extreme 300,000-volt stress trial as part of testing before installation deep underground in the US.

300,000 volts: US team puts world’s largest neutrino detector through extreme stress test — Read it
IceCube and Earth structure

IceCube researchers described how atmospheric neutrinos, produced in cosmic-ray-induced air showers, can be used to probe the interior structure of the Earth, complementing indirect methods such as seismic and gravitational measurements.

Probing the Earth’s interior using atmospheric neutrinos — Read it
IceCube WavePID tool

IceCube has presented WavePID, a tool that identifies detected neutrinos from the shape of the Cherenkov light pattern produced when a neutrino interacts with the ice, working on nanosecond timescales.

WavePID: A tool that identifies neutrinos at the nanosecond level — Read it
JUNO calibration background

A JUNO paper detailed the radioactive background contributed by the detector's calibration system, whose components sit close to the target volume. The collaboration reports extensive material screening and selection to keep the total singles background rate below the 10 Hz requirement, in the 0.7-12 MeV energy range within the fiducial volume, needed for the reactor antineutrino analysis.

The Radioactive Background of the JUNO Calibration System — arXiv
Neutrino Day outreach

SURF and Fermilab hosted the 18th annual Neutrino Day science festival in Lead, South Dakota, including a live broadcast from a mile underground featuring SURF laboratory director Mike Headley and DUNE senior scientist Sowjanya Gollapinni, alongside an Ask a Scientist table.

Theory highlights

recently published, with the links each record carries

  • Combining current neutrinoless double-beta decay results with ab initio nuclear matrix elements computed via the in-medium similarity renormalization group, this paper derives Bayesian global limits on the Majorana neutrino mass. In contrast to more phenomenological nuclear models, the ab initio treatment indicates that present-generation 0νββ experiments have likely not yet reached the sensitivity needed to probe the relevant mass range.

    Global ab initio neutrino mass limits from neutrinoless double-beta decay — arXiv · INSPIRE · DOI

  • This paper introduces TAMBO, a mountain-based observatory designed to detect tau neutrino air showers, aiming to help map the still poorly-charted high-energy neutrino sky and potentially reveal clues to new physics.

    Measuring the high-energy neutrino sky using the deep-valley neutrino observatory TAMBO — INSPIRE · DOI

  • The authors construct a minimal dark SU(2) gauge symmetry that produces a rank-two Dirac neutrino mass matrix, enforcing exactly one massless neutrino while remaining consistent with oscillation and cosmological data. Anomaly cancellation is shown to fix the required dark-sector particle content, yielding a fairly predictive Dirac-neutrino-mass framework.

    Minimal dark S U ( 2 ) origin of a massless Dirac neutrino — arXiv · INSPIRE · DOI

  • This study derives non-standard neutrino interactions generated by trilinear LLE couplings in R-parity-violating supersymmetry with light third-generation sleptons, and checks the resulting parameter space against existing experimental constraints. It finds that some NSI parameters, notably ϵee (up to about 8%) and ϵμμ (up to about 3.8%), can reach the percent level, while most other entries remain restricted to well below one percent.

    Neutrino nonstandard interactions from \(LLE\)-type \(R\)-parity violation — INSPIRE · DOI

  • Using the GENIE event generator, this paper benchmarks several competing models of pionless neutrino-argon scattering against recent MicroBooNE charged-current measurements, providing a framework for comparing nuclear-interaction models relevant to argon-based neutrino experiments. The comparison shows strong separation between some models, pointing to clearer modelling choices for experiments to adopt.

    Benchmarking state-of-the-art theory and empirical models of pionless neutrino-argon … — arXiv · INSPIRE · DOI

  • This paper examines how well DUNE and NOvA, combined, could constrain a scenario in which the third neutrino mass state decays invisibly into a sterile state under the normal mass ordering. It finds the achievable constraint on the decay parameter depends significantly on the true value of θ23, with a stronger constraint if θ23 lies in the higher octant.

    Constraining invisible neutrino decay at NOνA and DUNE — INSPIRE · DOI
