Halzen Nobel Prize

The Royal Swedish Academy of Science announced on 6 October that the 2026 Nobel Prize in Physics goes to Francis Halzen, principal investigator of IceCube, for decisive contributions to the IceCube Neutrino Observatory and the discovery of high-energy neutrinos of astrophysical origin. Wisconsin Public Radio has since interviewed him about the award and the importance of neutrino research. UK scientists and engineers have completed half of the 137 Anode Plane Assemblies that the UK is producing for DUNE. These sensors will detect the tiny electrical signals produced when neutrinos interact with liquid argon in DUNE's underground detectors.

JUNO-TAO commissioning

The Taishan Antineutrino Observatory is the JUNO satellite that measures the reactor antineutrino spectrum at 43.2 m from a reactor core. It uses a 2.8-ton liquid-scintillator detector read out by 4,024 silicon photomultiplier tiles. Installation was completed in 2025 and physics data taking began on 10 February 2026. The JUNO Collaboration reports stable cryogenic operation, with about 90% of the readout channels retained.

Baikal-GVD point sources

Baikal-GVD is under construction in Lake Baikal and is now 70% complete. A search for point-like astrophysical neutrino sources in its 2019-2024 track-like dataset found no significant source. Upper limits were computed for a selection of candidate objects and compared with published IceCube, ANTARES and KM3NeT results.

Neutrino cross section

A new analysis of IceCube data determines muon-neutrino-nucleon charged-current cross sections across the TeV scale. The authors note that existing measurements still carry uncertainties of about 20-40% and that no single measurement covers the range from sub-TeV to tens of TeV.

Fast X-ray transients

A time-integrated search used 14 years of public IceCube muon-track data for neutrinos from 27 fast X-ray transients detected by Einstein Probe. No statistically significant excess was found. The largest test statistic was TS=4.77 for EP240801a, with a local p-value of 0.04, and 90% confidence level upper limits on the muon-neutrino flux were derived.

When can decoherence reveal a Majorana phase? A phase-covariance no-go theorem for two-flavor neutrino oscillations

The paper proves that, in open two-flavour neutrino oscillations, every completely positive trace-preserving evolution covariant under rotations of the mass-coherence plane is exactly blind to the Majorana phase. It also identifies, for the unital Pauli-GKSL class, when a rate anisotropy can supply the phase reference needed to see it. This is relevant to anyone asking whether decoherence could ever reveal the Dirac or Majorana nature of neutrinos.

Direct calculation of the two-neutrino double-beta decay rate including the full lepton-energy dependence

The authors compute the two-neutrino double-beta decay rate with the full interdependence of nuclear structure and lepton kinematics, without the usual closure, non-closure or Taylor-expansion approximations. They present the resulting deviations in decay rates and electron spectra for 82Se and 136Xe. The spectra matter to double-beta decay searches because those searches model the two-neutrino mode as a background.

Thermal leptogenesis in the Babu-Nandi-Tavartkiladze model of neutrino mass

The paper studies neutrino mass and thermal leptogenesis in the Babu-Nandi-Tavartkiladze model, which has a scalar quadruplet and two vectorlike fermion triplets. Neutrino masses arise from a dimension-7 operator at tree level and a dimension-5 operator at one loop, accommodating sub-eV masses with new physics at the TeV scale. It asks whether thermal leptogenesis is viable in a framework that differs from canonical seesaw leptogenesis.

Constraining ultralight vector bosons via solar neutrino oscillations: The gauged L μ - L τ model and prospects for JUNO and XLZD

The paper builds a gauged U(1) L_mu - L_tau extension with an ultralight vector boson that mixes kinetically with the photon. This generates an extra matter potential in the Sun that modifies MSW flavour conversion. The author uses recent solar neutrino measurements from Borexino, SNO and Super-Kamiokande and discusses prospects for JUNO and XLZD.

Paleodetectors for neutrino signals from diverse Galactic stellar collapses

The paper examines whether neutrinos from past Galactic core-collapse supernovae could be detected through nuclear recoil tracks in ancient minerals, known as paleodetectors. It varies the neutron-star mass, includes failed supernovae that form black holes, and considers the nuclear equation of state. Sensitivity is found to be enhanced for models with larger neutrino emission.

Neutrino Emissivity in Δ -Direct-Urca Processes Inside Neutron Stars

The paper studies Delta-direct-Urca neutrino emission in neutron-star matter containing Delta isobars, noting that the specific contribution of this process to neutrino emissivity had not been explicitly examined. Nucleonic and hyperonic Urca processes are considered dominant cooling mechanisms. The paper matters to neutrino physicists as a case where neutrino emission controls the thermal evolution of dense matter.
