DUNE construction

Fermilab, CERN and SURF crews have started building the first of two cryostats, the insulated containers that will hold liquid argon for the experiment. Fermilab News also reports that construction has begun on the detector in South Dakota. At a progress report at the Sanford Lab, Fermilab leaders discussed the project, future hiring and the work to outfit the caverns with infrastructure.

T2K with gadolinium

T2K presented updated oscillation measurements using 21.4 (16.3) x 10^20 protons on target in neutrino (antineutrino) mode, including 1.7 x 10^20 POT more neutrino data than the previous result. For the first time the analysis includes data taken with Super-Kamiokande doped with gadolinium. The far-detector selection and the systematic error treatment were improved, and both semi-frequentist and Bayesian analyses favour normal mass ordering.

KM3NeT/ARCA searches

KM3NeT announced two papers based on ARCA data. One is a search for diffuse neutrino emission from the whole sky and from the Galactic Ridge, using the first two years of data and comparing theoretical predictions with the measurements. The other is a stacking search for point sources, which combines signals from ultra-luminous infrared galaxies and from blazars.

Neutrino Emissivity in Δ -Direct-Urca Processes Inside Neutron Stars

The paper studies neutrino emission from Δ-direct-Urca processes in neutron-star cores containing Δ isobars, a contribution the abstract says had not been explicitly evaluated. It matters for the thermal evolution of neutron stars, where nucleonic and hyperonic Urca processes are the dominant cooling mechanisms.

Determination of the spatial extent of a neutrino wave packet from electron capture decay experiments

The authors use the measured width of the 7Li recoil peak after 7Be K-electron capture (Smolsky et al.) to determine the spatial extent of the neutrino wave packet. The approach ties the wave packet to its production mechanism and to decoherence, which sets a common energy uncertainty for the emitted particles.

Search for an all-sky and a galactic ridge diffuse neutrino emission with the first 2 years of KM3NeT/ARCA data

The paper shows that the 2025 KM3NeT/ARCA data from the first configurations (6 to 21 detection units, 640 days of livetime) were used to fit a single-flavour diffuse astrophysical flux. The all-sky fit gives a spectral index of 3.00 with asymmetric uncertainties at 68% credible level, and the Galactic Ridge fit does not yield a result in the excerpt shown.

When can decoherence reveal a Majorana phase? A phase-covariance no-go theorem for two-flavor neutrino oscillations

The paper proves a no-go theorem: any completely positive trace-preserving evolution that is covariant under rotations of the mass-coherence plane is blind to a Majorana phase in two-flavour oscillations. A rate anisotropy in the environment can supply a phase reference, which is the condition under which decoherence could reveal the phase.

Quasisteady flavor configuration of multienergy neutrino ensembles

The authors derive semianalytic approximations for the quasisteady outcome of slow and fast flavour conversion in quasihomogeneous neutrino ensembles. They report agreement with multiangle and multienergy solutions of the kinetic equations independent of mass ordering, which offers a subgrid scheme for supernova simulations that cannot include neutrino quantum kinetics.

Exploring anomaly-free dark photon models through solar neutrino-electron scattering in ton-scale dark matter detectors

The paper uses solar neutrino-electron scattering data from PandaX-4T and XENONnT to set exclusion limits on anomaly-free U(1)_X dark photon models with kinetic and mass mixing. The limits depend on the leptonic charge assignment of each model, and model-independent limits are given for pure kinetic and pure mass mixing.
