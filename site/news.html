IceCube Nobel Prize

The Royal Swedish Academy of Science announced the 2026 Nobel Prize in Physics for Francis Halzen, principal investigator of IceCube, for decisive contributions to the observatory and the discovery of high-energy neutrinos of astrophysical origin. Wisconsin Public Radio interviewed Halzen about the prize and the importance of neutrino research. KM3NeT sent congratulations, and the South Pole station gathered at the IceCube Lab for a celebratory photo.

JUNO-TAO

The Taishan Antineutrino Observatory is a satellite of JUNO. It measures the reactor antineutrino spectrum at a baseline of 43.2 m with a 2.8-ton liquid-scintillator detector read out by 4,024 silicon photomultiplier tiles. Installation was completed in 2025 and physics data taking began on 10 February 2026. The JUNO Collaboration reports stable cryogenic operation, with about 90% of the readout channels retained.

P-ONE photomultipliers

P-ONE is a planned cubic-kilometre-scale neutrino telescope for the Pacific Ocean, using Ocean Networks Canada infrastructure. Its first detector line will carry 320 three-inch Hamamatsu R14374-10 photomultiplier tubes. A new article characterises all the tubes to be integrated into that line before deployment, to check compliance with the detector acceptance criteria.

Baikal-GVD sources

Baikal-GVD, a cubic-kilometre-scale detector under construction in Lake Baikal, is now 70% complete. A search for point-like astrophysical neutrino sources used a five-year track-like dataset from 2019-2024. No significant source was found, so upper limits were computed for selected candidate objects and compared with IceCube, ANTARES and KM3NeT results.

T2K near detector

The upgraded ND280 includes SuperFGD, a segmented scintillator detector of about 2 million optically isolated 1 cm³ cubes. Its performance in the T2K beam is described in one paper. A second paper presents an event selection for muon-neutrino charged-current interactions with single positively charged pion production in the new modules, noting that the upgrade was completed in 2024. The selection targets the interaction-modelling uncertainties that limit T2K's oscillation analysis.

NνDEx simulation

NνDEx is planned for the China Jinping Underground Laboratory. It will search for neutrinoless double beta decay of 82Se using a high-pressure gas time projection chamber filled with 82SeF6 and read out by a Topmetal pixel system. A new paper presents a full simulation and reconstruction framework for it, built on the REST software toolkit.

Neutrino Emissivity in Δ -Direct-Urca Processes Inside Neutron Stars

The paper examines the contribution of Δ-isobar direct-Urca processes to neutrino emissivity in neutron stars. The record states that this contribution had not been explicitly determined, although nucleonic and hyperonic Urca processes are known to be dominant cooling mechanisms. It matters for the thermal evolution of massive neutron stars if they contain Δ isobars.

Direct calculation of the two-neutrino double-beta decay rate including the full lepton-energy dependence

The authors compute two-neutrino double beta decay rates with the full interdependence of nuclear structure and lepton kinematics, rather than decoupling them. They show deviations from closure, non-closure and Taylor-expansion treatments for 82Se and 136Xe. This is relevant to the modelling of rates and electron spectra for these isotopes.

Constraining ultralight vector bosons via solar neutrino oscillations: The gauged L μ - L τ model and prospects for JUNO and XLZD

The paper uses a gauged Lμ-Lτ model with an ultralight vector boson that mixes kinetically with the photon. This produces an extra matter potential in the Sun and so modifies MSW flavour conversion. It uses Borexino, SNO and Super-Kamiokande solar measurements and discusses prospects for JUNO and XLZD.

Thermal leptogenesis in the Babu-Nandi-Tavartkiladze model of neutrino mass

The authors study thermal leptogenesis in the Babu-Nandi-Tavartkiladze model, where neutrino masses arise from a dimension-7 operator at tree level and a dimension-5 operator at one loop. The model gives sub-eV neutrino masses with new physics near the TeV scale. The paper asks whether leptogenesis works in this setting, which differs from canonical seesaw leptogenesis.

Paleodetectors for neutrino signals from diverse Galactic stellar collapses

The paper asks whether neutrinos from past Galactic core-collapse supernovae could be detected through nuclear recoil tracks in ancient minerals (paleodetectors). It varies neutron-star masses, includes failed supernovae that form black holes, and considers the nuclear equation of state. Sensitivity is larger for models with larger neutrino emission.

Magnetorotational Supernova Neutrino Spectra Versus Mass Ordering

The paper considers magnetic and oscillation effects on neutrinos emitted from a protoneutron star in a supernova. It reports that oscillations in the outer stellar layers harden the electron antineutrino spectrum, most strongly for the inverted mass ordering. That ordering dependence would favour ground-based detection of supernova neutrinos.
