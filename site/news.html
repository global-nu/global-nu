Experiments and results

DUNE turns to AI

Researchers are developing automated, AI-based trigger and analysis systems to sift through the experiment's data volumes, aiming to identify neutrino interactions, flag signs of a stellar explosion, and monitor detector health. The effort draws on machine learning experience built up at Fermilab over previous years.

DUNE detector stress test

A prototype detector was subjected to a 300,000-volt stress trial, testing its high-voltage tolerance ahead of installation deep underground in the United States.

PIP-II accelerator milestone

Fermilab crews installed the first beamline component in the tunnel that will house the new PIP-II linear accelerator, a milestone for the laboratory's future neutrino research programme.

IceCube's WavePID tool

A newly described tool uses the shape of the light pattern produced when a neutrino interacts with ice to identify neutrino events at the nanosecond level. The tool, called WavePID, exploits the blue light emitted by secondary particles created in such interactions.

WavePID: A tool that identifies neutrinos at the nanosecond level — Read it
Probing Earth's interior

Atmospheric neutrinos, produced in cosmic-ray-induced air showers, are being used to investigate the structure of the Earth's interior, complementing seismic and gravitational methods. The newly deployed IceCube Upgrade, installed during the 2025-26 polar season, is expected to lower the detector's energy threshold and improve reconstruction of multi-GeV atmospheric neutrinos, enhancing sensitivity to Earth matter effects. Winterover crews at the Pole spent recent weeks on Upgrade calibrations alongside routine detector maintenance.

KM3NeT diffuse neutrino search

Analysis of the first 640 days of livetime from the ARCA detector, in configurations of 6 to 21 active detection units, searched for a diffuse astrophysical neutrino flux across the whole sky and from the Galactic Ridge. The all-sky fit found a single-flavour flux consistent with a power law of spectral index 3.00 (+0.30/-0.35) at 68% credible level; the dedicated Galactic Ridge search did not yield constraints on the flux.

Input for LEGEND

A new measurement examined the electron capture of 76As into excited and ground states of 76Ge, decay branches relevant to the nuclear matrix element used to interpret LEGEND's neutrinoless double-beta decay search. Electron capture into the 76Ge ground state has not yet been observed.

Theory highlights

recently published, with preprint, record and journal

  • Dey and Dutta examine how combined analyses of the NOvA and DUNE long-baseline experiments could constrain invisible decay of the third neutrino mass state into a sterile state, under the normal mass ordering. They find that the true value of the mixing angle theta_23 significantly affects the achievable constraint on the decay parameter, with values in the higher octant giving a stronger bound.

    Constraining invisible neutrino decay at NO$\nu $A and DUNE — INSPIRE · DOI

  • Shickele, Jokiniemi, Belley and collaborators combine current neutrinoless double-beta decay search results with nuclear matrix elements computed from ab initio nuclear theory, within a Bayesian framework, to set global limits on the Majorana neutrino mass scale. In contrast to phenomenological nuclear models, their ab initio calculation indicates that the current generation of 0vbb experiments has likely not yet reached the sensitivity needed to fully probe the relevant mass region.

    Global ab initio neutrino mass limits from neutrinoless double-beta decay — arXiv · INSPIRE · DOI

  • Argüelles, Bazo, Briceño and collaborators describe TAMBO, a mountain-based observatory that exploits deep-valley geometry to map the high-energy neutrino sky, an approach intended to improve the field's ability to survey this energy range and look for signs of new physics.

    Measuring the high-energy neutrino sky using the deep-valley neutrino observatory TAMBO — INSPIRE · DOI

  • Liu, Gardiner and Dytman use the GENIE event generator to test a range of pionless charged-current interaction models against recent MicroBooNE measurements on argon, finding strong separation between competing models in some cases. The comparison gives upcoming neutrino experiments a clearer basis for choosing interaction models in their simulations.

    Benchmarking state-of-the-art theory and empirical models of pionless neutrino-argon scatt — arXiv · INSPIRE · DOI

  • Majhi, Pal, Ghosh and collaborators compare two formalisms for introducing quantum decoherence into neutrino oscillation probabilities, one defined in the matter mass eigenstate basis and one in the vacuum mass eigenstate basis, and show that the two approaches predict different sensitivities at the future DUNE and P2SO long-baseline experiments.

    Impact of different neutrino decoherence formalisms at the future long-baseline experiment — arXiv · INSPIRE · DOI

  • Wang and Ma search for correlations between the ultra-high-energy neutrino event KM3-230213A, reported by KM3NeT with a reconstructed energy peaking at 220 PeV, and gamma-ray bursts, accounting for the neutrino's angular uncertainty and the possibility of Lorentz invariance violation.

    Correlation between Ultrahigh-energy Neutrino KM3-230213A and Gamma-Ray Bursts — arXiv · INSPIRE · DOI
