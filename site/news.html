Experiments and results

IceCube funding renewed

The US National Science Foundation has renewed its cooperative agreement with the University of Wisconsin–Madison to operate and maintain the IceCube Neutrino Observatory, covering the period 2026 to 2031. The five-year agreement secures continued running of the South Pole detector.

The U.S. National Science Foundation renews NSF IceCube operation and maintenance contract — Read it
DUNE automated triggers

DUNE researchers are developing AI systems to sift through the experiment's data, aiming to identify neutrino interactions, flag signs of a stellar explosion and spot problems inside the detector automatically. The effort draws on machine-learning work already established at Fermilab and forms part of preparations for the experiment's data analysis.

US lab to hunt most elusive ‘ghost particles’ deep underground with automated triggers — Read itDUNE uses AI to transform the future of neutrino research — Read it
DUNE detector stress test

A prototype DUNE detector module was put through an extreme 300,000-volt stress trial to probe its limits ahead of installation deep underground in the United States.

300,000 volts: US team puts world’s largest neutrino detector through extreme stress test — Read it
Probing Earth with neutrinos

IceCube is using atmospheric neutrinos, produced in cosmic-ray-induced air showers, to investigate the interior structure of the Earth, complementing indirect methods such as seismic and gravitational measurements.

Probing the Earth’s interior using atmospheric neutrinos — Read it
IceCube's WavePID tool

IceCube has described WavePID, a tool that identifies neutrino interactions from the shape of the Cherenkov light pattern produced when a neutrino interacts with the ice, working at the nanosecond level.

WavePID: A tool that identifies neutrinos at the nanosecond level — Read it

Theory highlights

recently published, with the links each record carries

  • This paper presents TAMBO, the Tau Air-shower Mountain-Based Observatory, a mountain-based instrument designed to map the high-energy neutrino sky more fully than current facilities allow. Its authors position it as a route to closing gaps in astrophysical neutrino observations and to searching for new physics along the way.

    Measuring the high-energy neutrino sky using the deep-valley neutrino observatory TAMBO — INSPIRE · DOI

  • This study examines how a future high-energy muon collider could probe non-standard neutrino-two-photon interactions, via a process in which a muon scatters off a photon to produce a neutrino pair, treated with a model-independent effective-field-theory approach. The authors simulate signal and background at parton level to assess sensitivity to these anomalous couplings.

    Investigation of neutrino electromagnetic properties at muon antimuon collider — INSPIRE · DOI

  • The authors propose that leptogenesis, driven by the decay of heavy right-handed neutrinos in the early Universe, could generate a gravitational-wave signal. Such a signal would give an independent handle on leptogenesis and on the minimal seesaw mechanism, both otherwise hard to test directly.

    Probing leptogenesis and the minimal neutrino seesaw mechanism through gravitational waves — INSPIRE · DOI

  • This paper derives global limits on the Majorana neutrino mass by combining results from neutrinoless double-beta decay searches with ab initio nuclear matrix elements computed via the in-medium similarity renormalization group. In contrast to earlier nuclear-model-based limits, the ab initio results indicate that the current generation of experiments has likely not yet reached the sensitivity needed to probe the relevant mass range.

    Global ab initio neutrino mass limits from neutrinoless double-beta decay — arXiv · INSPIRE · DOI

  • This work benchmarks a range of theoretical and empirical neutrino-argon scattering models within the GENIE event generator against recent MicroBooNE measurements of pionless charged-current interactions. The comparison shows strong separation between competing models in some cases, offering clearer model choices for upcoming argon-based neutrino experiments.

    Benchmarking state-of-the-art theory and empirical models of pionless neutrino-argon … — arXiv · INSPIRE · DOI

  • This paper studies how combining NOvA and DUNE data could constrain invisible decay of the heaviest neutrino mass state into a sterile state, under the normal mass hierarchy. It finds that the true value of theta23 significantly affects how tightly the decay parameter can be constrained.

    Constraining invisible neutrino decay at NOνA and DUNE — INSPIRE · DOI
