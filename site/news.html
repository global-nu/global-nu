KM3-230213A review

A review summarises the properties of the ultra-high-energy event KM3-230213A, with an inferred parent-neutrino energy of order 10^17 eV. It discusses interpretations in terms of active galactic nuclei, transient sources and cosmogenic neutrinos. Despite extensive follow-up, no unambiguous electromagnetic counterpart has been found.

Auger sterile search

The Pierre Auger Collaboration studied its sensitivity to sterile neutrinos with masses between MeV and 100 MeV, in a 3+1 framework with mixing mainly to the tau flavour. Such neutrinos could cross long chords of the Earth and produce tau leptons that decay in the atmosphere, generating detectable air showers. Exposures are computed with a detailed Monte Carlo simulation.

Project 8 dissociator

Project 8 aims at a model-independent neutrino mass measurement through cyclotron radiation emission spectroscopy of tritium beta decay, with a sensitivity goal of 40 meV/c^2. A new paper reports a prototype electron cyclotron resonance dissociator that ignites a hydrogen plasma for the planned atomic-tritium beamline.

CMS heavy neutral leptons

CMS reported two searches for heavy neutral leptons. One targets a Majorana state in top-quark decays with same-sign, same-flavour dilepton final states, using 138 fb^-1 at 13 TeV. The other looks for long-lived states in W decays with a prompt muon and a displaced vertex of two muons, using 138 fb^-1 at 13 TeV and 34.6 fb^-1 at 13.6 TeV.

Neutrino Emissivity in Δ -Direct-Urca Processes Inside Neutron Stars

The paper computes the neutrino emissivity of Δ-direct-Urca processes in neutron stars, which had not been explicitly evaluated even though Δ isobars are proposed to exist in dense cores. It matters for neutron-star cooling, where Urca processes are the dominant neutrino-emitting mechanisms.

The authors study what kiloton-scale detectors such as Hyper-Kamiokande can learn about the primary cosmic-ray spectrum from atmospheric neutrinos. When can decoherence reveal a Majorana phase? A phase-covariance no-go theorem for two-flavor neutrino oscillations

The paper proves a no-go theorem: open two-flavour oscillation evolutions covariant under rotations of the mass-coherence plane are exactly blind to a Majorana phase. A rate anisotropy can supply an environmental phase reference, which sets when decoherence could reveal the phase.

Exploring anomaly-free dark photon models through solar neutrino-electron scattering in ton-scale dark matter detectors

Using low-energy solar neutrino-electron scattering data from PandaX-4T and XENONnT, the paper derives exclusion limits on anomaly-free U(1)_X dark photon models with kinetic and mass mixing. The strength of the limits correlates with the leptonic charge assignments of each of six benchmark models.

Thermal leptogenesis in the Babu-Nandi-Tavartkiladze model of neutrino mass

The paper studies neutrino mass and thermal leptogenesis in the Babu-Nandi-Tavartkiladze model, where neutrino masses arise from a dimension-7 operator at tree level and a dimension-5 operator at one loop. The model accommodates sub-eV neutrino masses with new physics at the TeV scale, and leptogenesis here differs from the canonical seesaw case.
