SBND cross section

SBND, the Short-Baseline Near Detector at Fermilab, measured the total and single-differential inclusive electron-neutrino charged-current cross sections on argon. The sample is a mixture of 95.2% neutrino and 4.8% antineutrino events. The authors describe it as the highest-statistics inclusive measurement of this channel on argon to date and the first one using the Booster Neutrino Beam.

KM3NeT/ARCA diffuse search

KM3NeT published a search for a diffuse astrophysical neutrino flux, over the full sky and from the Galactic Ridge, using the first ARCA configurations with 6, 8, 19 and 21 detection units (640 days of livetime in total). The all-sky fit under a single power law gives a spectral index of 3.00 with uncertainties of +0.30 and -0.35 at 68% credible level. The collaboration announced the paper on 20 November.

JUNO ultrapure water

JUNO is a 20 kton liquid-scintillator detector designed primarily to determine the neutrino mass ordering. Because the scintillator touches ultrapure water during filling, the water must contain less than 50 μBq/m3 of radium. A new paper describes a measurement system sensitive to radium in water down to 3.9 μBq/m3 and an optimised 100 t/h production process that combines selective ion-exchange resin with membrane separation.

Reactor source near JUNO

A phenomenology paper proposes placing a movable reactor antineutrino source within about 3 km of the JUNO detector. The authors argue that no planned experiment is expected to surpass Daya Bay's precision on θ13, and that JUNO's large fiducial mass would give a statistically significant event count from a relatively small reactor over a few years. This is a proposal, not an approved programme.

Geoneutrino decay limits

A new analysis uses geoneutrinos to test neutrino decay for the first time. The authors compute the geoneutrino signal at Borexino, KamLAND, SNO+ and JUNO for visible decay and compare it with the published data. They find no evidence of decay that holds across Earth models.

CYGNO electron directionality

The CYGNO collaboration studied how well its optical time-projection chamber reconstructs the initial direction of low-energy electron recoils, using the 50 L LIME prototype with a scientific CMOS readout. Directionality could help separate solar-neutrino interactions from backgrounds. The algorithm finds the interaction point from the asymmetry of the track light and takes the direction from the early part of the ionisation trail, before multiple scattering erases it.

MINERvA open dataset

A paper presents a probabilistic surrogate of the MINERvA detector response, trained on an open simulation dataset. It maps generator-level final-state particles and interaction geometry to reconstructed observables, without relying on generator-specific interaction labels. The aim is to let people outside the original collaboration compare interaction models with open neutrino data.

Neutrino Emissivity in Δ -Direct-Urca Processes Inside Neutron Stars

The paper computes the neutrino emissivity of Δ-direct-Urca processes in neutron stars, which can occur alongside the nucleonic Urca processes if Δ isobars are present in the dense core. The abstract says this contribution had not been explicitly determined before. It matters for neutron-star cooling, where Urca processes are the dominant neutrino-emitting mechanisms.

Toward measuring the low-energy cosmic-ray spectrum with next-generation neutrino detectors

The authors ask how well kiloton-scale detectors such as Hyper-Kamiokande could determine the primary cosmic-ray spectrum from the atmospheric neutrino flux. They report that future detectors can distinguish between cosmic-ray models with high statistical significance, including uncertainties on neutrino cross sections and hadronic interactions. They also introduce a technique for reconstructing the primary spectrum.

When can decoherence reveal a Majorana phase? A phase-covariance no-go theorem for two-flavor neutrino oscillations

The paper proves a no-go theorem for observing a Majorana phase in open two-flavour neutrino oscillations. Every completely positive trace-preserving evolution that is covariant under rotations of the mass-coherence plane is exactly blind to the phase. A rate anisotropy in the environment can supply a phase reference, and the paper gives the resulting finite-time dependence.

Comparative periodogram analysis of 22 years of Super-Kamiokande solar ⁸B neutrino data: classical, phase-based, and information theoretic methods

This is a comparison of nine periodogram algorithms applied to the 22-year Super-Kamiokande solar 8B neutrino dataset (1996-2018). The authors find that the Generalized Lomb-Scargle method gives the most statistically robust detections because it handles heteroscedastic uncertainties correctly. The work is relevant to anyone searching for time modulation in solar neutrino rates.

Quasisteady flavor configuration of multienergy neutrino ensembles

Hydrodynamic simulations of supernovae cannot include neutrino quantum kinetics, so they need subgrid schemes for the effect of neutrino self-interaction. The paper gives semianalytic approximations for the outcomes of slow and fast flavour conversion in quasihomogeneous systems. The authors report excellent agreement with multiangle and multienergy solutions of the kinetic equations, independent of the mass ordering.

The paper studies a gauged U(1) Lμ-Lτ model with an ultralight vector boson that kinetically mixes with the photon.
