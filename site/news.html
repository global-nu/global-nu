IceCube Nobel Prize

The Royal Swedish Academy of Sciences announced on 6 October that the 2026 Nobel Prize in Physics goes to Francis Halzen, IceCube principal investigator, for decisive contributions to the IceCube Neutrino Observatory and the discovery of high-energy neutrinos of astrophysical origin. At the South Pole, the entire station gathered at the IceCube Lab for a group photo on hearing the news. Halzen has also been interviewed on the importance of neutrino research and scientific collaboration.

P-ONE photomultipliers

P-ONE is a planned cubic-kilometre-scale neutrino telescope for the Pacific Ocean, using Ocean Networks Canada deep-sea infrastructure. A new paper characterises all 320 3-inch R14374-10 photomultiplier tubes for the first detector line before deployment, and reports that all of them satisfy the specified acceptance criteria.

IceCube transient searches

An analysis of 14 years of public IceCube muon-track data looked for neutrinos from 27 fast X-ray transients detected by Einstein Probe. It found no statistically significant excess, and the largest test statistic, for EP240801a, has a local p-value of 0.04. The authors therefore set 90% confidence level upper limits on the muon-neutrino flux.

Neutrino Emissivity in Δ -Direct-Urca Processes Inside Neutron Stars

The paper calculates neutrino emissivity from Δ-direct-Urca processes in neutron stars, motivated by the possible presence of Δ isobars in dense cores. It addresses a contribution to neutrino cooling that, according to the abstract, had not been explicitly determined before. It is relevant for anyone modelling neutron-star thermal evolution.

When can decoherence reveal a Majorana phase? A phase-covariance no-go theorem for two-flavor neutrino oscillations

The paper proves a no-go theorem: open two-flavour neutrino oscillations with evolution covariant under rotations of the mass-coherence plane are exactly blind to a Majorana phase. It also shows that a rate anisotropy in the environment can supply a phase reference. This defines when decoherence could make a Majorana phase observable in oscillations.

Constraining ultralight vector bosons via solar neutrino oscillations: The gauged L μ - L τ model and prospects for JUNO and XLZD

The paper uses solar neutrino data from Borexino, SNO and Super-Kamiokande to constrain an ultralight vector boson in the gauged L_μ − L_τ model. The boson, through kinetic mixing with the photon, would generate a matter potential in the Sun that modifies MSW oscillations. It also considers prospects for JUNO and XLZD.

Direct calculation of the two-neutrino double-beta decay rate including the full lepton-energy dependence

The paper computes the two-neutrino double-beta decay rate without decoupling nuclear structure from the lepton kinematics, and compares it with closure, non-closure and Taylor-expansion approximations for 82Se and 136Xe. It reports deviations in both the rates and the electron spectra from those traditional methods. This is relevant to the interpretation of double-beta decay spectra.

Thermal leptogenesis in the Babu-Nandi-Tavartkiladze model of neutrino mass

The paper studies thermal leptogenesis in the Babu-Nandi-Tavartkiladze model, in which neutrino masses arise from a dimension-7 operator at tree level and a dimension-5 operator at one loop. The model gives sub-eV neutrino masses with new physics at the TeV scale. The authors argue that leptogenesis here differs from canonical seesaw leptogenesis.

Investigation of neutrino electromagnetic properties at muon antimuon collider

The paper investigates two-photon neutrino interactions at a future muon collider, using dimension-7 effective operators and the process μ⁻γ → μ⁻νν̄ with a Weizsäcker–Williams photon. It uses MadGraph5_aMC@NLO and PYTHIA 8 to simulate signal and background. It probes neutrino electromagnetic properties in a way complementary to low-energy experiments.
