Experiments and results

IceCube Earth tomography

Atmospheric neutrinos, produced in cosmic-ray-induced air showers and detected by IceCube, are being used to probe the interior structure of the Earth, complementing indirect methods such as seismic and gravitational measurements.

Probing the Earth’s interior using atmospheric neutrinos — Read it
IceCube WavePID tool

A new tool called WavePID identifies neutrino interactions from the shape of the Cherenkov light pattern produced when a neutrino interacts with the ice, working at nanosecond timing precision.

WavePID: A tool that identifies neutrinos at the nanosecond level — Read it
DUNE AI data analysis

DUNE researchers are developing AI systems to sort through large volumes of detector data, identify neutrino interactions, spot signs of a stellar explosion, and detect problems inside the detector, building on machine-learning work carried out at Fermilab.

US lab to hunt most elusive ‘ghost particles’ deep underground with automated triggers — Read itDUNE uses AI to transform the future of neutrino research — Read it
DUNE detector stress test

A DUNE prototype detector was subjected to an extreme 300,000-volt stress trial to test its limits ahead of installation deep underground in the US.

300,000 volts: US team puts world’s largest neutrino detector through extreme stress test — Read it
JUNO calibration background

JUNO has detailed the radioactive-background budget of its calibration system, some of whose components sit close to the liquid-scintillator target and could contribute to the overall singles background rate. The collaboration reports extensive material screening and selection to keep this contribution under control, needed to meet the detector's background requirement for reactor-antineutrino analysis.

The Radioactive Background of the JUNO Calibration System — arXiv
NINJA emulsion measurement

The NINJA nuclear emulsion detector, exposed to the J-PARC muon-neutrino beam, has produced a measurement of charged-particle multiplicities and kinematic distributions in charged-current neutrino interactions on water. Eighty-two events were selected from a fiducial water volume and compared with Monte Carlo predictions.

Measurement of reconstructed final-state kinematics in charged-current interactions on … — arXiv

Theory highlights

recently published, with the links each record carries

  • This paper describes TAMBO, a mountain-based deep-valley neutrino observatory intended to help map the high-energy neutrino sky, an area where physicists have struggled to build a complete picture. The authors present it as a route to both astrophysical neutrino mapping and possible clues to new physics.

    Measuring the high-energy neutrino sky using the deep-valley neutrino observatory TAMBO — INSPIRE · DOI

  • The authors combine the latest neutrinoless double-beta decay search results with ab initio nuclear theory, using nuclear matrix elements from chiral effective field theory within the in-medium similarity renormalization group, to derive global Bayesian limits on the Majorana neutrino mass. In contrast to conventional nuclear models, the ab initio results indicate that the current generation of neutrinoless double-beta decay experiments has likely not yet reached the sensitivity needed to probe the mass region of interest.

    Global ab initio neutrino mass limits from neutrinoless double-beta decay — arXiv · INSPIRE · DOI

  • This paper proposes a minimal dark SU(2) gauge symmetry that produces a rank-two Dirac neutrino mass matrix, forcing one neutrino to be exactly massless while remaining consistent with oscillation data and cosmological constraints. Anomaly cancellation requires a second dark fermionic doublet, giving a fully specified, minimal dark-sector completion.

    Minimal dark S U ( 2 ) origin of a massless Dirac neutrino — arXiv · INSPIRE · DOI

  • Using the GENIE event generator, the authors benchmark a range of neutrino-argon interaction models — including different nucleon form factors, quasielastic cross-section treatments, and nuclear ground-state descriptions — against recent MicroBooNE measurements of pionless charged-current interactions. They find strong separation between competing models in some cases, offering clearer model choices for future neutrino-scattering simulations.

    Benchmarking state-of-the-art theory and empirical models of pionless neutrino-argon … — arXiv · INSPIRE · DOI

  • This study examines how well combined DUNE and NOvA data could constrain invisible decay of the third neutrino mass state into a sterile state under the normal mass ordering. The authors find that the true value of the mixing angle theta23 strongly affects the achievable constraint on the decay parameter, with the higher octant giving a notably tighter bound.

    Constraining invisible neutrino decay at NOνA and DUNE — INSPIRE · DOI

  • By treating the Peccei-Quinn symmetry as a flavour symmetry within a KSVZ axion framework combined with the type-I seesaw mechanism, this paper derives a specific zero texture in the inverse Majorana neutrino mass matrix. Together with the small measured value of theta13, this texture predicts an inverted neutrino mass ordering, linking axion-lepton couplings directly to the mass-ordering question.

    Neutrino mass hierarchy and axion–lepton couplings in a minimal flavored νKSVZ model — INSPIRE · DOI
