Experiments and results

IceCube Earth Probe

A new study examines how the IceCube Upgrade, a densely instrumented central region deployed during the 2025-26 polar season, could use atmospheric neutrino oscillations to probe Earth matter effects and constrain features of the Preliminary Reference Earth Model. Lowering the detector's energy threshold for multi-GeV atmospheric neutrinos sharpens this sensitivity to the planet's interior structure. A companion piece from the collaboration explains how atmospheric neutrinos, produced in cosmic-ray air showers, offer a probe of the Earth's interior that complements seismic and gravitational methods.

IceCube WavePID Tool

A new analysis tool, WavePID, identifies the character of a neutrino interaction from the nanosecond-scale timing and shape of the light pattern it produces when a neutrino interacts with the ice. The technique adds to the observatory's toolkit for classifying events without new hardware.

WavePID: A tool that identifies neutrinos at the nanosecond level — Read it
DUNE AI Data Triggers

Automated, AI-based triggers are under development to sift through the large data volumes DUNE will produce, aiming to identify neutrino interactions, flag signatures of a nearby stellar explosion, and catch problems inside the detector. The effort draws on decades of machine-learning work at Fermilab and is part of preparations for the experiment's eventual data analysis.

DUNE High-Voltage Test

The collaboration subjected a prototype detector to a 300,000-volt stress trial ahead of installation deep underground in the United States. The trial forms part of testing the detector's high-voltage system before construction of the full-scale far detector.

300,000 volts: US team puts world’s largest neutrino detector through extreme stress test — Read it
KM3NeT/ARCA Diffuse Search

Using data from configurations of 6 to 21 active detection units, corresponding to a total livetime of 640 days, the collaboration searched for a diffuse astrophysical neutrino flux across the full sky and from the Galactic Ridge. The all-sky analysis produced a fitted single-flavour flux under a single power-law spectral assumption, while the Galactic Ridge search did not yield constraints on the flux.

Search for an all-sky and a Galactic Ridge diffuse neutrino emission with the first 2 year — arXiv
NOvA Hydrogen Cross Section

The collaboration measured the cross section for muon antineutrino charged-current quasielastic scattering on hydrogen using the near detector, with an exposure of 1.2×10^21 protons on target in the NuMI beam. The selection yielded 35,509 signal events, the highest-statistics sample of antineutrino-hydrogen interactions measured to date, with backgrounds from heavier nuclei constrained using dedicated control samples.

LEGEND Nuclear Input

A new measurement examined the electron-capture decay of 76As into the first excited state of 76Ge, a decay branch relevant to the nuclear matrix elements used to interpret LEGEND's search for neutrinoless double beta decay in 76Ge. The work refines the experimental input on the branching of the 76As decay that feeds into those nuclear matrix element calculations.

Theory highlights

recently published, with preprint, record and journal

  • This paper presents the Tau Air-shower Mountain-Based Observatory (TAMBO), a mountain-based instrument concept aimed at mapping the high-energy neutrino sky, an area the field has struggled to picture fully despite a decade of progress in neutrino astronomy. The authors frame TAMBO's design as a route to filling that gap and to searching for signs of new physics along the way.

    Measuring the high-energy neutrino sky using the deep-valley neutrino observatory TAMBO — INSPIRE · DOI

  • Combining results from several neutrinoless double-beta decay searches with nuclear matrix elements computed ab initio in the in-medium similarity renormalization group approach, this paper derives global Bayesian limits on the Majorana neutrino mass. In contrast to phenomenological nuclear models, the ab initio calculations indicate that the current generation of neutrinoless double-beta decay experiments has likely not yet reached the sensitivity needed to probe the relevant mass scale.

    Global ab initio neutrino mass limits from neutrinoless double-beta decay — arXiv · INSPIRE · DOI

  • This paper derives neutrino non-standard interactions generated by trilinear LLE couplings in R-parity-violating supersymmetry with a split slepton spectrum, systematically classifying the resulting coupling patterns against existing experimental constraints. It finds that percent-level NSI can survive in selected diagonal channels, while most off-diagonal couplings remain restricted to the sub-percent level.

    Neutrino nonstandard interactions from \(LLE\)-type \(R\)-parity violation — INSPIRE · DOI

  • This paper proposes a secluded dark-matter framework built on an extra U(1) gauge symmetry, in which a Dirac dark-matter particle, heavy neutrinos, and a Higgs-mixing singlet scalar share the Yukawa structure that generates active neutrino masses. The construction ties the scalar-Higgs mixing angle directly to the heavy-neutrino masses, linking dark-matter phenomenology to the seesaw sector that sets neutrino mass.

    Neutrino mass and secluded dark matter puzzles solved together — INSPIRE · DOI

  • Using the JWST-revealed population of active galactic nuclei at redshifts above five as sources of ultrahigh-energy protons, this paper predicts a cosmogenic neutrino flux with a bump around 50 PeV from interactions with the high-redshift cosmic microwave background. The predicted flux is potentially consistent with IceCube's current neutrino intensity estimate, making confirmation of the 50-PeV feature a possible probe of ultrahigh-energy cosmic-ray emission from these early AGN.

    Ultrahigh-energy cosmogenic neutrino emissions in the high-redshift universe — arXiv · INSPIRE · DOI

  • Comparing two formalisms for defining the quantum-decoherence matrix, one in the matter mass eigenstate basis and one in the vacuum mass eigenstate basis rotated into matter, this paper shows the two approaches give different oscillation probabilities and different projected sensitivities to the decoherence parameter at the future DUNE and P2SO long-baseline experiments. The result cautions that decoherence-search sensitivities reported by long-baseline experiments depend on which formalism is assumed.

    Impact of different neutrino decoherence formalisms at the future long-baseline experiment — arXiv · INSPIRE · DOI
