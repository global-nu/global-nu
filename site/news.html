Experiments and results

DUNE AI systems

Researchers are preparing for data analysis by developing AI systems that sort through large volumes of data, identify neutrino interactions, spot signs of a stellar explosion, and detect problems inside the detector. The effort draws on decades of machine learning work carried out at Fermilab.

DUNE detector stress test

A prototype detector has been subjected to a 300,000-volt stress trial, testing its limits before installation deep underground in the US.

SURF and partner organisations held the 18th annual Neutrino Day in Lead, South Dakota, drawing crowds to a free science festival. IceCube Earth's interior

Atmospheric neutrinos, produced in cosmic-ray-induced air showers, are being used to investigate the structure of the Earth's interior, complementing indirect methods such as seismic and gravitational measurements.

IceCube WavePID tool

A newly described tool, WavePID, identifies neutrino interactions in the ice at the nanosecond level, using the shape of the light pattern produced by secondary particles when a neutrino interacts with a molecule of ice.

WavePID: A tool that identifies neutrinos at the nanosecond level — Read it
NOvA hydrogen cross-section

The collaboration reported a measurement of the muon-antineutrino charged-current quasi-elastic cross section on hydrogen using the near detector in the NuMI beam, drawn from a 1.2 × 10^21 proton-on-target exposure. A topological and kinematic selection yielded 35,509 signal events in the 10.8%-hydrogen-rich detector, the highest statistics of (anti)neutrino-hydrogen interactions measured to date, with backgrounds from heavier nuclei constrained using dedicated control samples.

Theory highlights

recently published, with the links each record carries

  • A paper in Nature Astronomy presents TAMBO, a proposed mountain-based observatory designed to help map the high-energy neutrino sky more completely than has so far been possible. The authors frame the project as a route to both filling gaps in that map and turning up clues of new physics.

    Measuring the high-energy neutrino sky using the deep-valley neutrino observatory TAMBO — INSPIRE · DOI

  • Combining likelihoods from several neutrinoless double-beta decay experiments with nuclear matrix elements computed in an ab initio in-medium similarity renormalization group approach, the authors derive global Bayesian limits on the Majorana neutrino mass. In contrast to results based on more conventional nuclear models, their ab initio calculation indicates that current-generation 0vbb experiments have likely not yet reached the sensitivity needed to probe the relevant mass range.

    Global ab initio neutrino mass limits from neutrinoless double-beta decay — arXiv · INSPIRE · DOI

  • Using the GENIE event generator, the authors compare a range of established and newer models of neutrino-argon interactions against recent MicroBooNE measurements of pionless charged-current events. They report a strong separation between competing models in some cases, pointing to concrete model choices for upcoming argon-based neutrino experiments.

    Benchmarking state-of-the-art theory and empirical models of pionless neutrino-argon … — arXiv · INSPIRE · DOI

  • The paper compares two different formalisms for introducing quantum decoherence into long-baseline neutrino oscillation probabilities, one defined in the matter mass basis and the other in the vacuum mass basis before rotation. It shows the two formalisms give different sensitivities at the future DUNE and P2SO experiments, a distinction relevant to how either experiment would interpret a decoherence signal.

    Impact of different neutrino decoherence formalisms at the future long-baseline … — arXiv · INSPIRE · DOI

  • Using energy density functional nuclear models, the authors calculate inclusive charged-current quasielastic neutrino scattering on carbon-12 and compare the results with MINERvA data. They examine how the in-medium effective mass of the nucleon affects the single- and double-differential cross sections, feeding into the nuclear modelling used by oscillation experiments.

    The role of effective mass in charged-current quasielastic neutrino-nucleus scattering … — INSPIRE · DOI

  • Assuming the heaviest neutrino mass state decays invisibly to a sterile state under the normal mass hierarchy, the authors study how well combined NOvA and DUNE data could constrain the decay parameter. They find that the true value of theta_23 has a significant effect on the achievable constraint on the decay parameter.

    Constraining invisible neutrino decay at NOνA and DUNE — INSPIRE · DOI
