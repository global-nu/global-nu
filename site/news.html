Experiments and results

Nobel Prize in Physics

The Royal Swedish Academy of Sciences announced on 6 October that the 2026 Nobel Prize in Physics goes to Francis Halzen of the University of Wisconsin–Madison, the principal investigator of IceCube. IceCube's announcement quotes the citation as being for decisive contributions to the IceCube Neutrino Observatory and the discovery of high-energy neutrinos of astrophysical origin. CERN's statement says the work is paving the way for a new generation of observatories that use neutrinos as a messenger of extreme environments.

KM3NeT construction

The KM3NeT Collaboration reports two further steps in marine operations for ARCA and ORCA in the past week. For ARCA, Cable Termination Frame 3 (CTF3) was installed at the deep-sea site during a campaign conducted by Alcatel Submarine Networks.

KM3NeT and ESFRI

KM3NeT announced that it has been recognised as a Landmark in the ESFRI Roadmap 2026. ESFRI is the European strategic body for coherent policy on major scientific facilities, and its Roadmap identifies the most critical next-generation infrastructures. Separately, the collaboration congratulated Francis Halzen on the Nobel Prize.

Galactic centre analysis

IceCube published a post on using IceCube and HAWC data to investigate the centre of the Galaxy. The post presents the region as a window into how cosmic rays are accelerated to extreme energies, with particular interest in particles reaching petaelectronvolt energies. It contains dense gas and potential accelerators, including a supermassive black hole.

Using IceCube and HAWC data to investigate the center of our galaxy — Read it
Baikal-GVD point sources

Baikal-GVD is a cubic-kilometre-scale detector under construction in Lake Baikal and is now 70% complete. A search for point-like astrophysical sources used a 5-year track-like dataset collected in 2019-2024. No significant source was found, so upper limits were computed for selected candidate objects and compared with published IceCube, ANTARES and KM3NeT results.

Search for Point-Like Neutrino Sources with Baikal-GVD — INSPIRE · DOI
T2K near detector upgrade

The ND280 upgrade of the T2K near detector was completed in 2024, and its leading role is to constrain neutrino-nucleus interaction modelling, a leading systematic uncertainty in T2K oscillation analyses. A new event selection targets muon-neutrino charged-current interactions with single positively charged pion production in the new modules. A separate paper proposes a neural network to distinguish electrons, photons, muons, pions and protons in the SuperFGD component.

Theory highlights

recently published, with the links each record carries

  • The authors propose a calculation of two-neutrino double-beta decay rates that keeps the full interdependence between nuclear structure and lepton kinematics, instead of decoupling them. They present deviations in rates and electron spectra from closure, non-closure and Taylor-expansion approximations for 82Se and 136Xe. This matters to anyone who relies on those approximations in double-beta decay analyses.

    Direct calculation of the two-neutrino double-beta decay rate including the full … — arXiv · INSPIRE · DOI

  • The paper studies scalar-mediated neutrino self-interactions acting on the diffuse supernova neutrino background as it propagates through the cosmic neutrino background. It uses a full three-flavour framework with the complete PMNS structure and considers four flavour-diagonal coupling structures. The motivation is that such interactions could leave spectral imprints on a background expected to be detected by next-generation observatories.

    Diffuse Supernova Neutrinos with Secret Neutrino Interactions — arXiv · INSPIRE · DOI

  • The paper investigates whether neutrinos from past Galactic core-collapse supernovae could be detected through nuclear recoil tracks in ancient minerals. It varies neutron-star masses, includes failed supernovae that form black holes, and considers the nuclear equation of state. Sensitivity is found to be enhanced for models with larger neutrino emission.

    Paleodetectors for neutrino signals from diverse Galactic stellar collapses — arXiv · INSPIRE · DOI

  • The paper examines neutrino mass and thermal leptogenesis in a model with a scalar quadruplet and two vectorlike fermion triplets, where masses arise from a dimension-7 operator at tree level and a dimension-5 operator at one loop. Sub-eV neutrino masses are accommodated even with a new-physics scale of order TeV. Leptogenesis in this setting differs from the canonical seesaw case.

    Thermal leptogenesis in the Babu-Nandi-Tavartkiladze model of neutrino mass — arXiv · INSPIRE · DOI

  • The authors study composite models in which heavy neutrinos are bound states of a near-conformal strongly coupled sector and mix with Standard Model neutrinos through an inverse seesaw. Matching yields transition dipole operators with couplings of order 10^-6 to 10^-8 GeV^-1, parametrically larger than loop-level predictions of minimal Dirac or Majorana models. They carry out an event-level simulation of the production-and-decay signature.

    Neutrino dipole moments and radiative signatures from partial compositeness — arXiv · INSPIRE · DOI

  • This experimental review covers high-energy neutrino astronomy, from the detection of a diffuse flux of TeV-PeV extraterrestrial neutrinos to the first identified individual sources. It describes the road to the present, the current status of the field and future developments. It is a convenient entry point for physicists outside the subfield.

    Neutrino Astronomy at High Energies-An Experimental Review — arXiv · INSPIRE · DOI
