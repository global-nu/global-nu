Theory highlights

recently published, with the links each record carries

  • This paper presents the first combined oscillation analysis merging atmospheric neutrino data from Super-Kamiokande, IceCube-DeepCore and KM3NeT/ORCA with reactor data from Daya Bay, fitting 839,048 events across 1536 bins with 91 parameters. The unified model describes all datasets without significant tensions and prefers the normal mass ordering over the inverted one at 3σ significance, a combination long thought infeasible outside single collaborations.

    Atmospheric neutrino oscillations: The full picture — arXiv · INSPIRE · DOI

  • This work combines results from multiple neutrinoless double-beta decay experiments with ab initio nuclear matrix elements from the in-medium similarity renormalization group approach to set global Bayesian limits on the Majorana neutrino mass. Unlike phenomenological nuclear models, the ab initio calculation indicates that current-generation 0νββ experiments have likely not yet reached the sensitivity needed to probe the target mass scale.

    Global ab initio neutrino mass limits from neutrinoless double-beta decay — arXiv · INSPIRE · DOI

  • This paper introduces TAMBO, a mountain-based neutrino observatory sited in a deep valley, aimed at mapping the high-energy neutrino sky more completely than existing instruments have managed. The authors argue the observatory could also turn up clues of new physics alongside its astronomy goals.

    Measuring the high-energy neutrino sky using the deep-valley neutrino observatory TAMBO — INSPIRE · DOI

  • The authors build an accretion-disk model for white dwarf tidal disruption events by intermediate-mass black holes, in which debris accretes at extremely super-Eddington rates. At the resulting high gas and radiation temperatures, positron-electron pair annihilation becomes prolific enough to produce MeV neutrino emission alongside the predicted gravitational-wave and photon signals.

    Multimessenger View of White Dwarf Tidal Disruption Events by Intermediate-mass Black … — arXiv · INSPIRE · DOI

  • This study revisits accretion-disk winds from active galactic nuclei as a possible source of the cosmic neutrino and gamma-ray backgrounds, calibrating a lepto-hadronic wind model against radio and GeV gamma-ray data from nearby Seyfert galaxies. Cosmic rays accelerated at the wind's forward and reverse shocks are shown to produce synchrotron, external-Compton and hadronic emission relevant to both backgrounds.

    Revisiting Disk Winds in Active Galactic Nuclei as an Origin of Cosmic Gamma-Ray and … — arXiv · INSPIRE · DOI

  • This paper proposes a minimal Standard Model extension in which a single U(1)PQ symmetry simultaneously drives an inverse seesaw mechanism for neutrino masses and stabilises a fermionic dark matter candidate, with the same symmetry breaking producing an axion-like particle that mediates between the two sectors. The framework generates neutrino masses at the TeV scale and predicts a correlation between the effective Majorana mass and a CP-violating phase that could be tested in future experiments.

    Fermionic dark matter and neutrino masses in a minimal U(1)PQ inverse seesaw framework — INSPIRE · DOI
