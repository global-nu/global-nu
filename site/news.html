DUNE construction

Construction of the Long-Baseline Neutrino Facility, which hosts DUNE, is entering its next phase, with critical components arriving. Super-Kamiokande DSNB

Super-Kamiokande reported an indication of electron antineutrinos from the diffuse supernova neutrino background, from a spectral fit to a 5003.0-day dataset. The analysis improves the rejection of atmospheric-neutrino backgrounds, the evaluation of systematic uncertainties and the spectral fitting method. For a representative model the best-fit flux is 3.6 +1.6/−1.5 cm⁻² s⁻¹ above 13.3 MeV, a 2.6σ tension with the null hypothesis. Other signal spectral shapes gave similar significances.

CONUS+ at Leibstadt

CONUS+ reports the most precise measurement to date of reactor coherent elastic neutrino-nucleus scattering, using germanium detectors. An exposure of 1296 kg·days reactor-on gave 1555 ± 152 events at 10.2σ, within 0.6 standard deviations of the Standard Model expectation of 1701 ± 198. The same data give sin²θ_W = 0.225 ± 0.022 at a momentum transfer of about 10 MeV. A companion paper describes the run-2 analysis, after three of the original 1 kg detectors were replaced with larger 2.42 kg diodes.

SBND long-lived particles

SBND is a liquid argon time projection chamber 110 m from the target on the Booster Neutrino Beam. Neutrino data taking with the full detector began in December 2024. A new search for long-lived particles from neutrino-nucleus upscattering uses a subset of its Cosmic Ray Tagger, which ran as a standalone detector in 2017–2018.

Baikal-GVD

Baikal-GVD is a cubic-kilometre-scale detector under construction in Lake Baikal and is now 70% complete. A search for point-like sources in five years of track-like events from 2019–2024 found no significant source. The authors therefore compute upper limits for selected candidate objects and compare them with published IceCube, ANTARES and KM3NeT results.

T2K SuperFGD

SuperFGD is a key component of the upgrade of the T2K near detector ND280. It is a segmented plastic scintillator made of about 2 million optically isolated 1 cm³ cubes, which provides 3D images of neutrino interactions. A paper on its performance in the T2K beam covers its design, calibration and parameters such as light yield and time resolution.

Neutrino Emissivity in Δ -Direct-Urca Processes Inside Neutron Stars

The paper examines the contribution of Δ-direct-Urca processes to neutrino emissivity in neutron stars, where Δ isobars may accompany nucleons and hyperons in the dense core. The authors note that this contribution had not been explicitly evaluated before. It matters for neutrino cooling of neutron stars, in which Urca processes are the dominant mechanisms.

The paper studies a gauged U(1) Lμ−Lτ model with an ultralight vector boson that mixes kinetically with the photon. When can decoherence reveal a Majorana phase? A phase-covariance no-go theorem for two-flavor neutrino oscillations

The paper proves a no-go theorem for observing a Majorana phase in open two-flavour neutrino oscillations: any completely positive trace-preserving evolution covariant under rotations of the mass-coherence plane is exactly blind to the phase. A rate anisotropy in the environment can supply a phase reference. This identifies when decoherence could in principle reveal a Majorana phase.

Thermal leptogenesis in the Babu-Nandi-Tavartkiladze model of neutrino mass

The paper studies neutrino mass and thermal leptogenesis in the Babu-Nandi-Tavartkiladze model, with a scalar quadruplet and vectorlike fermion triplets. Neutrino masses arise from a dimension-7 operator at tree level and a dimension-5 operator at one loop, so sub-eV masses are possible with new physics at the TeV scale. Leptogenesis here differs from the canonical seesaw case, which makes the model a testable target.

Paleodetectors for neutrino signals from diverse Galactic stellar collapses

The paper investigates whether neutrinos from past Galactic core-collapse supernovae could be detected through nuclear-recoil tracks in ancient minerals, called paleodetectors. It varies neutron-star masses, includes failed supernovae that form black holes, and considers the role of the nuclear equation of state. Sensitivity is enhanced for models with larger neutrino emission, in particular more compact remnants.

Fermionic dark matter and neutrino masses in a minimal U(1)PQ inverse seesaw framework

The paper proposes a minimal extension of the Standard Model in which a single global U(1)PQ symmetry sets the scales of the inverse seesaw and stabilises a fermionic dark matter candidate. The resulting axion-like particle mediates between the dark and visible sectors. It predicts a strong correlation between the effective Majorana mass and the CP-violating phase, which makes the model testable in future experiments.
