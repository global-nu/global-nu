Experiments and results

Nobel Prize in Physics

On 6 October the Royal Swedish Academy of Sciences announced the 2026 Nobel Prize in Physics for Francis Halzen of the University of Wisconsin–Madison. The citation, as quoted by IceCube, reads "for decisive contributions to the IceCube Neutrino Observatory and the discovery of high-energy neutrinos of astrophysical origin". CERN describes the work as the discovery of high-energy neutrinos coming from far beyond our Solar System, and IceCube states that Halzen proposed and proved that ice could be used as a detection medium.

ARCA and ORCA construction

The KM3NeT Collaboration reports a period of marine operations with two further steps for ARCA and ORCA. At the ARCA deep-sea site, Cable Termination Frame 3 (CTF3) was installed during a campaign conducted by Alcatel Submarine Networks. Separately, KM3NeT announced that it has been recognised as a Landmark in the ESFRI Roadmap 2026.

SBND detector paper

The SBND Collaboration published a paper describing the as-built detector and its first year of beam operation. SBND is a 112 tonne active-mass liquid argon time projection chamber in the Booster Neutrino Beam at Fermilab, part of the Short-Baseline Neutrino Program, and has operated since 2024. The paper reports that all detector systems performed at or above design requirements, with an electron lifetime exceeding 30 ms, ten times the design requirement, for more than 95% of Run 1.

Short-Baseline Near Detector (SBND): Design and Initial Performance — arXiv
PandaX-4T supernova background

PandaX-4T presents the first search for the diffuse supernova neutrino background using coherent elastic neutrino-nucleus scattering. With a total exposure of 1.54 tonne·year from the commissioning and first science runs, it observed no significant event excess over the expected background. The collaboration sets a 90% confidence level upper limit of (2.27–2.43) × 10^3 cm^-2 s^-1 per flavour on the ν_x flux, for neutrino energies above 19.3 MeV.

Search for Diffuse Supernova Neutrino Background of All Flavors with PandaX-4T — arXiv
Combined atmospheric fit

A preprint combines the latest publicly available atmospheric-neutrino data from Super-Kamiokande (all stages before the gadolinium phase), IceCube-DeepCore and the six-detection-unit configuration of KM3NeT/ORCA. All event samples are fitted simultaneously, with shared systematic uncertainties treated as fully correlated and detector-response uncertainties modelled independently for each experiment. The authors present the result as evidence for normal neutrino ordering and also quote constraints on the atmospheric oscillation parameters.

Evidence for Normal Neutrino Ordering with Combined Atmospheric Neutrino Experiments — arXiv
JUNO and an empirical relation

A single-author preprint takes the JUNO Collaboration's reported measurements of Δm²_21 and Δm²_31, which it describes as the most precise to date. It tests an empirical relation between the two mass-squared differences, proposed on empirical grounds, whose combination of square roots is predicted to equal √2. The author reports a value of 1.4143 (+0.0036, −0.0038), a relative precision of 0.27%, and concludes that the relation agrees excellently with the data. This is the author's interpretation, not a JUNO result.

Theory highlights

recently published, with the links each record carries

  • This experimental review traces how high-energy neutrino astronomy developed, from the detection of a diffuse extraterrestrial flux at TeV and PeV energies to the first identified individual sources. It also covers the present status and sketches future developments. It is a useful single reference for the field behind this year's Nobel Prize.

    Neutrino Astronomy at High Energies-An Experimental Review — arXiv · INSPIRE · DOI

  • The paper presents a search for astrophysical point-like neutrino sources using five years of track-like events (2019–2024) from Baikal-GVD, a cubic-kilometre detector in Lake Baikal that is now 70% complete. No significant source was found, so upper limits are computed for selected candidate objects and compared with IceCube, ANTARES and KM3NeT results.

    Search for Point-Like Neutrino Sources with Baikal-GVD — INSPIRE · DOI

  • The authors compute two-neutrino double-beta decay rates without decoupling nuclear structure from the lepton kinematics, keeping the full lepton-energy dependence in one formula. They compare decay rates and electron spectra with the closure, non-closure and Taylor-expansion approximations for 82Se and 136Xe. This matters to anyone interpreting double-beta spectra, where the two-neutrino mode is the background to the neutrinoless search.

    Direct calculation of the two-neutrino double-beta decay rate including the full … — arXiv · INSPIRE · DOI

  • The paper asks whether nuclear-recoil tracks in ancient minerals could record neutrinos from past Galactic core-collapse supernovae. It varies neutron-star masses, includes failed supernovae that form black holes, and considers the role of the nuclear equation of state. It quantifies how the sensitivity grows for models with larger neutrino emission.

    Paleodetectors for neutrino signals from diverse Galactic stellar collapses — arXiv · INSPIRE · DOI

  • The authors study how scalar-mediated neutrino self-interactions would imprint on the diffuse supernova neutrino background as it crosses the cosmic neutrino background. They use a full three-flavour framework that keeps the complete PMNS structure and consider four representative flavour-diagonal coupling structures. This links future background detection to tests of non-standard neutrino interactions.

    Diffuse Supernova Neutrinos with Secret Neutrino Interactions — arXiv · INSPIRE · DOI

  • The paper considers composite neutrino models in which heavy neutrinos are bound states of a near-conformal strongly coupled sector, mixing with Standard Model neutrinos through an inverse seesaw. Matching the conformal dynamics onto the low-energy theory gives transition dipole operators parametrically larger than in minimal Dirac or Majorana models, and the authors simulate the resulting production-and-decay signatures at event level. It offers a concrete route from small neutrino masses to testable electromagnetic couplings.

    Neutrino dipole moments and radiative signatures from partial compositeness — arXiv · INSPIRE · DOI
