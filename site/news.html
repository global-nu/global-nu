DUNE construction

Fermilab, CERN and SURF crews have started building the first of two cryostats, the insulated containers that will hold liquid argon for DUNE. Fermilab News also reports that construction of the underground detector has begun in South Dakota, with the University of Texas at Arlington working on the field cage and other components. T2K with gadolinium

T2K presented updated oscillation parameter measurements that include, for the first time, data taken with gadolinium-doped Super-Kamiokande. The analysis adds neutrino data relative to the previous result and has an improved far-detector selection and systematic treatment. Both the semi-frequentist and the Bayesian analyses favour normal mass ordering. They report results on sin²θ23, Δm²32, sin²θ13 and δCP.

KM3NeT/ARCA diffuse flux

KM3NeT searched for an all-sky and a Galactic Ridge diffuse neutrino flux in the first 2 years of ARCA data. The dataset comes from detector configurations with 6, 8, 19 and 21 detection units and has a livetime of 640 days. Under a single power-law assumption, the all-sky fit gives a spectral index of 3.00 with an asymmetric uncertainty of +0.30 and -0.35 at 68% credible level.

SBND electron neutrinos

SBND measured the total and single-differential inclusive electron neutrino charged-current cross sections on argon. The sample is 95.2% νe and 4.8% antiνe. The collaboration describes it as the highest-statistics inclusive νe + antiνe measurement on argon to date and the first one with the Booster Neutrino Beam. The channel feeds future electron-neutrino appearance studies in long- and short-baseline oscillation analyses.

PIKACHU background study

The PIKACHU Phase 1 search for two-neutrino double-beta decay of 160Gd uses Ce:GAGG scintillators at the Kamioka underground laboratory. The new paper characterises the backgrounds with delayed-coincidence analysis and GEANT4-based spectral fits. Surface contamination of the crystal, seen as a distortion of the 214Po alpha spectrum, led to an improved background model. No significant excess from 160Gd 2ν2β decay was observed.

NνDEx simulation study

NνDEx plans a search for neutrinoless double-beta decay of 82Se in a high-pressure 82SeF6 negative-ion time projection chamber. A new simulation study compares FFT deconvolution with direct waveform fitting for reconstructing the arrival-time separation of SeF5⁻ and SeF6⁻ ions, which estimates the longitudinal coordinate without an external trigger. Direct fitting accepted 873 and FFT accepted 855 of 873 geometry-selected single depositions. On the 855 common events the arrival-time separation RMSEs were 0.180 and 0.222 ms respectively.

Determination of the spatial extent of a neutrino wave packet from electron capture decay experiments

The paper revisits the 7Li recoil-peak measurement after 7Be electron capture reported by Smolsky et al. It takes the measured width (FWHM = 6.7 eV, σE = 2.9 eV) to infer the spatial extent of the neutrino wave packet, and ties that extent to the production mechanism and to decoherence. It bears on how wave-packet size is constrained by laboratory data.

When can decoherence reveal a Majorana phase? A phase-covariance no-go theorem for two-flavor neutrino oscillations

The author proves a no-go theorem for observing a Majorana phase in open two-flavour neutrino oscillations. Every completely positive trace-preserving evolution that is covariant under rotations of the mass-coherence plane is exactly blind to the phase, including the standard nonunital phase-covariant qubit semigroups. Within the unital Pauli-GKSL class, a rate anisotropy between the two transverse rates supplies an environmental phase reference.

Neutrino Emissivity in Δ -Direct-Urca Processes Inside Neutron Stars

The authors compute the neutrino emissivity of Δ-direct-Urca processes in neutron-star matter containing Δ isobars. Quasisteady flavor configuration of multienergy neutrino ensembles

The paper gives semianalytic approximations for the quasisteady outcome of slow and fast flavour conversion in quasihomogeneous neutrino ensembles. The authors state that the ansatz agrees well with multiangle and multienergy solutions of the kinetic equations, independent of the mass ordering. The goal is a subgrid scheme for supernova simulations that cannot include neutrino quantum kinetics.

Toward measuring the low-energy cosmic-ray spectrum with next-generation neutrino detectors

The paper asks how well kiloton-scale detectors such as Hyper-Kamiokande could determine the primary cosmic-ray spectrum from the atmospheric neutrino flux. The authors find that future detectors can distinguish between cosmic-ray models with high statistical significance, including uncertainties in neutrino cross sections and hadronic interactions. They also introduce a technique for reconstructing the primary spectrum.
