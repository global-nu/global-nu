Experiments and results

IceCube funding renewal

The US National Science Foundation renewed its cooperative agreement with the University of Wisconsin-Madison to operate and maintain IceCube, a five-year, $53 million award covering 2026-2031.

The U.S. National Science Foundation renews NSF IceCube operation and maintenance contract — Read it
IceCube Upgrade sensitivity

The IceCube Upgrade, installed within the DeepCore infill array in 2025/2026 with new densely instrumented multi-PMT optical modules, is expected to enhance sensitivity in the GeV regime, with commissioning expected to be complete by the end of 2026. A new study projects its performance for neutrino transient searches, steady point-source emission such as from NGC 1068, and diffuse emission.

Astrophysical Sensitivity Projections for the IceCube Upgrade — arXiv
Atmospheric neutrinos probe Earth

IceCube researchers are using atmospheric neutrinos, produced by cosmic-ray air showers, to investigate the properties of Earth's interior, complementing seismic and gravitational measurements.

Probing the Earth’s interior using atmospheric neutrinos — Read it
DUNE's AI push

DUNE researchers are developing AI systems to sort through the experiment's data, identify neutrino interactions, flag possible signs of a stellar explosion, and detect problems inside the detector, building on machine-learning expertise at Fermilab.

US lab to hunt most elusive ‘ghost particles’ deep underground with automated triggers — Read itDUNE uses AI to transform the future of neutrino research — Read it
Prototype detector stress test

A DUNE prototype detector was subjected to an extreme 300,000-volt stress trial ahead of its installation deep underground, testing the limits of the design before the full-scale detectors are built.

300,000 volts: US team puts world’s largest neutrino detector through extreme stress test — Read it
Faster DUNE photon simulation

A GPU-based implementation called Opticks was applied to DUNE's far-detector horizontal-drift geometry, propagating the same optical photons as GEANT4 with a speedup of about 313 over single-threaded GEANT4, easing one of the most computationally demanding parts of the detector simulation.

Accelerating Optical Photon Simulation in DUNE with Opticks — arXiv
NUCLEUS reactor CEνNS

The NUCLEUS experiment, which aims to measure coherent elastic neutrino-nucleus scattering from reactor antineutrinos at the Chooz nuclear power plant, has developed and commissioned the cryogenic target-detector module for its Technical Run, comprising four gram-scale CaWO4 detectors with a total target mass of 6.96 g.

Development and Commissioning of the Cryogenic Target Detectors for the Technical Run of … — arXiv
RENO near-detector concept

A study evaluated the expected performance of a proposed 170-ton water Cherenkov detector at the RENO near-detector site for measuring reactor antineutrino-electron elastic scattering, estimating the precision achievable on the reactor spectrum normalisation under different event-selection scenarios.

Expected performance of a water Cherenkov detector for reactor antineutrino--electron … — arXiv
ESSνSB detector design

A study explored near water-Cherenkov detector design options for the proposed ESSνSB experiment, using graph neural networks to classify electron- and muon-neutrino charged-current events and testing whether a smaller, less-instrumented detector could still meet the required flavour-identification performance.

Theory highlights

recently published, with the links each record carries

  • A perspective on TAMBO, a mountain-based observatory designed to detect tau neutrinos through Earth-skimming interactions, describes the instrument's aim to help complete the map of the high-energy neutrino sky and search for signs of new physics.

    Measuring the high-energy neutrino sky using the deep-valley neutrino observatory TAMBO — INSPIRE · DOI

  • This paper combines results from current neutrinoless double-beta decay searches with nuclear matrix elements from ab initio chiral effective field theory to set global Bayesian limits on the Majorana neutrino mass, concluding that the current generation of experiments has likely not yet reached the sensitivity that other nuclear models suggested.

    Global ab initio neutrino mass limits from neutrinoless double-beta decay — arXiv · INSPIRE · DOI

  • Using the GENIE event generator, this study benchmarks a range of pionless neutrino-argon interaction models against recent MicroBooNE charged-current measurements, finding clear separations between competing models that could guide choices for upcoming argon-based neutrino experiments.

    Benchmarking state-of-the-art theory and empirical models of pionless neutrino-argon … — arXiv · INSPIRE · DOI

  • This paper revisits accretion-disk winds from active galactic nuclei as a possible source of the cosmic neutrino and gamma-ray backgrounds, building a lepto-hadronic wind model calibrated against radio and GeV data from nearby Seyfert galaxies.

    Revisiting Disk Winds in Active Galactic Nuclei as an Origin of Cosmic Gamma-Ray and … — arXiv · INSPIRE · DOI

  • By treating the Peccei-Quinn symmetry as a flavour symmetry within a KSVZ axion framework combined with the type-I seesaw, this paper derives a texture for the inverse Majorana neutrino mass matrix that predicts an inverted neutrino mass ordering together with specific axion-lepton couplings.

    Neutrino mass hierarchy and axion–lepton couplings in a minimal flavored νKSVZ model — INSPIRE · DOI

  • This paper constructs a radiative neutrino mass model in which semi-annihilation of Dirac dark matter produces boosted dark matter and a neutrino, finding that DARWIN could probe the scenario for dark matter masses of 20-50 MeV.

    A radiative neutrino mass model with semi-annihilating boosted dark matter — arXiv · INSPIRE · DOI
