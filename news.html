Nobel Prize for Halzen

The Royal Swedish Academy of Science announced the 2026 Nobel Prize in Physics for Francis Halzen, IceCube principal investigator, for decisive contributions to the IceCube Neutrino Observatory and the discovery of high-energy neutrinos of astrophysical origin. JUNO-TAO first data

The JUNO Collaboration reports commissioning performance and first results from the Taishan Antineutrino Observatory, a satellite of JUNO. It uses a 2.8-ton liquid-scintillator detector with 4,024 silicon photomultiplier tiles, 43.2 m from a reactor core, to measure the reactor antineutrino spectrum. Installation was completed in 2025 and physics data taking began on 10 February 2026. Stable cryogenic operation has been achieved.

T2K ND280 upgrade

The ND280 upgrade was completed in 2024. Its SuperFGD is a segmented scintillator detector of about 2 million optically isolated 1 cm³ cubes read out by three orthogonal wavelength-shifting fibres. A new event selection for muon-neutrino charged-current interactions with single positively charged pion production targets the new modules, as a step towards reducing the dominant systematic uncertainties of the oscillation analysis.

Neutrino Emissivity in Δ -Direct-Urca Processes Inside Neutron Stars

The paper studies the neutrino emissivity of Δ-direct-Urca processes in neutron stars, which are expected to contain Δ isobars if observed massive stars include heavier baryons. It matters for neutron-star thermal evolution, where nucleonic and hyperonic Urca processes are the dominant cooling mechanisms.

The paper extends the Standard Model with a gauged U(1) Lμ−Lτ symmetry and an ultralight vector boson, which modifies solar-neutrino MSW oscillations through kinetic mixing with the photon. It uses Borexino, SNO and Super-Kamiokande solar data and discusses prospects for JUNO and XLZD.

Direct calculation of the two-neutrino double-beta decay rate including the full lepton-energy dependence

The authors calculate two-neutrino double-beta decay rates without decoupling nuclear structure from the lepton kinematics, and compare with closure, non-closure and Taylor-expansion approximations for 82Se and 136Xe. This bears on the two-neutrino spectrum and rate, which underlie neutrinoless double-beta decay searches in these isotopes.

Paleodetectors for neutrino signals from diverse Galactic stellar collapses

The paper assesses whether nuclear recoil tracks in ancient minerals could register neutrinos from past Galactic core-collapse supernovae. It varies neutron-star masses and includes failed supernovae that form black holes, and examines the role of the nuclear equation of state. Sensitivity is larger for models with larger neutrino emission.

Thermal leptogenesis in the Babu-Nandi-Tavartkiladze model of neutrino mass

The paper examines thermal leptogenesis in the Babu-Nandi-Tavartkiladze model, in which neutrino masses arise from a dimension-7 operator at tree level and a dimension-5 operator at one loop. The model accommodates sub-eV neutrino masses with new physics at the TeV scale. The authors state that leptogenesis here differs from the canonical seesaw case.

Magnetorotational Supernova Neutrino Spectra Versus Mass Ordering

The paper considers magnetic and oscillation effects on neutrinos from a protoneutron star in a supernova. It finds that oscillations in the outer stellar layers harden the electron antineutrino spectrum, most strongly for the inverted mass ordering. This is relevant to the dependence of ground-based supernova-neutrino detection on the mass ordering.
