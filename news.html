Experiments and results

JUNO oscillation fit

The collaboration reports an improved measurement of oscillation parameters from 207.2 live days of data, comprising 8,294 inverse beta-decay candidates at an average reactor baseline of 52.5 km. The precision on the atmospheric splitting Δm²31 is approximately 1%, described as world-leading. The fit gives Δm²31 = +2.509 (+0.027/−0.025) ×10⁻³ eV² for normal ordering and −2.482 (+0.025/−0.026) ×10⁻³ eV² for inverted ordering, with the Daya Bay constraint used in the fit, and the paper presents this as an indication on the mass ordering.

DUNE cryostat steel

Crews at the Sanford Underground Research Facility have moved about 39% of the I-beam steel needed to build the cryostat. The steel already placed fills the south cavern, which is 500 feet long, 65 feet wide and about 7 stories tall. Separately, the collaboration met at Unicamp, where the Fermilab report mentions an evaluation day.

KM3NeT ARCA

A sea campaign at the ARCA site over the past two weeks, announced on 23 September 2026, is described as very successful. It enlarged the submarine infrastructure and the detector and included an upgrade, which the collaboration calls a significant step in construction. MicroBooNE cross sections

The collaboration presents the first double-differential cross-section measurements of muon-neutrino charged-current interactions on argon with no mesons and one or more protons. They are given as a function of proton multiplicity and kinematics, up to the fourth-leading proton. The analysis uses the full Booster Neutrino Beam dataset, corresponding to 1.30×10²¹ protons on target.

MINERvA superscaling

The collaboration reports the first measurement of the superscaling variable ψ′ built from visible final-state kinematics. It uses charged-current quasielastic-like muon-neutrino interactions on carbon in the medium-energy NuMI beam. The variable is used as an externally calibrated quantity whose peak position is sensitive to the invisible nuclear missing energy.

EXO-200 public data

A subset of EXO-200 calibration data has been released publicly for machine-learning applications. It contains about one million ²²⁸Th calibration events taken between 26 February and 2 March 2018, near the end of Phase-II. For each event it provides raw U-wire, V-wire and APD waveforms together with reconstructed charge, light and rotated energies, distributed in HDF5 format.

ANTARES solar WIMPs

The collaboration has searched the full ANTARES dataset for neutrinos from WIMP annihilation in the Sun. The detector is sensitive from a few tens of GeV to the TeV scale, which suits WIMP masses below a few TeV. The excerpt available here does not state the result.

Theory highlights

recently published, with the links each record carries

  • This paper presents what it calls the first combined oscillation analysis of recent atmospheric datasets from Super-Kamiokande, IceCube-DeepCore and KM3NeT/ORCA, together with Daya Bay reactor data. A single physics model describes all datasets with no significant parameter tensions, and the fit prefers normal over inverted ordering at 3σ. It matters because such combinations had long been considered infeasible outside the collaborations.

    Atmospheric neutrino oscillations: The full picture — arXiv · INSPIRE · DOI

  • The authors combine current neutrinoless double-beta decay results with nuclear matrix elements from ab initio calculations based on chiral effective field theory, in a Bayesian framework. They conclude that, in contrast to nuclear models, the ab initio results indicate that the current generation of experiments has likely not yet reached the required sensitivities. This bears directly on how far present searches can be read as probes of Majorana neutrino mass.

    Global ab initio neutrino mass limits from neutrinoless double-beta decay — arXiv · INSPIRE · DOI

  • The paper calculates two-neutrino double-beta decay rates without decoupling nuclear structure from the lepton kinematics. It compares the resulting rates and electron spectra with closure, non-closure and Taylor-expansion approximations for ⁸²Se and ⁸⁸Xe-type cases, specifically ⁸²Se and ¹³⁶Xe. This is relevant to anyone modelling the two-neutrino spectrum as a background or a benchmark in double-beta searches.

    Direct calculation of the two-neutrino double-beta decay rate including the full … — arXiv · INSPIRE · DOI

  • The authors ask whether neutrinos from past Galactic core-collapse supernovae could be detected as nuclear recoil tracks in ancient minerals. They vary neutron-star masses, include failed supernovae that form black holes, and consider the role of the nuclear equation of state. They find that sensitivity is enhanced for models with larger neutrino emission.

    Paleodetectors for neutrino signals from diverse Galactic stellar collapses — arXiv · INSPIRE · DOI

  • The paper studies thermal leptogenesis in a model with a scalar quadruplet and two vectorlike fermion triplets. Neutrino masses arise from a dimension-7 operator at tree level and a dimension-5 operator at one loop, and sub-eV masses are accommodated even for a new-physics scale of O(TeV). The authors argue this makes the model a target for experimental searches and examine leptogenesis in a setting that differs from canonical seesaw leptogenesis.

    Thermal leptogenesis in the Babu-Nandi-Tavartkiladze model of neutrino mass — arXiv · INSPIRE · DOI

  • The paper considers magnetorotational supernova neutrino spectra, including oscillation effects in the outer layers of the star. It finds that the hardness of the electron antineutrino spectrum increases significantly. The enhancement is most pronounced for the inverted mass ordering, which the authors say favours ground-based detection.

    Magnetorotational Supernova Neutrino Spectra Versus Mass Ordering — INSPIRE · DOI
