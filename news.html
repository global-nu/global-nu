Experiments and results

IceCube Upgrade

The collaboration published projected sensitivities for the IceCube Upgrade, the new extension installed in the existing DeepCore infill array in 2025/2026 that adds densely instrumented multi-PMT optical modules, with commissioning expected to be complete by the end of 2026. The projections cover neutrino transient searches, steady point-source emission such as from NGC 1068, and diffuse emission.

IceCube Funding Renewal

The US National Science Foundation renewed its cooperative agreement with the University of Wisconsin-Madison to operate and maintain the NSF IceCube Neutrino Observatory. The five-year, $53 million agreement covers operations from 2026 to 2031.

DUNE AI Triggers

DUNE researchers are developing AI systems to sort through the experiment's data, aiming to identify neutrino interactions, spot signs of a stellar explosion, and detect problems inside the detector automatically. The effort builds on decades of machine-learning work at Fermilab.

DUNE Detector Stress Test

A DUNE prototype detector was subjected to an extreme 300,000-volt stress trial, testing its limits ahead of installation deep underground in the US.

XENONnT Solar Neutrinos

The XENON Collaboration reported the first measurement of solar neutrinos through elastic neutrino-electron scattering at the keV scale, using 2.46 tonne-years of exposure from XENONnT's first two science runs and covering electron recoil energies between 1 and 140 keV. They rejected the background-only hypothesis at 5.0 sigma and measured a solar pp neutrino flux of (10.2 ± 2.0) × 10^10 cm^-2 s^-1, noting the result is larger than expectation.

SuperCDMS SNOLAB

The deep-underground SuperCDMS SNOLAB experiment has begun a preliminary phase of its dark matter search. The collaboration says early scientific results are possible even before the detector reaches optimal sensitivity when the full search begins next year.

SuperCDMS SNOLAB begins preliminary phase of dark matter hunt — Read it
Super-K Proton Decay Search

Using the full 401 kiloton-year exposure of Super-Kamiokande's pure water phase, the collaboration searched for proton decay to a charged antilepton plus an invisible massless particle and found no significant signal above the atmospheric neutrino background. They set 90% confidence-level lower limits on the partial proton lifetime of 1.72 × 10^33 years for the positron channel and 0.61 × 10^33 years for the muon channel, improving on previous limits by factors of 2 and 1.5 respectively.

HAWC Anomalous Events

An independent analysis examined two track-like events reported by the HAWC Collaboration in a search for Earth-skimming neutrinos, pointing back to the Pico de Orizaba volcano, and found them kinematically inconsistent with all identified backgrounds using published HAWC information. The analysis places the in-tank energy deposit above roughly 190-210 GeV, or a worst case of 122-136 GeV, exceeding the 100 GeV maximum expected from backgrounds.

Anomalous horizontal track-like events at the HAWC observatory: candidate … — arXiv
ARA Diffuse UHE Search

The Askaryan Radio Array presented its first array-wide diffuse search for ultra-high-energy neutrinos, using data collected from January 2013 to December 2023 across its five autonomous South Pole stations, the largest livetime of any in-ice radio array. The analysis incorporates improved detector characterization, data-driven noise and electronics models, and updated interaction and lepton-propagation modelling.

The First Array-Wide Diffuse Flux Search for UHE Neutrinos with the Askaryan Radio Array — arXiv
IceCube Extra Dimensions

Using 10.7 years of upward-going muon neutrino data between 0.5 and 100 TeV, IceCube searched for the spectral distortions that low-scale gravity theories addressing the hierarchy problem predict from matter effects as neutrinos cross the Earth. The analysis placed constraints on the compactification scale in large extra dimension models.

Searching for Extra Dimensions and Copies of the Standard Model with IceCube — arXiv

Theory highlights

recently published, with the links each record carries

  • This paper derives, for the first time, the effective Lagrangian describing photon-neutrino interaction by considering neutrino pair emission from a variable classical electromagnetic field in the second Born approximation. It identifies a class of fields for which the effect vanishes, extending nonlinear QED-type processes to neutrinos.

    Emission of pairs of neutrinos by a variable field in the second Born approximation … — INSPIRE · DOI

  • This paper investigates non-standard neutrino-two-photon interactions at a future muon-antimuon collider using a model-independent effective field theory with dimension-7 operators, simulating signal and background with MadGraph5_aMC@NLO and PYTHIA8. It gives neutrino physicists a concrete sensitivity estimate for probing anomalous neutrino electromagnetic couplings at high-energy lepton colliders.

    Investigation of neutrino electromagnetic properties at muon antimuon collider — INSPIRE · DOI

  • This paper builds a minimal flavoured axion model combining the KSVZ framework with a type-I seesaw, in which flavour-dependent Peccei-Quinn charges fix a specific zero texture in the inverse Majorana neutrino mass matrix. Together with the observed smallness of theta13, the texture predicts an inverted neutrino mass ordering with 17 to 45 for the ratio of the lightest to heaviest mass eigenstates.

    Neutrino mass hierarchy and axion–lepton couplings in a minimal flavored νKSVZ model — INSPIRE · DOI

  • This paper studies how well combined NOvA and DUNE data could constrain invisible decay of the third neutrino mass state into a sterile state under the normal mass hierarchy. It finds that the true value of theta23 substantially affects the achievable constraint on the decay parameter, with the higher octant improving sensitivity.

    Constraining invisible neutrino decay at NOνA and DUNE — INSPIRE · DOI

  • This paper models three-flavour neutrino oscillations as a qutrit coupled to random telegraph noise acting as pure dephasing in the mass basis, deriving time-dependent dephasing rates and propagating survival probabilities and flavour coherence. It shows the noise's switching rate relative to the coupling strength determines whether coherence decays smoothly or shows memory-induced revivals, with implications for how decoherence is modelled in oscillation analyses.

    Non-Markovian Dephasing and Flavor Coherence in a Three-Flavor Neutrino Qutrit — INSPIRE · DOI

  • This paper develops an open-quantum-system formalism for the response of superfluid helium-4 to low-energy neutrino scattering, targeting coherent elastic neutrino-atom scattering, a process not yet observed. It is directly relevant to a planned experiment at the National Center for Physics and Mathematics in Sarov that would use a high-intensity tritium neutrino source with a superfluid He-4 detector to probe neutrino physics at very low energies.

    The Formalism of Open Quantum Systems for Superfluid Helium Response to Neutrino … — INSPIRE · DOI
