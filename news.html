In summary. This week's neutrino news is dominated by ongoing searches and infrastructure rather than new discoveries: IceCube reports constraints from an extra-dimensions search and outlines projected sensitivities for its planned Upgrade, Super-Kamiokande has tightened proton-decay lifetime limits, and KM3NeT and IceCube both report source-stacking or coincidence searches for astrophysical neutrinos with no signal announced. On the theory side, published work this week spans a proposed tagged-beam facility at CERN (nuSCOPE), a test of modified gravity using solar neutrino oscillations, and refined predictions for neutrino production in molecular clouds and in supernova outflows. Experiments and results

IceCube tests extra dimensions

IceCube analysed 10.7 years of upward-going muon neutrino data, from 0.5 to 100 TeV, looking for the spectral distortions that low-scale-gravity models proposed to solve the hierarchy problem would imprint as neutrinos cross the Earth. No such distortion was found, and the collaboration used the result to constrain large-extra-dimension scenarios, including the compactification scale.

IceCube Upgrade sensitivities

IceCube has found evidence that active galaxies are among the sources of high-energy astrophysical neutrinos and likely sites of hadronic particle acceleration. A new report examines the projected sensitivities of the planned IceCube Upgrade for continuing this hunt for neutrino sources.

Projected sensitivities of the IceCube Upgrade — Read it
Super-K proton decay limits

Using the full pure-water phase of Super-Kamiokande, a 401 kiloton-year exposure, the collaboration searched for proton decay to a charged lepton plus an invisible massless particle and saw no signal beyond the expected atmospheric neutrino background. The analysis set new lower limits on the proton partial lifetime of 1.72×10^33 years for decay to a positron and 0.61×10^33 years for decay to a muon, improving on prior limits by factors of 2 and 1.5 respectively.

KM3NeT source stacking search

Using 336 days of data from the ARCA component of KM3NeT, collected with 19-21 detection units and yielding 1544 track-like neutrino candidates, the collaboration carried out a stacking search for high-energy neutrino emission from a sample of 75 ultra-luminous infrared galaxies and two categories of blazars.

IceCube tidal disruption search

IceCube searched for neutrinos coincident with 89 tidal disruption events, selected from optical/UV and X-ray observations, using time-dependent likelihood methods applied both to individual sources and to the population as a whole.

Search for Neutrinos from Tidal Disruption Events with IceCube — arXiv
DUNE automated triggers

DUNE researchers are developing AI systems to help sort through the large volumes of data the experiment will collect underground, aiming to identify neutrino interactions, spot signs of a stellar explosion, and detect problems inside the detector.

Lake Vermilion detector

A new underwater neutrino detection experiment, the Lake Vermilion neutrino detector, led by Fermilab, is set to begin testing this autumn in Lake Superior and Lake Vermilion, continuing Minnesota's history of neutrino research.

IceCube funding renewed

The US National Science Foundation has renewed its cooperative agreement with the University of Wisconsin–Madison to operate and maintain the IceCube Neutrino Observatory, a five-year, $53 million agreement covering 2026 to 2031.

ARA diffuse UHE search

The Askaryan Radio Array at the South Pole has completed its first array-wide diffuse search for ultra-high-energy neutrinos, using data collected from January 2013 to December 2023 across its five autonomous in-ice radio stations, incorporating improved detector characterisation and simulation.

Theory highlights

recently published, with the links each record carries

  • The paper lays out nuSCOPE, a proposed short-baseline experiment at CERN building on the ENUBET and NuTAG monitored-beam concept, which tags and monitors neutrinos so the muon and electron neutrino flux is exceptionally well controlled and the neutrino energy can be reconstructed event by event. This targets the poor knowledge of GeV-scale neutrino cross sections, currently projected to be a leading source of uncertainty for next-generation oscillation experiments.

    nuSCOPE: A monitored and tagged neutrino beam — INSPIRE · DOI

  • The authors study how spacetime torsion in modified teleparallel gravity alters neutrino flavour oscillation and the matter-enhanced MSW resonance, then use solar neutrino data to place new bounds on the torsion-neutrino coupling. They find the resulting modification to the survival probability could account for observational discrepancies without invoking new particle physics.

    Testing modified teleparallel gravity by neutrino oscillations — INSPIRE · DOI

  • Using a Geant4 Monte Carlo simulation of protons, alpha particles and heavier cosmic-ray nuclei propagating through a molecular cloud, the authors compute, for the first time within this framework, the radial energy deposition and the differential flux of secondary particles, including neutrinos, from hadronic interactions. This gives a more complete multi-species prediction for gamma-ray and neutrino emission from cosmic-ray-irradiated molecular clouds.

    Hadronic cosmic-ray interactions in molecular clouds: Gamma-ray, neutrino, and secondary … — INSPIRE · DOI

  • The paper computes, in the second Born approximation, the emission of neutrino pairs by a variable classical electromagnetic field and derives, for the first time, an effective Lagrangian for the photon-neutrino interaction. It also identifies a class of fields for which this pair-emission effect vanishes, unlike its QED photon-emission analogue.

    Emission of pairs of neutrinos by a variable field in the second Born approximation … — INSPIRE · DOI

  • Working within a model-independent effective field theory, the authors study anomalous neutrino-two-photon couplings via the process μ⁻γ→μ⁻νν̄ at a future muon collider, parameterising the interaction with dimension-7 operators and simulating signal and background with MadGraph5_aMC@NLO and Pythia 8. This offers a projected sensitivity to neutrino electromagnetic properties at a facility that does not yet exist experimentally.

    Investigation of neutrino electromagnetic properties at muon antimuon collider — INSPIRE · DOI

  • Based on fallback core-collapse supernova simulations, the authors model neutrino emission from neutrino-dominated accretion flows and examine how progenitor mass, metallicity and explosion energy shape the resulting heavy-lepton neutrino spectra. They show that flavour conversion during propagation can significantly alter the predicted diffuse neutrino background from these flows and its detectability.
