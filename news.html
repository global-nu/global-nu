Experiments and results

Super-Kamiokande DSNB

A spectral fit of a 5003.0-day dataset gave an indication of electron antineutrinos from the diffuse supernova neutrino background. The best-fit flux was 3.6 +1.6/-1.5 cm^-2 s^-1 for E_nu > 13.3 MeV, a 2.6σ tension with the null hypothesis. The collaboration lists improved rejection of atmospheric-neutrino backgrounds, updated systematic uncertainties and a refined spectral fit as the key changes from earlier searches.

CONUS+ reactor CEvNS

CONUS+ reports the most precise measurement to date of reactor coherent elastic neutrino-nucleus scattering, using germanium detectors at the Leibstadt nuclear power plant. A blind likelihood analysis with 1296 kg·days of reactor-on exposure yielded 1555 ± 152 events at 10.2σ, within 0.6 standard deviations of the Standard Model expectation of 1701 ± 198. The result gives sin²θ_W = 0.225 ± 0.022 at a momentum transfer of about 10 MeV, consistent with the Standard Model. A companion paper describes the run 2 detector performance, background model and signal extraction.

Neutrino Emissivity in Δ -Direct-Urca Processes Inside Neutron Stars

The paper calculates the neutrino emissivity of Δ-direct-Urca processes in neutron-star interiors, which had not been explicitly evaluated before. These processes can accompany the nucleonic Urca processes that dominate neutron-star cooling, so the result bears on the thermal evolution of stars containing Δ isobars.

When can decoherence reveal a Majorana phase? A phase-covariance no-go theorem for two-flavor neutrino oscillations

The paper proves a no-go theorem: any completely positive trace-preserving evolution covariant under rotations of the mass-coherence plane is exactly blind to a Majorana phase in two-flavour oscillations. A rate anisotropy among the transverse decoherence rates can supply an environmental phase reference. It sets a clear condition for when decoherence could make a Majorana phase visible.

Thermal leptogenesis in the Babu-Nandi-Tavartkiladze model of neutrino mass

The paper studies neutrino mass and thermal leptogenesis in the Babu-Nandi-Tavartkiladze model, where neutrino masses arise from a dimension-7 operator at tree level and a dimension-5 operator at one loop. It accommodates sub-eV neutrino masses with new physics at the TeV scale, and it examines whether thermal leptogenesis works in a way distinct from canonical seesaw leptogenesis.

Paleodetectors for neutrino signals from diverse Galactic stellar collapses

The paper investigates whether nuclear recoil tracks in ancient minerals could record neutrinos from past Galactic core-collapse supernovae. It includes variations in neutron-star mass, failed supernovae that form black holes, and the nuclear equation of state, and it finds larger sensitivity for models with larger neutrino emission.
