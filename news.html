Experiments and results

IceCube Nobel Prize

The Royal Swedish Academy of Sciences awarded the 2026 Nobel Prize in Physics to Francis Halzen of the University of Wisconsin–Madison. IceCube describes him as its principal investigator. The citation is for decisive contributions to the IceCube Neutrino Observatory and the discovery of high-energy neutrinos of astrophysical origin.

IceCube decoherence search

IceCube searched for quantum-gravity-induced decoherence of flavour oscillations using 368,071 atmospheric neutrinos between 0.5 and 100 TeV. This is an order of magnitude higher in energy than any previous decoherence search. The analysis found no evidence for decoherence and sets the strongest constraints to date on Planck-scale decoherence.

Planck-Scale Constraints on Quantum Gravity-Induced Neutrino Decoherence with IceCube — arXiv
IceCube infrared flares

IceCube has posted a search for high-energy neutrinos from infrared flares. It is motivated by neutrino emission from the blazar TXS 0506+056 and the Seyfert galaxy NGC 1068, which point to accretion onto supermassive black holes as a possible origin of the diffuse flux. The motivation also cites external candidate sources identified in IceCube data, including AT2019dsg and AT2019fdr.

Search for High-Energy Neutrinos from Infrared Flares — arXiv
Galactic centre with HAWC

IceCube published a news item on using IceCube and HAWC data to investigate the centre of the Galaxy. The region contains dense gas and potential accelerators, including a supermassive black hole. Particles accelerated to petaelectronvolt energies are of particular interest.

KM3NeT sea operations

KM3NeT reports a period of intense marine operations, with two further steps for ARCA and ORCA. During a campaign by Alcatel Submarine Networks, Cable Termination Frame 3 (CTF3) was installed at the ARCA deep-sea site.

KM3NeT ESFRI Landmark

The KM3NeT Collaboration announced that it has been recognised as a Landmark in the ESFRI Roadmap 2026. ESFRI is the European Strategy Forum on Research Infrastructures, and its Roadmap identifies the most critical next-generation facilities.

Baikal-GVD point sources

Baikal-GVD is a cubic-kilometre-scale detector under construction in Lake Baikal and is now 70% complete. A search for point-like astrophysical sources with a five-year track-like dataset from 2019–2024 found no significant source. The authors computed upper limits for selected candidate objects and compared them with published IceCube, ANTARES and KM3NeT results.

Search for Point-Like Neutrino Sources with Baikal-GVD — INSPIRE · DOI
T2K near detector

The ND280 upgrade of the T2K near detector was completed in 2024 and has enhanced its tracking capabilities. A new event selection targets muon-neutrino charged-current interactions with single positively charged pion production in the new modules. The work is presented as a step towards reducing the leading systematic uncertainties of T2K, which stem from neutrino-nucleus interaction modelling.

Theory highlights

recently published, with the links each record carries

  • The paper computes two-neutrino double-beta decay rates without decoupling nuclear structure from the lepton kinematics. It presents deviations of the rates and electron spectra from closure, non-closure and Taylor-expansion approximations for 82Se and 136Xe. This matters to anyone interpreting double-beta spectra, because the approximations enter the standard calculations.

    Direct calculation of the two-neutrino double-beta decay rate including the full … — arXiv · INSPIRE · DOI

  • The authors compare nine periodogram algorithms on the 22-year Super-Kamiokande solar 8B neutrino dataset (1996–2018), using hierarchical temporal segmentation to separate astrophysical signals from detector systematics. They conclude that the Generalized Lomb-Scargle method gives the most statistically robust detections because it handles heteroscedastic uncertainties. It is a methodological reference for periodicity searches in solar neutrino data.

    Comparative periodogram analysis of 22 years of Super-Kamiokande solar ⁸B neutrino data … — arXiv · INSPIRE · DOI

  • The paper studies an ultralight vector boson of the gauged L_mu − L_tau symmetry that mixes kinetically with the photon and so adds a matter potential in the Sun, modifying MSW oscillations. It uses solar neutrino measurements from Borexino, SNO and Super-Kamiokande, and discusses prospects for JUNO and XLZD. It shows how solar data can probe very light new mediators.

    Constraining ultralight vector bosons via solar neutrino oscillations: The gauged L μ - L … — arXiv · INSPIRE · DOI

  • The authors study scalar-mediated neutrino self-interactions acting on the diffuse supernova neutrino background as it crosses the cosmic neutrino background. They work in a full three-flavour framework that keeps the complete PMNS structure. The aim is to find the spectral imprints such interactions would leave for next-generation detectors.

    Diffuse Supernova Neutrinos with Secret Neutrino Interactions — arXiv · INSPIRE · DOI

  • The paper examines neutrino mass and thermal leptogenesis in the Babu-Nandi-Tavartkiladze model, which has a scalar quadruplet and two vectorlike fermion triplets. Neutrino masses arise from a dimension-7 operator at tree level and a dimension-5 operator at one loop, allowing sub-eV masses with a TeV-scale new physics scale. It explores how leptogenesis works in this setting, which differs from canonical seesaw leptogenesis.

    Thermal leptogenesis in the Babu-Nandi-Tavartkiladze model of neutrino mass — arXiv · INSPIRE · DOI

  • The paper investigates whether neutrinos from past Galactic core-collapse supernovae could leave nuclear recoil tracks in ancient minerals (paleodetectors). It varies neutron-star masses, includes failed supernovae that form black holes, and considers the nuclear equation of state. Sensitivity is found to improve for models with larger neutrino emission.

    Paleodetectors for neutrino signals from diverse Galactic stellar collapses — arXiv · INSPIRE · DOI
