Experiments and results

SBND first results

The Short-Baseline Near Detector at Fermilab released its first neutrino-argon cross-section measurement. It covers muon neutrino charged-current interactions with a single proton and no pions in the final state, as functions of transverse kinematic imbalance variables. The analysis used a subset of Run 1 data corresponding to 8.8×10^19 protons-on-target and selected 12,804 signal candidates with a purity of 91%. The authors state that the cross sections discriminate strongly among event-generator predictions. Fermilab describes the result as improving neutrino interaction modelling in preparation for DUNE.

DUNE construction

Fermilab reports that construction of the Long-Baseline Neutrino Facility is entering its next phase, with critical components arriving and partner institutions contributing. These sensors detect the electrical signals produced when neutrinos interact in the liquid argon.

KM3NeT and gravitational waves

KM3NeT published a paper describing a search for joint observations of high-energy neutrinos and gravitational waves, using KM3NeT and ANTARES data from the O3 observing run. KM3-230213A review

A new review summarises the properties of the ultra-high-energy event KM3-230213A, detected by KM3NeT with an inferred parent-neutrino energy of O(10^17 eV). It discusses interpretations involving active galactic nuclei, transient sources and cosmogenic neutrinos, and the role of multi-messenger constraints. According to the abstract, no unambiguous electromagnetic counterpart has been found despite extensive follow-up observations.

TAMBO prototype

TAMBO aims to detect tau neutrinos from 1 PeV to 1,000 PeV, an energy range between the reach of optical-Cherenkov and radio-based experiments. It will use up to 5,000 particle detectors installed on the face of a steep hill in the Colca valley. A proceedings contribution presents the project and the TAMBITO prototype.

Project 8 atom source

Project 8 aims to measure the neutrino mass from tritium beta decay using cyclotron radiation emission spectroscopy, with a final sensitivity goal of 40 meV/c^2. A new paper reports a prototype electron cyclotron resonance dissociator for hydrogen, using a tunable 1 kW, 2.45 GHz RF generator and electromagnets to ignite a plasma. This is one stage of the planned atomic-tritium beamline.

CMS heavy neutral leptons

CMS posted two searches for heavy neutral leptons in 13 TeV proton-proton data. One targets a Majorana HNL in top quark decays with same-sign, same-flavour dileptons, using 138 fb^-1. The other targets long-lived HNLs from W decays, with one prompt muon and a displaced vertex of two muons reconstructed in the muon system.

When can decoherence reveal a Majorana phase? A phase-covariance no-go theorem for two-flavor neutrino oscillations

The paper proves a no-go theorem: any completely positive trace-preserving evolution covariant under rotations of the mass-coherence plane is blind to a Majorana phase in two-flavour oscillations. It also identifies how a rate anisotropy in the decoherence can act as an environmental phase reference. This clarifies when open-system effects could reveal a Majorana phase.

Thermal leptogenesis in the Babu-Nandi-Tavartkiladze model of neutrino mass

The paper studies thermal leptogenesis in the Babu-Nandi-Tavartkiladze model, where neutrino masses arise from a dimension-7 operator at tree level and a dimension-5 operator at one loop. The model accommodates sub-eV neutrino masses with new physics at the TeV scale, and the paper examines whether leptogenesis is viable in it.
