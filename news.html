Halzen Nobel Prize

The Royal Swedish Academy of Science announced on 6 October that the 2026 Nobel Prize in Physics goes to Francis Halzen, IceCube principal investigator, for decisive contributions to the IceCube Neutrino Observatory and the discovery of high-energy neutrinos of astrophysical origin. JUNO-TAO commissioning

The Taishan Antineutrino Observatory is a satellite of JUNO that measures the reactor antineutrino spectrum at a baseline of 43.2 m, using a 2.8-ton liquid-scintillator detector operated at -50 degrees and read out by 4,024 silicon photomultiplier tiles. Installation was completed in 2025 and physics data taking began on 10 February 2026. The JUNO Collaboration reports stable cryogenic operation and about 90% of the readout channels retained, based on commissioning data and the first physics runs.

NνDEx simulation framework

NνDEx is designed to search for neutrinoless double-beta decay of 82Se and is planned for deployment at the China Jinping Underground Laboratory. It uses a high-pressure time projection chamber filled with 82SeF6 gas and a Topmetal pixelated charge readout. A simulation and reconstruction framework based on the REST software toolkit has now been developed for it.

Direct calculation of the two-neutrino double-beta decay rate including the full lepton-energy dependence

The paper computes two-neutrino double-beta decay rates without decoupling nuclear structure from the lepton kinematics, and compares the resulting rates and electron spectra with closure, non-closure and Taylor-expansion approximations for 82Se and 136Xe. It matters to anyone interpreting these decays, since the approximations commonly used so far are the baseline against which the deviations are measured.

The authors extend the Standard Model with a gauged U(1) Lμ-Lτ symmetry whose ultralight vector boson, through kinetic mixing with the photon, generates an effective matter potential in the Sun and so modifies MSW flavour conversion. Thermal leptogenesis in the Babu-Nandi-Tavartkiladze model of neutrino mass

The paper studies thermal leptogenesis in a model where neutrino masses arise from a dimension-7 operator at tree level and a dimension-5 operator at one loop, allowing sub-eV masses with new physics at the TeV scale. Diffuse Supernova Neutrinos with Secret Neutrino Interactions

The work examines how scalar-mediated neutrino self-interactions during propagation through the cosmic neutrino background could leave spectral imprints on the diffuse supernova neutrino background. Paleodetectors for neutrino signals from diverse Galactic stellar collapses

The paper assesses whether nuclear recoil tracks in ancient minerals could record neutrinos from past Galactic core-collapse supernovae, including different neutron star masses, failed supernovae forming black holes, and the nuclear equation of state. The sensitivity is found to be enhanced for models with larger neutrino emission, which links paleodetector reach to remnant compactness.
