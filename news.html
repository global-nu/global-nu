Halzen Nobel Prize

The Royal Swedish Academy of Science announced on 6 October that the 2026 Nobel Prize in Physics goes to Francis Halzen, principal investigator of IceCube, for decisive contributions to the IceCube Neutrino Observatory and the discovery of high-energy neutrinos of astrophysical origin. Wisconsin Public Radio has since interviewed Halzen on the prize and on the importance of neutrino research. The Fermilab News item presents the interview under the theme of scientific collaboration.

KM3NeT ESFRI Landmark

On 16 October the KM3NeT Collaboration announced that it has been recognised as a Landmark in the ESFRI Roadmap 2026. ESFRI is the European Strategy Forum on Research Infrastructures, the strategic body for European policy on major scientific facilities. The collaboration also sent its congratulations to Halzen on the Nobel Prize.

Baikal-GVD point sources

Baikal-GVD is a cubic-kilometre-scale detector under construction in Lake Baikal and is now 70% complete, having taken data in partial configurations since 2016. A search for point-like astrophysical sources in its 2019-2024 track-like dataset found no significant source, so upper limits were computed for selected candidate objects. These are compared with published results from IceCube, ANTARES and KM3NeT.

Search for Point-Like Neutrino Sources with Baikal-GVD — INSPIRE · DOI
T2K near detector upgrade

The leading systematic uncertainties in T2K's oscillation analysis come from modelling neutrino-nucleus interactions, which are largely constrained by measurements at the near detector ND280. The upgrade of ND280 was completed in 2024, and a new selection targets muon neutrino charged-current interactions with single positively charged pion production in the new modules. The authors present it as a step towards reducing the dominant systematic uncertainties.

Theory highlights

recently published, with the links each record carries

  • The paper addresses the contribution of Δ-direct-Urca processes to neutrino emissivity in neutron stars, motivated by the possibility that massive neutron stars contain Δ isobars and hyperons. It matters to neutrino physicists because Urca processes are described as dominant cooling mechanisms in neutron-star thermal evolution.

    Neutrino Emissivity in Δ -Direct-Urca Processes Inside Neutron Stars — arXiv · INSPIRE · DOI

  • The authors study a gauged U(1) Lμ−Lτ model with an ultralight vector boson that, through kinetic mixing with the photon, produces an effective matter potential in the Sun and so modifies MSW flavour conversion. The analysis uses solar neutrino measurements from Borexino, SNO and Super-Kamiokande and considers prospects for JUNO and XLZD, making it a test of new physics with solar data.

    Constraining ultralight vector bosons via solar neutrino oscillations: The gauged L μ - L … — arXiv · INSPIRE · DOI

  • The paper examines thermal leptogenesis in the Babu-Nandi-Tavartkiladze model, where neutrino masses arise from a dimension-7 operator at tree level and a dimension-5 operator at one loop. The model gives sub-eV neutrino masses with a new-physics scale at the TeV level, and its leptogenesis is described as distinct from the canonical seesaw case.

    Thermal leptogenesis in the Babu-Nandi-Tavartkiladze model of neutrino mass — arXiv · INSPIRE · DOI

  • The authors compute the two-neutrino double-beta decay rate with the full interdependence of nuclear structure and lepton kinematics, rather than decoupling them as earlier calculations did. They compare rates and electron spectra with closure, non-closure and Taylor-expansion approximations for 82Se and 136Xe, which is relevant to anyone modelling the two-neutrino spectrum in double-beta experiments.

    Direct calculation of the two-neutrino double-beta decay rate including the full … — arXiv · INSPIRE · DOI

  • The paper studies how scalar-mediated neutrino self-interactions, acting as DSNB neutrinos cross the cosmic neutrino background, would leave spectral imprints on the Diffuse Supernova Neutrino Background. It works in a full three-flavour framework that keeps the complete PMNS structure and considers four flavour-diagonal coupling structures.

    Diffuse Supernova Neutrinos with Secret Neutrino Interactions — arXiv · INSPIRE · DOI

  • The authors investigate whether neutrinos from past Galactic core-collapse supernovae could be detected through nuclear-recoil tracks in ancient minerals (paleodetectors). They include varying neutron-star masses, failed supernovae forming black holes and the role of the nuclear equation of state, and find that sensitivity is enhanced for models with larger neutrino emission.

    Paleodetectors for neutrino signals from diverse Galactic stellar collapses — arXiv · INSPIRE · DOI
