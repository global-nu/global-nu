NOvA hydrogen cross section

The NOvA collaboration reported a measurement of the muon antineutrino charged-current quasi-elastic cross section on hydrogen using the near detector, based on a 1.2×10^21 proton-on-target exposure in the NuMI beam. The analysis selected 35,509 signal events in the hydrogen-rich (10.8%) detector, the highest-statistics sample of such interactions measured to date, with backgrounds from heavier nuclei constrained using dedicated control samples.

Theory highlights

recently published, with the links each record carries

  • Working within the normal mass hierarchy, this study examines how well DUNE and NOvA, separately and combined, could constrain a decay of the neutrino mass state nu3 into a sterile state. The authors find that the true value of the mixing angle theta23 strongly affects the achievable constraint, with the higher octant giving the tightest limits.

    Constraining invisible neutrino decay at NOνA and DUNE — INSPIRE · DOI

  • This paper models three-flavor neutrino oscillations as a qutrit coupled to a classical random-telegraph-noise environment acting as pure dephasing in the mass basis. It shows that the ratio of the noise's switching rate to the coupling strength determines whether flavor coherence decays monotonically or exhibits memory-induced revivals, and uses this to diagnose CP-divisibility of the evolution.

    Non-Markovian Dephasing and Flavor Coherence in a Three-Flavor Neutrino Qutrit — INSPIRE · DOI

  • This paper develops an open-quantum-systems formalism for how superfluid helium-4 responds to coherent elastic neutrino-atom scattering, a process not yet observed. It is timed to a planned experiment at the National Center for Physics and Mathematics in Sarov, which will use a high-intensity tritium neutrino source with a superfluid He-4 detector to test Standard Model and beyond-Standard-Model neutrino physics at very low energies.

    The Formalism of Open Quantum Systems for Superfluid Helium Response to Neutrino … — INSPIRE · DOI

  • Using effective Lagrangians, chiral perturbation theory, and a hadronic molecular model, this paper computes cross sections and Dalitz plots for antineutrino-proton scattering into hyperons, charmed baryons, and candidate hadronic molecular states. The authors argue that future high-statistics neutrino facilities could use such channels to probe these exotic baryonic states.

    Neutrino-proton scattering as a probe of exotic baryonic states: Hyperons, charmed … — INSPIRE · DOI

  • This Nature Astronomy piece describes TAMBO, the Tau Air-shower Mountain-Based Observatory, a mountain-based instrument intended to help map the high-energy neutrino sky, an area where physicists have so far struggled to build a complete picture, while also searching for signs of new physics.

    Measuring the high-energy neutrino sky using the deep-valley neutrino observatory TAMBO — INSPIRE · DOI

  • Using quantum kinetic simulations, this proceedings paper studies pairwise collective neutrino flavor conversion in core-collapse supernovae and binary neutron-star mergers, showing that the neutrino system evolves asymptotically toward quasi-steady states through turbulence-like cascades combined with collisions with the background matter. This bears on modelling explosive events that are also promising sources of gravitational waves.
