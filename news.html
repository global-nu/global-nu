DUNE construction

Fermilab, CERN and SURF crews have started building the first of two cryostats, the large insulated containers that will hold liquid argon for DUNE. KM3NeT/ARCA diffuse flux

KM3NeT announced a search for an all-sky and a Galactic Ridge diffuse neutrino emission using the first two years of ARCA data. The published analysis uses the first ARCA configurations, with 6, 8, 19 and 21 detection units and a total livetime of 640 days. For the all-sky fit under a single power law, the paper reports a spectral index of 3.00 with uncertainties of +0.30 and -0.35 at 68% credible level.

SBND cross sections

SBND has measured total and single-differential inclusive electron-neutrino charged-current cross sections on argon. The authors describe it as the highest-statistics inclusive measurement of this channel on argon to date and the first using the Booster Neutrino Beam. They stress that such direct measurements matter for long- and short-baseline oscillation studies based on electron-neutrino appearance.

SHiP sterile sensitivity

The SHiP Collaboration studies a facility approved for the beam-dump area at the CERN SPS North Area. It argues that the baseline detector configuration is a two-baseline experiment for sterile-neutrino-induced oscillations. Neutrino interactions are recorded in the scattering and neutrino detector and, about 70 m further downstream, in the calorimeter system.

JUNO preparation

JUNO requires ultrapure water with a radium concentration below 50 μBq/m3, because the liquid scintillator is in direct contact with the water during filling. A new paper describes a measurement system sensitive down to 3.9 μBq/m3 and an optimised 100 t/h water production process. A separate study considers a movable reactor antineutrino source placed within about 3 km of the JUNO detector, aimed at precision on the reactor mixing angle.

ProtoDUNE-SP muon seasons

The DUNE Collaboration reports a seasonal modulation of the cosmic-ray muon rate in the ProtoDUNE single-phase detector at the CERN Neutrino Platform. The data span December 2018 to July 2020. The modulation has a significance of 4σ against the null hypothesis, with the rate highest in winter, consistent with other surface experiments with a low muon-energy threshold.

IceCube neutral currents

A phenomenological study asks how well IceCube can constrain the neutral-current neutrino–nucleon cross section with 12 years of data. It considers two strategies: neutrino absorption and regeneration in the Earth, and the track-to-cascade event ratio. The authors note that, unlike the charged-current cross section, neutral-current interactions at high energy remain largely unexplored experimentally.

CYGNO directionality

The CYGNO collaboration studies the reconstruction of the initial direction of low-energy electron recoils in its 50 L LIME prototype, which uses an optical time-projection chamber with a sCMOS readout. Electron-recoil directionality could help separate solar-neutrino interactions from backgrounds. The algorithm infers the direction from the early part of the ionization trail, before multiple scattering erases the directional information.

MINERvA surrogate model

A new probabilistic surrogate of the MINERvA detector response is trained on the open simulation dataset. It maps generator-level final-state particles and interaction geometry to reconstructed observables. The aim is to let people outside the original collaboration compare interaction models with open neutrino data without computationally intensive methods.

Neutrino Emissivity in Δ -Direct-Urca Processes Inside Neutron Stars

The paper computes the contribution of Δ-direct-Urca processes to neutrino emissivity in neutron stars containing Δ isobars, which had not been treated explicitly before. Toward measuring the low-energy cosmic-ray spectrum with next-generation neutrino detectors

The authors examine whether kiloton-scale detectors such as Hyper-Kamiokande could constrain the primary cosmic-ray spectrum through the atmospheric neutrino flux. They find that future detectors can distinguish between cosmic-ray models with high statistical significance, even allowing for uncertainties in cross sections and hadronic interactions. This links atmospheric-neutrino measurements to a flux input that is usually taken from satellite and balloon data.

Motivated by updated IceCube data for NGC 1068 down to about 0.2 TeV, the paper calculates neutrino and cascade gamma-ray production from hadronic channels. When can decoherence reveal a Majorana phase? A phase-covariance no-go theorem for two-flavor neutrino oscillations

The paper proves that every completely positive trace-preserving evolution covariant under rotations of the mass-coherence plane is exactly blind to a Majorana phase in two-flavour oscillations. It also shows that a rate anisotropy can supply an environmental phase reference. This clarifies when decoherence could in principle reveal a Majorana phase.

Quasisteady flavor configuration of multienergy neutrino ensembles

The authors present semianalytic approximations for the quasisteady outcome of slow and fast flavour conversions in quasihomogeneous neutrino ensembles. They report excellent agreement with multiangle and multienergy solutions of the kinetic equations, independent of mass ordering. Such subgrid schemes are needed because supernova hydrodynamic simulations cannot include neutrino quantum kinetics.

Exploring anomaly-free dark photon models through solar neutrino-electron scattering in ton-scale dark matter detectors

The paper uses low-energy solar neutrino–electron scattering data from PandaX-4T and XENONnT to constrain anomaly-free U(1)_X dark photon models with kinetic and mass mixing. It derives exclusion limits for six benchmark models and finds that their stringency correlates with the leptonic charge assignments. It also gives model-independent limits for pure kinetic and pure mass mixing.
