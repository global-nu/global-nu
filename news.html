IceCube decoherence search

IceCube searched for quantum-gravity-induced decoherence of flavour oscillations using 368,071 atmospheric neutrinos between 0.5 and 100 TeV. This is an order of magnitude higher in energy than any previous decoherence search. The analysis found no evidence for decoherence and sets the strongest constraints to date on Planck-scale decoherence.

IceCube infrared flares

IceCube has posted a search for high-energy neutrinos from infrared flares. It is motivated by neutrino emission from the blazar TXS 0506+056 and the Seyfert galaxy NGC 1068, which point to accretion onto supermassive black holes as a possible origin of the diffuse flux. The motivation also cites external candidate sources identified in IceCube data, including AT2019dsg and AT2019fdr.

Baikal-GVD point sources

Baikal-GVD is a cubic-kilometre-scale detector under construction in Lake Baikal and is now 70% complete. A search for point-like astrophysical sources with a five-year track-like dataset from 2019–2024 found no significant source. The authors computed upper limits for selected candidate objects and compared them with published IceCube, ANTARES and KM3NeT results.

Direct calculation of the two-neutrino double-beta decay rate including the full lepton-energy dependence

The paper computes two-neutrino double-beta decay rates without decoupling nuclear structure from the lepton kinematics. It presents deviations of the rates and electron spectra from closure, non-closure and Taylor-expansion approximations for 82Se and 136Xe. This matters to anyone interpreting double-beta spectra, because the approximations enter the standard calculations.

Comparative periodogram analysis of 22 years of Super-Kamiokande solar ⁸B neutrino data: classical, phase-based, and information theoretic methods

The authors compare nine periodogram algorithms on the 22-year Super-Kamiokande solar 8B neutrino dataset (1996–2018), using hierarchical temporal segmentation to separate astrophysical signals from detector systematics. They conclude that the Generalized Lomb-Scargle method gives the most statistically robust detections because it handles heteroscedastic uncertainties. It is a methodological reference for periodicity searches in solar neutrino data.

The paper studies an ultralight vector boson of the gauged L_mu − L_tau symmetry that mixes kinetically with the photon and so adds a matter potential in the Sun, modifying MSW oscillations. The authors study scalar-mediated neutrino self-interactions acting on the diffuse supernova neutrino background as it crosses the cosmic neutrino background. Thermal leptogenesis in the Babu-Nandi-Tavartkiladze model of neutrino mass

The paper examines neutrino mass and thermal leptogenesis in the Babu-Nandi-Tavartkiladze model, which has a scalar quadruplet and two vectorlike fermion triplets. Neutrino masses arise from a dimension-7 operator at tree level and a dimension-5 operator at one loop, allowing sub-eV masses with a TeV-scale new physics scale. It explores how leptogenesis works in this setting, which differs from canonical seesaw leptogenesis.

Paleodetectors for neutrino signals from diverse Galactic stellar collapses

The paper investigates whether neutrinos from past Galactic core-collapse supernovae could leave nuclear recoil tracks in ancient minerals (paleodetectors). It varies neutron-star masses, includes failed supernovae that form black holes, and considers the nuclear equation of state. Sensitivity is found to improve for models with larger neutrino emission.
