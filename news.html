Experiments and results

DUNE prototype stress test

A prototype detector for the Deep Underground Neutrino Experiment, destined for installation deep underground in the US, has been put through an extreme 300,000-volt stress trial. The test is intended to establish how the new detector technology performs under such conditions before it goes underground.

IceCube Upgrade: Earth studies

A new study estimates the sensitivity of the IceCube Upgrade, deployed during the 2025-26 polar season, to probing the Earth's interior using atmospheric neutrino oscillations. The Upgrade's lower energy threshold and improved reconstruction for multi-GeV atmospheric neutrinos are expected to sharpen sensitivity to Earth matter effects and to key features of the Preliminary Reference Earth Model.

KM3NeT diffuse search

The collaboration searched for an all-sky and Galactic Ridge diffuse astrophysical neutrino flux using the first two years of KM3NeT/ARCA data, totalling 640 days of livetime across configurations of 6, 8, 19 and 21 active detection units. The Galactic Ridge fit did not yield constraints on the flux, while the all-sky analysis fitted a single-flavour flux under a single power-law assumption.

NOvA monopole search

The collaboration searched for a magnetic monopole component of the cosmic-ray flux using a 2,743-live-day exposure of the Far Detector, a 14-kilotonne liquid scintillator detector built primarily to observe GeV-scale electron neutrinos. No monopole events were found, and the detector's small overburden let the search cover a previously unexplored low-mass region.

Timing-Based Search for Magnetic Monopoles with the NOvA Detector on the Surface — arXiv · DOI
LEGEND: 76Ge nuclear input

A new measurement investigates the branching ratios of 76As beta-minus decay and electron capture, quantities relevant to the nuclear matrix element used to interpret LEGEND's search for neutrinoless double beta decay in 76Ge. Electron capture into the first excited state of 76Ge had been measured once before this work, while electron capture into the 76Ge ground state has still not been observed.

recently published, with preprint, record and journal

  • This paper studies invisible decay of the neutrino mass state ν3 into a sterile state under the normal mass ordering, using the combined sensitivity of the NOvA and DUNE long-baseline experiments to constrain the decay parameter. The authors find that the true value of θ23 significantly affects the achievable constraint, with the higher octant giving a notably stronger bound.

    Constraining invisible neutrino decay at NO$\nu $A and DUNE — INSPIRE · DOI

  • This paper models three-flavour neutrino oscillation as a qutrit coupled to a random telegraph noise environment that acts as pure dephasing in the mass basis, showing that the ratio of the noise's switching rate to its coupling strength determines whether flavour coherence decays smoothly or shows memory-induced revivals. The framework gives a concrete way to diagnose non-Markovian decoherence effects in oscillation data.

    Non-Markovian Dephasing and Flavor Coherence in a Three-Flavor Neutrino Qutrit — INSPIRE · DOI

  • This paper introduces TAMBO, a mountain-based observatory being developed to map the high-energy neutrino sky, an area where physicists have so far struggled to build a complete picture. It sets out the observatory's goal of resolving neutrino sources and searching for signs of new physics in the same dataset.

    Measuring the high-energy neutrino sky using the deep-valley neutrino observatory TAMBO — INSPIRE · DOI

  • This paper computes cross sections and kinematic distributions for antineutrino-proton scattering into channels containing hyperons, charmed baryons and hadronic molecular states, using effective Lagrangians, chiral perturbation theory and a hadronic molecular model. The authors argue that future high-statistics neutrino facilities could use these channels as a complementary way to search for exotic baryonic states.

    Neutrino-proton scattering as a probe of exotic baryonic states: Hyperons, charmed baryons — INSPIRE · DOI

  • This paper studies a mixed dark matter scenario in which WIMPs accrete onto primordial black holes to form ultracompact minihalos, boosting the WIMP annihilation rate, and derives the resulting extragalactic muon neutrino flux for several annihilation channels. It extends earlier work that focused on the associated gamma-ray signal, giving neutrino telescopes an additional handle on the same primordial black hole dark matter scenario.

  • This paper derives global limits on the Majorana neutrino mass by combining likelihoods from several neutrinoless double beta decay experiments with nuclear matrix elements computed from first-principles chiral effective field theory in the in-medium similarity renormalization group approach. In contrast to phenomenological nuclear models, the ab initio calculation indicates that the current generation of 0νββ experiments has likely not yet reached the sensitivity needed to probe the mass region of interest.
