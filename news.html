Experiments and results

KM3NeT ARCA

A sea campaign at the ARCA site over the past two weeks is reported as a significant step in construction. It enlarged the submarine infrastructure and the detector, and included an upgrade. The collaboration announced it under the title "Welcome, ARCA62!".

DUNE cryostat steel

Crews at the Sanford Underground Research Facility have moved about 39% of the I-beam steel needed to build the cryostat, nearly 2 million pounds. That steel completely fills the south cavern, which is 500 feet long and 65 feet wide. Separately, the collaboration met at Unicamp to discuss progress of the project.

IceCube source searches

The collaboration published summaries of several analyses in recent weeks. They include a search for the source of the 220 PeV KM3NeT event, a steady-state search with DeepCore that follows the NGC 1068 evidence, and a search for emission from tidal disruption events. Baikal-GVD point sources

The detector in Lake Baikal is under construction and is now 70% complete. A search for point-like sources with a 5-year track-like dataset from 2019-2024 found no significant source. Upper limits are given for selected candidate objects and compared with published IceCube, ANTARES and KM3NeT results.

Search for Point-Like Neutrino Sources with Baikal-GVD — INSPIRE · DOI
T2K near-detector upgrade

The upgrade of ND280 was completed in 2024, and its leading purpose is to constrain the neutrino-interaction modelling that dominates T2K's systematic uncertainties. Two recent contributions concern the new detector modules. One proposes a deep neural network to identify electrons, photons, muons, pions and protons in SuperFGD. The other presents an event selection for muon-neutrino charged-current interactions with a single positively charged pion.

Jinping 1-ton prototype

The 1-ton prototype for the Jinping neutrino experiment has completed a hardware upgrade at CJPL-I and has operated in both water and liquid-scintillator modes. The PMT count was raised from 30 to 60 with 8-inch MCP-PMTs. The paper reports muon detection and direction reconstruction from 85 days (Water-I) and 79 days (Water-II) of water-mode data.

Muon Detection and Direction Reconstruction with the Upgraded 1-ton Water Cherenkov … — arXiv
EXO-200 public data

A subset of EXO-200 calibration data has been released publicly for AI/ML applications. It comprises about one million events from 228Th calibration campaigns between 26 February and 2 March 2018, near the end of Phase-II. Each event has raw U-wire, V-wire and APD waveforms plus reconstructed quantities, distributed in HDF5.

Theory highlights

recently published, with the links each record carries

  • The paper presents what it describes as the first combined oscillation analysis of recent atmospheric datasets from Super-Kamiokande, IceCube-DeepCore and KM3NeT/ORCA, together with Daya Bay reactor data. A single physics model describes all datasets with no significant parameter tensions, fitting 839 048 events across 1536 bins with 91 parameters. The combination prefers the normal over the inverted mass ordering at 3σ, which is relevant to anyone following the ordering question.

    Atmospheric neutrino oscillations: The full picture — arXiv · INSPIRE · DOI

  • The authors combine current neutrinoless double-beta decay results with ab initio nuclear matrix elements from chiral effective field theory in a Bayesian framework to derive global Majorana mass limits. Unlike results from nuclear models, the ab initio results indicate that the current generation of experiments has likely not yet reached the required sensitivity. The choice of matrix elements therefore affects how far current searches reach.

    Global ab initio neutrino mass limits from neutrinoless double-beta decay — arXiv · INSPIRE · DOI

  • The paper studies whether neutrinos from past Galactic core-collapse supernovae could be detected as nuclear recoil tracks in ancient minerals (paleodetectors). It varies neutron star masses, includes failed supernovae that form black holes, and considers the nuclear equation of state. Sensitivity is enhanced for models with larger neutrino emission.

    Paleodetectors for neutrino signals from diverse Galactic stellar collapses — arXiv · INSPIRE · DOI

  • The paper considers magnetic and oscillation effects on neutrinos emitted from a protoneutron star in a supernova. The hardness of the electron antineutrino spectrum increases significantly through oscillations in the outer layers of the star. The enhancement is most pronounced for the inverted mass ordering and is favourable for ground-based detection.

    Magnetorotational Supernova Neutrino Spectra Versus Mass Ordering — INSPIRE · DOI

  • The paper studies invisible decay of ν3 into a sterile state, assuming normal ordering, and asks how well NOvA and DUNE can constrain the decay parameter. The constraint depends significantly on the true value of θ23. This matters for assessing how much of the decay bound comes from the octant of θ23.

    Constraining invisible neutrino decay at NOνA and DUNE — INSPIRE · DOI

  • The authors build composite neutrino models in which heavy neutrinos are bound states of a near-conformal strongly coupled sector, mixing with Standard Model neutrinos through an inverse seesaw. Matching onto the low-energy theory gives transition dipole couplings of order 10^-6 to 10^-8 GeV^-1, parametrically larger than loop-level predictions of minimal Dirac or Majorana models. They carry out an event-level simulation of the production-and-decay signature.

    Neutrino dipole moments and radiative signatures from partial compositeness — arXiv · INSPIRE · DOI
