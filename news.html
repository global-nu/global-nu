Experiments and results

DUNE data-analysis AI

Researchers are developing AI systems to sort through the large data volumes DUNE will produce, aiming to identify neutrino interactions, flag possible signs of a stellar explosion, and spot problems inside the detector. The work draws on decades of machine-learning experience at Fermilab.

DUNE high-voltage test

A prototype detector was subjected to an extreme 300,000-volt stress trial as part of testing ahead of installation deep underground in the US.

300,000 volts: US team puts world’s largest neutrino detector through extreme stress test — Read it
IceCube and Earth's interior

Atmospheric neutrinos, produced in cosmic-ray-induced air showers, are being used by IceCube to probe the structure of the Earth's interior, complementing indirect methods such as seismic and gravitational measurements.

Probing the Earth’s interior using atmospheric neutrinos — Read it
IceCube WavePID tool

A new tool called WavePID identifies neutrino interactions in the ice from the shape of the light pattern produced when secondary particles emit light, working at the nanosecond level.

WavePID: A tool that identifies neutrinos at the nanosecond level — Read it
DUNE outreach in Lead

SURF and partner organisations, including Fermilab, hosted the 18th annual Neutrino Day science festival in Lead, South Dakota, with an interactive livestream from underground and an Ask a Scientist table. SDPB's live broadcast from a mile underground featured SURF laboratory director Mike Headley and DUNE senior scientist Sowjanya Gollapinni.

JUNO calibration backgrounds

A new study characterises the radioactive background contributed by JUNO's calibration system, whose components sit close to the detector's target volume. Based on extensive material screening, the work supports the requirement that the total singles background rate from radioactivity stay below 10 Hz in the 0.7-12 MeV range within the fiducial volume.

The Radioactive Background of the JUNO Calibration System — arXiv
NINJA water-target measurement

The NINJA emulsion detector, exposed to the J-PARC muon-neutrino beam, reported reconstructed kinematics of muons, protons and charged pions from charged-current interactions on water. The analysis used an exposure of 4.63×10^20 protons on target and selected 82 events from a 3.9 kg fiducial water volume, comparing the distributions with Monte Carlo predictions.

Measurement of reconstructed final-state kinematics in charged-current interactions on … — arXiv

Theory highlights

recently published, with the links each record carries

  • TAMBO, a proposed mountain-based neutrino observatory, is presented as a route to filling gaps in the mapping of the high-energy neutrino sky that have persisted despite a decade of progress in neutrino astronomy. The authors argue it could also help uncover signs of new physics along the way.

    Measuring the high-energy neutrino sky using the deep-valley neutrino observatory TAMBO — INSPIRE · DOI

  • Combining results from multiple neutrinoless double-beta decay searches with nuclear matrix elements computed ab initio via the in-medium similarity renormalization group, the authors derive global Bayesian limits on the Majorana neutrino mass. In contrast to limits based on conventional nuclear models, their ab initio results indicate that the current generation of experiments has likely not yet reached the sensitivity needed to probe the required mass region.

    Global ab initio neutrino mass limits from neutrinoless double-beta decay — arXiv · INSPIRE · DOI

  • The authors propose a minimal dark SU(2) gauge symmetry under which one right-handed neutrino-like fermion is charged, forbidding its Standard Model Yukawa coupling and so enforcing one exactly massless neutrino, consistent with oscillation data. Anomaly cancellation and a discrete symmetry fix the minimal dark-sector completion needed to make the construction consistent.

    Minimal dark S U ( 2 ) origin of a massless Dirac neutrino — arXiv · INSPIRE · DOI

  • Using the GENIE event generator, the authors benchmark a range of theoretical and empirical models of pionless neutrino-argon scattering against recent MicroBooNE charged-current measurements. The comparison finds strong separation between competing models in some cases, giving experiments on argon targets clearer grounds for choosing between them.

    Benchmarking state-of-the-art theory and empirical models of pionless neutrino-argon … — arXiv · INSPIRE · DOI

  • Treating the Peccei-Quinn symmetry as a flavour symmetry, the authors build a minimal axion model combining the KSVZ framework with a type-I seesaw, using flavour-dependent PQ charges and two extra singlet scalars. The resulting texture in the inverse Majorana mass matrix predicts an inverted neutrino mass ordering with the ratio m1/m3 falling between about 17 and 45.

    Neutrino mass hierarchy and axion–lepton couplings in a minimal flavored νKSVZ model — INSPIRE · DOI

  • The authors derive an analytical expression for the maximum neutrino flux a blazar can produce within the one-zone leptohadronic jet model, given its observed X-ray flux, addressing the tension between that model and stringent X-ray constraints. Applying the approach to a sample of neutrino candidate blazars, they find agreement between the analytical estimate and full numerical modelling.

    On the Maximum Neutrino Flux of Blazars in the One-zone Leptohadronic Model — arXiv · INSPIRE · DOI
