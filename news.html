Halzen Nobel Prize

The Royal Swedish Academy of Sciences has awarded the 2026 Nobel Prize in Physics to Francis Halzen of the University of Wisconsin–Madison. IceCube reports that the citation is for decisive contributions to the IceCube Neutrino Observatory and the discovery of high-energy neutrinos of astrophysical origin. Halzen is IceCube's principal investigator, and Physics World describes the award as being for his work detecting cosmic neutrinos.

IceCube and HAWC

IceCube and HAWC data have been used to investigate the centre of the Galaxy. The region is of interest for how cosmic rays are accelerated to extremely high energies, in particular to PeV energies. It contains dense gas and potential accelerators, including a supermassive black hole.

PandaX-4T DSNB search

PandaX-4T reported the first search for the diffuse supernova neutrino background using coherent elastic neutrino-nucleus scattering. With an exposure of 1.54 tonne·year from the commissioning and first science runs, it observed no significant event excess over the expected background. It set an upper limit of (2.27–2.43) × 10^3 cm^-2 s^-1 per flavour at 90% confidence level on the ν_x flux, for incident neutrino energies above 19.3 MeV.

JUNO mass splittings

A preprint by I. Alikhanov argues that an empirical relation between the two mass-squared differences is strongly supported by JUNO's reported measurements of Δm²21 and Δm²31. The relation, a ratio of sums and differences of their square roots equal to √2, is found to give 1.4143 (+0.0036, −0.0038) from the data, a relative precision of 0.27%. This is an author's interpretation of JUNO results, not a JUNO Collaboration statement.

Theory highlights

recently published, with the links each record carries

  • The review surveys neutrino astronomy at high energies, from the detection of a diffuse flux of extraterrestrial neutrinos at TeV and PeV energies to the first identified individual sources. It describes the route to the present, the current status of the field and future developments. It is a useful single-source orientation for a physicist from a neighbouring subfield, and it is timely given this week's Nobel Prize.

    Neutrino Astronomy at High Energies-An Experimental Review — arXiv · INSPIRE · DOI

  • This review summarises the properties of KM3-230213A, the ultra-high-energy neutrino event detected by KM3NeT, and discusses interpretations including active galactic nuclei, transient sources and cosmogenic neutrinos. It stresses multimessenger constraints, and notes that despite extensive follow-up no unambiguous electromagnetic counterpart has been found. It is a compact entry point to the debate over the origin of the highest-energy neutrino yet seen.

    KM3-230213A: The Highest-Energy Cosmic Neutrino — INSPIRE · DOI

  • The paper presents a direct calculation of two-neutrino double-beta decay rates that keeps the full interdependence between nuclear structure and lepton kinematics, without closure, non-closure or Taylor-expansion approximations. It compares the resulting rates and electron spectra with those methods for 82Se and 136Xe. This matters for double-beta experiments because the electron spectrum shape of the two-neutrino mode is the background to neutrinoless searches.

    Direct calculation of the two-neutrino double-beta decay rate including the full … — arXiv · INSPIRE · DOI

  • The paper uses a Monte Carlo simulation to study neutrino electromagnetic properties, searching for anomalous neutrino-two-photon interactions through dimension-7 effective operators at a future muon collider. It uses the process μ−γ → μ−νν̄ with a Weizsäcker–Williams equivalent photon. It is of interest as a collider route to neutrino electromagnetic couplings.

    Investigation of neutrino electromagnetic properties at muon antimuon collider — INSPIRE · DOI

  • The authors study the paleodetector detectability of neutrinos from past Galactic core-collapse supernovae, via nuclear recoil tracks in ancient minerals. They vary neutron-star masses, include failed supernovae forming black holes, and consider the nuclear equation of state. The sensitivity is found to be enhanced for models with larger neutrino emission, which is relevant for anyone considering geological supernova-neutrino records.

    Paleodetectors for neutrino signals from diverse Galactic stellar collapses — arXiv · INSPIRE · DOI

  • The paper examines thermal leptogenesis in the Babu-Nandi-Tavartkiladze model, where neutrino masses arise from a dimension-7 operator at tree level and a dimension-5 operator at one loop. The model accommodates sub-eV neutrino masses with new physics at the TeV scale. The authors note that leptogenesis here differs from the canonical seesaw case, which makes the model a target for experimental searches.

    Thermal leptogenesis in the Babu-Nandi-Tavartkiladze model of neutrino mass — arXiv · INSPIRE · DOI
