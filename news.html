DUNE cryostat construction

Crews from Fermilab, CERN and SURF have begun building the first of two large cryostats, insulated containers that will hold liquid argon at very low temperature. The cryostats will house approximately 17,000 tons of liquid argon. Work nearly a mile underground in South Dakota currently consists of assembling large steel beams into the outer structure of the detector modules.

Neutrino Emissivity in Δ -Direct-Urca Processes Inside Neutron Stars

The paper studies neutrino emissivity from Δ-direct-Urca processes in neutron stars that contain Δ isobars. These processes are a possible additional channel alongside the nucleonic and hyperonic Urca processes, which the abstract describes as dominant cooling mechanisms. It is relevant to anyone using neutron-star thermal evolution as a probe of dense matter.

Toward measuring the low-energy cosmic-ray spectrum with next-generation neutrino detectors

The authors assess whether kiloton-scale detectors such as Hyper-Kamiokande could constrain the primary cosmic-ray spectrum through the atmospheric neutrino flux. They report that future detectors can differentiate between cosmic-ray models with high statistical significance, even allowing for uncertainties in neutrino cross sections and hadronic interactions. This would give full-sky, long-term monitoring that satellites and balloons do not provide.

Quasisteady flavor configuration of multienergy neutrino ensembles

The paper offers semianalytic approximations for the quasisteady outcome of slow and fast flavour conversion in neutrino-dense environments such as core-collapse supernovae. The ansatz agrees well with multiangle and multienergy solutions of the kinetic equations, independent of mass ordering. Such a scheme is useful because hydrodynamic simulations cannot include neutrino quantum kinetics directly.

When can decoherence reveal a Majorana phase? A phase-covariance no-go theorem for two-flavor neutrino oscillations

The author proves a no-go theorem: for open two-flavour neutrino oscillations, any completely positive trace-preserving evolution covariant under rotations of the mass-coherence plane is exactly blind to a Majorana phase. A rate anisotropy can supply an environmental phase reference, which gives a specific condition under which decoherence could reveal the phase.

The paper uses a gauged Lμ−Lτ extension of the Standard Model with an ultralight vector boson that modifies solar-neutrino MSW conversion through kinetic mixing with the photon. Multipolar neutrino radiation in binary neutron star mergers: Angular structure, rotational variability, and implications for electron fraction

The authors study the angular structure and time variability of neutrino emission from binary neutron star mergers using fully general-relativistic simulations with energy-integrated M1 transport. They decompose the neutrino energy flux into spherical harmonics to quantify its multipolar content. The abstract also lists implications for the electron fraction, which matters for modelling merger ejecta.
