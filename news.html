DUNE far-site construction

Fermilab, CERN and SURF teams have begun construction of the cryostats that will house approximately 17,000 tons of liquid argon. Crews are currently assembling large steel beams to form the outer structure of the detector modules, nearly a mile underground in South Dakota. An earlier Fermilab report described the Long-Baseline Neutrino Facility as entering its next phase, with critical components arriving.

DUNE collaboration and software

Black Hills State University became an institutional member of DUNE, which Fermilab describes as more than 1,500 collaborators from 228 institutions in 38 countries and CERN. SLAC scientists in the collaboration work on detector technologies and machine-learning frameworks for processing the data. DUNE near-detector sterile sensitivity

A DUNE Collaboration study uses simulated near-detector events to estimate sensitivity to an eV-scale sterile neutrino in the 3+1 framework. It uses three samples: highly elastic muon-neutrino charged-current, highly elastic electron-neutrino charged-current, and neutrino-electron elastic scattering. These highly exclusive samples are chosen to reduce neutrino-interaction and flux uncertainties. The result is a simulation-based sensitivity, not a measurement.

IceCube Galactic plane

IceCube presented the first measurement of how the high-energy neutrino flux varies along the Galactic plane, using 10 years of particle-shower data. Normalisation and spectral index were fitted in several regions, independently of model expectations. The spectral indices were 2.76 (+0.18, −0.15) in the inner region and 3.13 (+0.57, −0.37) in the outer region. Separately, more than 230 participants attended the collaboration's fall meeting in Madison.

Askaryan Radio Array

ARA reported the first array-wide search for diffuse ultrahigh-energy neutrinos, using all five stations. The search found no events on an expected background of 0.13 ± 0.01 (stat.) +0.05/−0.06 (syst.) events. The resulting upper limit is the most stringent from 50 EeV to 1000 EeV, and the paper provides the first constraints on the flux reported by KM3NeT. A companion paper describes the analysis methodology for 10.6 years of livetime.

Auger sterile-neutrino search

The Pierre Auger Collaboration investigated its sensitivity to sterile neutrinos with masses from MeV to 100 MeV, mixing mainly with the tau flavour. Such particles could cross long chords of the Earth, interact through their active component and produce tau leptons that decay in the atmosphere and generate air showers. The paper computes exposures with a Monte Carlo simulation of neutrino propagation.

NINJA scintillator tracker

A meter-scale scintillator tracker, FROST, was built and operated in the NINJA experiment to match muon tracks between the nuclear emulsion detectors and the downstream muon detector. During J-PARC neutrino-beam operation it reached a data acquisition efficiency of 99.59% and a detection efficiency of 99.946 ± 0.006% for selected neutrino-induced muons well within the active area.

Neutrino Emissivity in Δ -Direct-Urca Processes Inside Neutron Stars

The paper studies the contribution of Δ-direct-Urca processes to neutrino emissivity in neutron-star matter containing Δ isobars. These processes join the nucleonic and hyperonic Urca processes as candidate cooling mechanisms. The result matters for anyone using neutrino cooling to interpret neutron-star thermal evolution.

Exploring anomaly-free dark photon models through solar neutrino-electron scattering in ton-scale dark matter detectors

The authors use solar neutrino-electron scattering data from PandaX-4T and XENONnT to constrain anomaly-free U(1)_X dark photon models with kinetic and mass mixing. They derive exclusion limits for six benchmark models and find that their stringency correlates with the leptonic charge assignments. They also give model-independent limits for pure kinetic and pure mass mixing, which shows what dark matter detectors can add to neutrino-physics bounds.

Constraining ultralight vector bosons via solar neutrino oscillations: The gauged L μ - L τ model and prospects for JUNO and XLZD

The paper treats a gauged L_μ−L_τ model with an ultralight vector boson that modifies solar neutrino oscillations through an effective matter potential in the Sun. Quasisteady flavor configuration of multienergy neutrino ensembles

The authors give semianalytic approximations for the quasisteady outcome of slow and fast flavour conversion in quasihomogeneous neutrino ensembles. They report excellent agreement with multiangle, multienergy solutions of the kinetic equations, independent of the mass ordering. Such approximations are meant to serve as subgrid schemes for supernova simulations, which cannot include neutrino quantum kinetics directly.

When can decoherence reveal a Majorana phase? A phase-covariance no-go theorem for two-flavor neutrino oscillations

The paper proves a no-go theorem: any completely positive trace-preserving evolution covariant under rotations of the mass-coherence plane cannot reveal a Majorana phase in two-flavour oscillations. A rate anisotropy in the environment can supply a phase reference. This delimits when decoherence could make the Majorana nature of neutrinos observable in oscillations.

Comparative periodogram analysis of 22 years of Super-Kamiokande solar ⁸B neutrino data: classical, phase-based, and information theoretic methods

The authors apply nine periodogram algorithms to the 22-year Super-Kamiokande solar 8B neutrino dataset (1996–2018), with hierarchical temporal segmentation to separate astrophysical signals from detector systematics. They conclude that the Generalized Lomb-Scargle method gives the most statistically robust detections because it handles heteroscedastic uncertainties correctly. The paper is relevant to anyone looking for periodic modulations in solar neutrino rates.
