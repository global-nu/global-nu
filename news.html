Experiments and results

JUNO oscillation update

The analysis uses 207.2 live days and 8,294 inverse beta-decay candidates at an average reactor baseline of 52.5 km. It reaches a precision of approximately 1% on the atmospheric mass splitting, which the collaboration describes as world-leading. The fit gave Δm²31 = +2.509 (+0.027, −0.025) ×10⁻³ eV² for normal ordering and Δm²31 = −2.482 (+0.025, −0.026) ×10⁻³ eV² for inverted ordering, using the Daya Bay constraint. The title claims an indication of the mass ordering, but the record excerpt does not give its strength.

Super-Kamiokande reactor antineutrinos

Super-Kamiokande entered the SK-Gd phase in 2020, which improves its sensitivity to low-energy electron antineutrinos through neutron capture on gadolinium. Using 411.52 live days, the collaboration found prompt spectra and monthly rates consistent with the oscillated reactor prediction after accounting for accidental, geoneutrino and spallation backgrounds. The no-reactor hypothesis is disfavoured with a signal significance of approximately 6σ, which the paper presents as the first observation of reactor antineutrinos at the detector.

DUNE cryostat steel

Crews at the Sanford Underground Research Facility have moved about 39% of the I-beam steel needed to build the cryostat, nearly 2 million pounds. The steel fills the south cavern, which is 500 feet long and 65 feet wide. Separately, researchers met at Unicamp for the DUNE Collaboration Meeting to discuss progress on the project, which Fermilab leads.

MicroBooNE proton kinematics

The collaboration presented the first double-differential cross sections for muon-neutrino charged-current interactions on argon with no mesons and one or more protons. The measurement uses the full Booster Neutrino Beam dataset, 1.30 × 10²¹ protons on target. Cross sections are given as functions of proton multiplicity, the momenta and muon-proton opening angles of the leading and subleading protons up to the fourth-leading proton, and the total available kinetic energy.

Baikal-GVD point sources

The detector has taken data in partial configurations since 2016 and is now 70% complete. A point-source search on a five-year track-like dataset from 2019 to 2024 found no significant neutrino source. Upper limits were computed for a selection of candidate objects and compared with published results from IceCube, ANTARES and KM3NeT.

Search for Point-Like Neutrino Sources with Baikal-GVD — INSPIRE · DOI
T2K ND280 upgrade

The ND280 upgrade was completed in 2024 and has enhanced the near detector's tracking capabilities. Two recent papers address its analysis tools: a neural network to identify electrons, photons, muons, pions and protons in the SuperFGD target, and an event selection for muon-neutrino charged-current interactions with a single positively charged pion. Both aim at tighter constraints on neutrino-interaction modelling, which dominates the leading systematic uncertainties of T2K's oscillation analysis.

Jinping 1-ton prototype

The prototype for the Jinping neutrino experiment has completed a hardware upgrade at CJPL-I, which lies under 2400 m of rock. The mechanical support and PMT layout were redesigned, and the PMT count rose from 30 to 60 with 8-inch MCP-PMTs. The detector was run in water mode for 85 days and 79 days in the two periods reported, and the paper derives muon detection efficiency and direction reconstruction from these data.

Theory highlights

recently published, with the links each record carries

  • Combines atmospheric neutrino data from Super-Kamiokande, IceCube-DeepCore and KM3NeT/ORCA with Daya Bay reactor data in a single oscillation fit. The fit covers 839 048 events in 1536 bins with 91 parameters and shows no significant parameter tensions. It prefers the normal over the inverted mass ordering at 3σ, and it shows that such combinations are feasible outside the experimental collaborations.

    Atmospheric neutrino oscillations: The full picture — arXiv · INSPIRE · DOI

  • Derives global Majorana neutrino mass limits by combining neutrinoless double-beta decay results with ab initio nuclear matrix elements from chiral effective field theory. In contrast to nuclear models, the ab initio results indicate that the current generation of experiments has likely not yet reached the sensitivity needed to probe the mass region the paper targets. The nuclear-structure input therefore matters when judging how close current searches are.

    Global ab initio neutrino mass limits from neutrinoless double-beta decay — arXiv · INSPIRE · DOI

  • Calculates two-neutrino double-beta decay rates with the full interdependence of nuclear structure and lepton kinematics, avoiding the approximations that decouple them. It presents deviations of rates and electron spectra from closure, non-closure and Taylor-expansion methods for 82Se and 136Xe. This is relevant to anyone modelling the two-neutrino spectrum as a background to neutrinoless searches.

    Direct calculation of the two-neutrino double-beta decay rate including the full … — arXiv · INSPIRE · DOI

  • Studies whether nuclear recoil tracks in ancient minerals could record neutrinos from past Galactic core-collapse supernovae. It varies neutron-star masses, includes failed supernovae that form black holes, and considers the nuclear equation of state. The sensitivity enhancement is quantified for models with larger neutrino emission, which links a geological detection idea to uncertainties in collapse outcomes.

    Paleodetectors for neutrino signals from diverse Galactic stellar collapses — arXiv · INSPIRE · DOI

  • Examines composite neutrino models in which heavy neutrinos are bound states of a near-conformal strongly coupled sector, mixing with Standard Model neutrinos through an inverse seesaw. The resulting transition dipole couplings, of order 10⁻⁶ to 10⁻⁸ GeV⁻¹, are parametrically larger than loop-level predictions in minimal Dirac or Majorana models. The authors carry out an event-level simulation of production and decay signatures, which connects neutrino mass models to searches for dipole-mediated signals.

    Neutrino dipole moments and radiative signatures from partial compositeness — arXiv · INSPIRE · DOI

  • Investigates neutrino mass and thermal leptogenesis in the Babu-Nandi-Tavartkiladze model, where a scalar quadruplet and vectorlike fermion triplets generate masses through a dimension-7 operator at tree level and a dimension-5 operator at one loop. Sub-eV neutrino masses are accommodated with a new-physics scale of order TeV. The paper asks whether thermal leptogenesis remains viable, since this differs from canonical seesaw leptogenesis.
