DUNE construction

Construction of the Long-Baseline Neutrino Facility is entering its next phase, with critical components arriving and partner institutions contributing. In the UK, half of the 137 Anode Plane Assemblies, the sensors that detect the electrical signals from neutrino interactions in liquid argon, have been completed. Black Hills State University has also joined the collaboration, which now counts more than 1,500 collaborators from 228 institutions in 38 countries and CERN.

Project 8 dissociator

Project 8 aims to measure the neutrino mass through cyclotron radiation emission spectroscopy of tritium beta decay, with a stated sensitivity goal of 40 meV/c^2. A new paper describes a prototype electron cyclotron resonance dissociator for hydrogen, using a tunable 1 kW 2.45 GHz RF generator and electromagnets to ignite a plasma.

Auger sterile neutrinos

The Pierre Auger Collaboration investigated the observatory's sensitivity to sterile neutrinos with masses between MeV and 100 MeV, in a 3+1 framework with mixing mainly to the tau flavour. Such neutrinos could cross long chords of the Earth and produce tau leptons that decay in the atmosphere, giving detectable air showers. Exposures are computed with a Monte Carlo simulation of propagation.

CMS heavy neutral leptons

CMS reported a search for a Majorana heavy neutral lepton produced in top quark decays, using 138 fb^-1 of proton-proton data at 13 TeV collected in 2016-2018. The final state has same-sign, same-flavour dileptons, as expected for lepton-number-violating decays in a type-I seesaw model.

NINJA FROST tracker

A meter-scale scintillator tracker, FROST, was built and operated in the NINJA experiment to match muon tracks between the nuclear emulsion detectors and the downstream muon detector. During J-PARC neutrino-beam operation it reached a data acquisition efficiency of 99.59% and a detection efficiency of 99.946±0.006% for selected neutrino-induced muons well within the active area.

Thermal leptogenesis in the Babu-Nandi-Tavartkiladze model of neutrino mass

The paper studies thermal leptogenesis in the Babu-Nandi-Tavartkiladze model, where neutrino masses arise from a dimension-7 operator at tree level and a dimension-5 operator at one loop. The model gives sub-eV neutrino masses with new physics at the TeV scale, and leptogenesis differs from the canonical seesaw case.
