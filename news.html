Experiments and results

NOvA hydrogen cross-section

The collaboration reported the total cross section for muon antineutrino charged-current quasielastic scattering on hydrogen, measured in the near detector using a 1.2×10^21 protons-on-target exposure in the NuMI beam. The analysis selected 35,509 signal events, the highest-statistics sample of (anti)neutrino-hydrogen interactions collected to date, with backgrounds from heavier nuclei constrained using dedicated control samples.

DUNE AI triggers

Fermilab reported that researchers are developing AI systems to sort through the huge volumes of data DUNE will collect, aiming to identify neutrino interactions, spot signs of a stellar explosion, and detect problems inside the detector. The effort draws on decades of machine-learning work already under way at the laboratory.

WavePID particle ID

A new tool called WavePID identifies neutrino interactions in IceCube by analysing the shape of the light pattern they produce at the nanosecond level, exploiting the blue light emitted by secondary particles when a neutrino interacts with the ice.

Atmospheric neutrinos, produced in cosmic-ray-induced air showers and detected by IceCube, are being used to probe the structure of the Earth's interior, complementing indirect methods such as seismic and gravitational measurements.

Theory highlights

recently published, with the links each record carries

  • A mountain-based neutrino observatory called TAMBO is proposed to help map the high-energy neutrino sky, an area where the field has struggled to build a complete picture despite a decade of progress in neutrino astronomy. The paper, published in Nature Astronomy, describes how the deep-valley design aims to detect tau neutrino air showers and search for clues of new physics.

    Measuring the high-energy neutrino sky using the deep-valley neutrino observatory TAMBO — INSPIRE · DOI

  • Combining likelihood functions from a suite of neutrinoless double-beta decay experiments with nuclear matrix elements computed ab initio in the in-medium similarity renormalization group approach, this paper derives global Bayesian limits on the Majorana neutrino mass. In contrast to other nuclear models, the ab initio results indicate that current-generation 0νββ experiments have likely not yet reached the sensitivity needed to probe the relevant mass region.

    Global ab initio neutrino mass limits from neutrinoless double-beta decay — arXiv · INSPIRE · DOI

  • This paper tests a range of neutrino-argon interaction models implemented in the GENIE event generator against recent MicroBooNE measurements of pionless charged-current interactions. The comparison finds strong separation between competing models in some cases, giving upcoming neutrino experiments clearer choices for their simulations.

    Benchmarking state-of-the-art theory and empirical models of pionless neutrino-argon … — arXiv · INSPIRE · DOI

  • The paper derives an analytical expression for the maximum neutrino flux a blazar can produce as a function of its observed X-ray flux within the one-zone leptohadronic jet model, addressing the tension between neutrino candidate blazars and stringent X-ray constraints. Applied to a sample of neutrino candidate blazars, the analytical and full numerical results agree.

    On the Maximum Neutrino Flux of Blazars in the One-zone Leptohadronic Model — arXiv · INSPIRE · DOI

  • This paper proposes a minimal dark SU(2) gauge symmetry that forbids the Standard Model Yukawa coupling of one right-handed neutrino-like fermion, enforcing a rank-two Dirac neutrino mass matrix with one exactly massless neutrino while remaining consistent with oscillation and cosmological data. Anomaly cancellation requires a second dark-sector fermion doublet, and a discrete symmetry lets the two doublets pair up without generating Majorana masses.

    Minimal dark S U ( 2 ) origin of a massless Dirac neutrino — arXiv · INSPIRE · DOI

  • This paper builds a secluded dark matter framework with an extra U(1)_X gauge symmetry, a Dirac dark matter particle, three heavy neutrinos, and a singlet scalar that mixes with the Higgs. Because the Higgs-portal mixing angle arises at one loop from the same Yukawa couplings that generate active neutrino masses, the model links dark matter phenomenology directly to the neutrino mass spectrum.

    Neutrino mass and secluded dark matter puzzles solved together — INSPIRE · DOI
