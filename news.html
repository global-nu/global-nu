Experiments and results

Physics Nobel Prize

The Royal Swedish Academy of Sciences awarded the 2026 Nobel Prize in Physics to Francis Halzen of the University of Wisconsin–Madison, principal investigator of IceCube, 'for decisive contributions to the IceCube Neutrino Observatory and the discovery of high-energy neutrinos of astrophysical origin'. CERN describes the work as paving the way for a new generation of observatories that use neutrinos as a messenger of extreme environments.

KM3NeT construction

The collaboration reports two further marine-operation successes for ARCA and ORCA. For ARCA, Cable Termination Frame 3 was installed at the deep-sea site during a campaign conducted by Alcatel Submarine Networks. Separately, KM3NeT has been recognised as a Landmark in the ESFRI Roadmap 2026.

SBND performance

The SBND Collaboration published a paper describing the as-built detector, a 112 tonne active-mass liquid argon time projection chamber in the Booster Neutrino Beam at Fermilab, and its first year of beam operation. All detector systems performed at or above design requirements. The electron lifetime exceeded 30 ms, ten times the design requirement, for more than 95% of Run 1.

Short-Baseline Near Detector (SBND): Design and Initial Performance — arXiv
PandaX-4T supernova search

PandaX-4T reported the first search for the diffuse supernova neutrino background using coherent elastic neutrino-nucleus scattering, which is sensitive to all flavours. With a total exposure of 1.54 tonne·year from the commissioning and first science runs, it observed no significant excess over the expected background. It set a 90% confidence-level upper limit of (2.27–2.43)×10^3 cm^-2 s^-1 per flavour on the ν_x flux for neutrino energies above 19.3 MeV.

Search for Diffuse Supernova Neutrino Background of All Flavors with PandaX-4T — arXiv
Atmospheric neutrino combination

A preprint combines the latest publicly available atmospheric-neutrino data from Super-Kamiokande (all stages before the gadolinium phase), IceCube-DeepCore and the six-detection-unit configuration of KM3NeT/ORCA. The fit uses all event samples simultaneously, treats shared systematic uncertainties as fully correlated and models detector-response uncertainties independently for each experiment. The title reports evidence for normal neutrino ordering.

Evidence for Normal Neutrino Ordering with Combined Atmospheric Neutrino Experiments — arXiv
JUNO oscillation parameters

A preprint by I. Alikhanov states that the JUNO Collaboration has reported the most precise measurements to date of both Δm²21 and Δm²31. The author argues that an empirical relation between the two splittings, proposed earlier on empirical grounds, is in excellent agreement with these data. This is the author's interpretation, not a JUNO statement.

T2K near-detector upgrade

The upgrade of the ND280 near detector was completed in 2024 and has significantly enhanced its tracking capabilities. A published paper presents an event selection for muon-neutrino charged-current interactions with single positively charged pion production in the new modules, designed to maximise kinematic acceptance. A second paper proposes a deep neural network to distinguish electrons, photons, muons, pions and protons in the SuperFGD, the highly granular scintillator target of the upgraded detector.

Baikal-GVD point sources

Baikal-GVD is a cubic-kilometre-scale detector under construction in Lake Baikal and is now 70% complete. A search for point-like astrophysical sources with a five-year track-like dataset from 2019–2024 found no significant source. The authors computed upper limits for selected candidate objects and compared them with published results from IceCube, ANTARES and KM3NeT.

Search for Point-Like Neutrino Sources with Baikal-GVD — INSPIRE · DOI
Galactic centre with IceCube

IceCube published a piece on using IceCube and HAWC data to investigate the centre of the Galaxy. The region contains dense gas and potential accelerators, including a supermassive black hole, and offers a window on how cosmic rays reach extremely high energies. Particles accelerated to petaelectronvolt energies are of particular interest.

Theory highlights

recently published, with the links each record carries

  • The paper considers magnetic and oscillation effects on neutrinos emitted from a protoneutron star in a magnetorotational supernova. It finds that oscillations in the outer layers of the star harden the electron-antineutrino spectrum significantly, and that this enhancement is particularly pronounced for the inverted mass ordering. This is relevant to ground-based detection of supernova neutrinos.

    Magnetorotational Supernova Neutrino Spectra Versus Mass Ordering — INSPIRE · DOI

  • The authors compute two-neutrino double-beta decay rates without decoupling nuclear structure from the emitted lepton kinematics, using a single unified formula. They present deviations in rates and electron spectra from closure, non-closure and Taylor-expansion approximations for 82Se and 136Xe. The approach gives a more realistic description of the decay.

    Direct calculation of the two-neutrino double-beta decay rate including the full … — arXiv · INSPIRE · DOI

  • The paper studies the detectability of neutrinos from past Galactic core-collapse supernovae through nuclear-recoil tracks in ancient minerals (paleodetectors). It varies neutron-star masses, includes failed supernovae that form black holes, and considers the role of the nuclear equation of state. Sensitivity is enhanced for models with larger neutrino emission, which occurs for more compact remnants.

    Paleodetectors for neutrino signals from diverse Galactic stellar collapses — arXiv · INSPIRE · DOI

  • The authors study how scalar-mediated neutrino self-interactions during propagation through the cosmic neutrino background would leave spectral imprints on the diffuse supernova neutrino background. The analysis uses a full three-flavour framework that retains the complete PMNS structure and considers four representative flavour-diagonal coupling structures. It bears on what a future detection of this flux could constrain.

    Diffuse Supernova Neutrinos with Secret Neutrino Interactions — arXiv · INSPIRE · DOI

  • The paper investigates neutrino mass and thermal leptogenesis in the Babu-Nandi-Tavartkiladze model, which has a scalar quadruplet and a pair of vectorlike fermion triplets. Neutrino masses arise from a dimension-7 operator at tree level and a dimension-5 operator at one loop, so sub-eV masses are possible with new physics at the TeV scale. The authors explore whether thermal leptogenesis is viable here, which differs from canonical seesaw-based leptogenesis.

    Thermal leptogenesis in the Babu-Nandi-Tavartkiladze model of neutrino mass — arXiv · INSPIRE · DOI

  • The authors study composite neutrino models in which heavy neutrinos are bound states of a near-conformal strongly coupled sector and mix with Standard Model neutrinos through an inverse seesaw. They find enhanced electromagnetic transition dipole operators, parametrically larger than the loop-level predictions of minimal Dirac or Majorana models. They also carry out an event-level simulation of the production-and-decay signature.

    Neutrino dipole moments and radiative signatures from partial compositeness — arXiv · INSPIRE · DOI
