KM3NeT and ANTARES with gravitational waves

A joint ANTARES and KM3NeT paper presents two searches that combine LIGO-Virgo third-run data with ANTARES and ORCA data. In one, selected high-energy neutrino candidates serve as triggers to look for gravitational-wave candidates. In the other, subthreshold candidates are searched for spatial and temporal coincidences.

P-ONE photomultipliers

P-ONE is a planned cubic-kilometre-scale neutrino telescope for the Pacific Ocean, using Ocean Networks Canada infrastructure. A new paper presents the systematic characterisation of all the 3-inch Hamamatsu R14374-10 photomultiplier tubes for the first detector line before deployment. That line will carry 320 of them, and the work checks them against the detector acceptance criteria.

Baikal-GVD point sources

Baikal-GVD, a cubic-kilometre-scale detector under construction in Lake Baikal, is now 70% complete and has taken data in partial configurations since 2016. A search for point-like sources in a five-year track-like dataset from 2019 to 2024 found no significant source. Upper limits are given for selected candidate objects and compared with published IceCube, ANTARES and KM3NeT results.

T2K near detector upgrade

SuperFGD is a key component of the upgrade of the T2K near detector ND280. It is a segmented plastic scintillator made of about 2 million optically isolated 1 cm³ cubes, read out by wavelength-shifting fibres, and it provides 3D images of neutrino interactions. A recent paper reports its performance in the T2K neutrino beam.

IceCube data and X-ray transients

An analysis of 14 years of publicly available IceCube muon-track data searched for neutrinos from 27 fast X-ray transients detected by Einstein Probe. It uses the unbinned maximum-likelihood method of IceCube point-source searches and finds no statistically significant excess. The largest test statistic, TS=4.77 for EP240801a, corresponds to a local p-value of 0.04, and 90% confidence-level upper limits on the muon-neutrino flux are given.

Neutrino Emissivity in Δ -Direct-Urca Processes Inside Neutron Stars

The paper studies the neutrino emissivity of Δ-direct-Urca processes in neutron stars, motivated by the possibility that Δ isobars exist in the dense core of massive stars. Neutrino emission through Urca processes is a dominant cooling mechanism, so it matters for thermal-evolution modelling of neutron stars.

Constraining ultralight vector bosons via solar neutrino oscillations: The gauged L μ - L τ model and prospects for JUNO and XLZD

The authors extend the Standard Model with a gauged U(1) Lμ−Lτ symmetry and an ultralight vector boson that mixes kinetically with the photon. This produces an effective matter potential inside the Sun that modifies MSW flavour conversion. The paper uses solar neutrino measurements from Borexino, SNO and Super-Kamiokande, and its title points to prospects for JUNO and XLZD.

Direct calculation of the two-neutrino double-beta decay rate including the full lepton-energy dependence

The paper computes two-neutrino double-beta decay rates with the full interdependence of nuclear structure and lepton kinematics, rather than decoupling them as earlier calculations did. It compares the resulting rates and electron spectra with closure, non-closure and Taylor-expansion approximations for 82Se and 136Xe. This bears on how the two-neutrino spectrum is modelled in neutrinoless searches.

When can decoherence reveal a Majorana phase? A phase-covariance no-go theorem for two-flavor neutrino oscillations

The paper proves a no-go theorem for observing a Majorana phase through open two-flavour neutrino oscillations. Every completely positive trace-preserving evolution that is covariant under rotations of the mass-coherence plane is exactly blind to the phase. Within the unital Pauli-GKSL class, a rate anisotropy in the environment can supply a phase reference.

Thermal leptogenesis in the Babu-Nandi-Tavartkiladze model of neutrino mass

The authors study neutrino mass and thermal leptogenesis in the Babu-Nandi-Tavartkiladze model, which has a scalar quadruplet and two vectorlike fermion triplets. Neutrino masses arise from a dimension-7 operator at tree level and a dimension-5 operator at one loop, allowing sub-eV masses with new physics at the TeV scale. They examine whether thermal leptogenesis works in this setting, which differs from canonical seesaw leptogenesis.

Paleodetectors for neutrino signals from diverse Galactic stellar collapses

The paper asks whether neutrinos from past Galactic core-collapse supernovae could be recorded as nuclear-recoil tracks in ancient minerals, known as paleodetectors. It varies neutron-star masses, includes failed supernovae that form black holes, and considers the role of the nuclear equation of state. It quantifies how sensitivity grows for models with larger neutrino emission.
