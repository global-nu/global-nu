Experiments and results

Super-Kamiokande DSNB

Super-Kamiokande reports an indication of electron antineutrinos from the diffuse supernova neutrino background, from a spectral fit to a 5003.0-day dataset. Improvements over earlier searches include better rejection of atmospheric-neutrino backgrounds, updated systematic uncertainties and a refined spectral fit. For a representative model the best-fit flux is 3.6 +1.6/-1.5 cm^-2 s^-1 above 13.3 MeV, a 2.6σ tension with the null hypothesis. Other signal spectral shapes gave similar significances.

CONUS+ reactor CEvNS

CONUS+ reports what it calls the most precise measurement to date of coherent elastic neutrino-nucleus scattering from reactor antineutrinos, using germanium detectors at the Leibstadt power plant. A blind likelihood analysis of 1296 kg·days reactor-on exposure gives 1555 ± 152 events at 10.2σ, within 0.6 standard deviations of the Standard Model expectation of 1701 ± 198. The result also yields sin²θ_W = 0.225 ± 0.022 at a momentum transfer of about 10 MeV. A companion paper describes the run-2 analysis after three of the original 1 kg detectors were replaced by larger 2.42 kg diodes.

SBND long-lived particles

The SBND collaboration has searched for long-lived particles produced by neutrino-nucleus upscattering that decay to visible final states. The search uses a subset of the Cosmic Ray Tagger that ran as a standalone detector in 2017-2018, with data corresponding to 2.95×10^20 protons on target. Neutrino data taking with the full SBND detector began in December 2024. Separately, Fermilab highlights the SPINE machine-learning reconstruction, deployed in the SBN programme and DUNE.

Baikal-GVD point sources

Baikal-GVD, now 70% complete, has searched for point-like astrophysical neutrino sources in a five-year track-like dataset from 2019-2024. No significant source was found, so upper limits are set for selected candidate objects. The limits are compared with published results from IceCube, ANTARES and KM3NeT.

T2K SuperFGD

SuperFGD is a key component of the upgrade of the T2K near detector ND280. It is made of about 2 million optically isolated 1 cm³ scintillator cubes and provides 3D images of neutrino interactions. A companion paper proposes a deep neural network to distinguish electrons, photons, muons, pions and protons in the detector, with the aim of reducing systematic uncertainties in T2K's oscillation measurements.

PUEO balloon payload

The PUEO collaboration presents the design and flight of a balloon-borne antenna array. It is built to detect Askaryan emission from ultrahigh-energy neutrino interactions. Such neutrinos are expected to accompany ultrahigh-energy cosmic-ray production and propagation, and their low predicted flux requires very large detection volumes.

When can decoherence reveal a Majorana phase? A phase-covariance no-go theorem for two-flavor neutrino oscillations

The paper proves a no-go theorem for observing a Majorana phase through open two-flavour oscillations: any completely positive trace-preserving evolution covariant under rotations of the mass-coherence plane is exactly blind to the phase. A rate anisotropy among the transverse decoherence rates can supply an environmental phase reference. It is relevant to anyone asking whether decoherence could expose a Majorana phase in oscillation data.

The paper studies a gauged U(1) Lμ-Lτ model with an ultralight vector boson that mixes kinetically with the photon. Thermal leptogenesis in the Babu-Nandi-Tavartkiladze model of neutrino mass

The authors examine thermal leptogenesis in the Babu-Nandi-Tavartkiladze model, where neutrino masses arise from a dimension-7 operator at tree level and a dimension-5 operator at one loop. The model accommodates sub-eV neutrino masses with new physics at the TeV scale. The study asks whether leptogenesis works in this framework, which differs from canonical seesaw leptogenesis.

Fermionic dark matter and neutrino masses in a minimal U(1)PQ inverse seesaw framework

The paper proposes a minimal Standard Model extension in which a single global U(1)PQ symmetry sets the scales of an inverse seesaw and stabilises a fermionic dark matter candidate. The resulting axion-like particle mediates between the dark and visible sectors. The authors state that the framework predicts a strong correlation between the effective Majorana mass and the CP-violating phase, which makes it testable in future experiments.

The paper examines the neutrino emissivity of Δ-direct-Urca processes in neutron-star interiors, where Δ isobars may accompany nucleons and hyperons. Paleodetectors for neutrino signals from diverse Galactic stellar collapses

The paper investigates whether neutrinos from past Galactic core-collapse supernovae could leave nuclear-recoil tracks in ancient minerals used as paleodetectors. It includes variations in neutron-star mass, failed supernovae that form black holes, and the role of the nuclear equation of state. The authors quantify how sensitivity increases for models with larger neutrino emission.
