Experiments and results

DUNE construction

Construction of the Long-Baseline Neutrino Facility, which hosts DUNE, is entering its next phase, with critical components arriving and partner institutions contributing. UK teams have completed half of the 137 Anode Plane Assemblies, the sensors that detect the electrical signals from neutrino interactions in liquid argon. Black Hills State University has also joined the collaboration, which now counts more than 1,500 collaborators from 228 institutions in 38 countries and CERN.

SLAC researchers developed SPINE, a machine-learning program that reconstructs neutrino interactions from complex detector images. It is currently deployed in neutrino experiments including the SBN programme and DUNE.

KM3NeT and gravitational waves

KM3NeT published a paper on searches for coincident gravitational waves and high-energy neutrinos, using KM3NeT and ANTARES data from the O3 observing run. The motivation is that sources of gravitational waves may also produce high-energy neutrinos. Separately, the collaboration held its Autumn 2026 meeting in Siracusa, with status updates on detector operations on the agenda.

Project 8 tritium source

Project 8 aims to measure the neutrino mass from tritium beta decay using cyclotron radiation emission spectroscopy, with a stated sensitivity goal of 40 meV/c^2. A new preprint describes a prototype electron cyclotron resonance dissociator, driven by a tunable 1 kW, 2.45 GHz RF generator and electromagnets, that ignites a hydrogen plasma. It is one stage of the planned beamline that dissociates molecular tritium.

Auger sterile search

The Pierre Auger Collaboration studies the observatory's sensitivity to sterile neutrinos of MeV-to-100 MeV mass in a 3+1 framework, with the sterile state mixing mainly with the tau flavour. Such neutrinos could cross long chords of the Earth and produce tau leptons that decay in the atmosphere and generate air showers. The exposures are computed with a detailed Monte Carlo simulation of neutrino propagation.

CMS heavy neutral leptons

CMS reports a search for a Majorana heavy neutral lepton produced in top quark decays, in a type-I seesaw scenario. It uses 13 TeV proton-proton data from 2016 to 2018 corresponding to 138 fb^-1, and selects events with a same-sign, same-flavour lepton pair.

NINJA FROST tracker

A meter-scale scintillator tracker, FROST, was built and operated in the NINJA experiment to match muon tracks between the nuclear emulsion detectors and the downstream muon detector. During J-PARC neutrino-beam operation it reached a data acquisition efficiency of 99.59% and a detection efficiency of 99.946±0.006% for selected neutrino-induced muons well within the active area.

Neutrino Emissivity in Δ -Direct-Urca Processes Inside Neutron Stars

The paper evaluates the neutrino emissivity of Δ-direct-Urca processes in neutron-star matter, a contribution the authors say had not been explicitly computed. It matters for neutron-star thermal evolution, where Urca processes are the dominant cooling mechanisms.

Toward measuring the low-energy cosmic-ray spectrum with next-generation neutrino detectors

The authors investigate whether kiloton-scale detectors such as Hyper-Kamiokande could determine the primary cosmic-ray spectrum from the atmospheric neutrino flux. They find that future detectors can distinguish cosmic-ray models with high statistical significance, even with uncertainties in neutrino cross sections and hadronic interactions. This connects atmospheric-neutrino measurements to a cosmic-ray input that is normally measured by satellites and balloons.

Neutrino production at the extreme vicinity of the supermassive black hole in NGC 1068

Motivated by updated IceCube data for NGC 1068 extending down to about 0.2 TeV, the paper computes hadronic neutrino production and accompanying cascade gamma rays. It explores the parameter space with Latin hypercube sampling, constrained by multimessenger observations. It is relevant for what the neutrino data imply about conditions near the black hole.

Quasisteady flavor configuration of multienergy neutrino ensembles

The paper presents semianalytic approximations for the quasisteady outcome of slow and fast flavour conversion in neutrino-dense environments. The ansatz agrees well with multiangle and multienergy solutions of the kinetic equations, independently of the mass ordering. Such subgrid schemes matter because supernova hydrodynamic simulations cannot include neutrino quantum kinetics.

When can decoherence reveal a Majorana phase? A phase-covariance no-go theorem for two-flavor neutrino oscillations

The paper proves a no-go theorem for observing a Majorana phase in open two-flavour neutrino oscillations. Any completely positive trace-preserving evolution covariant under rotations of the mass-coherence plane is exactly phase blind. A rate anisotropy can supply an environmental phase reference, which tells experimentalists what a decoherence environment would need to look like.

Exploring anomaly-free dark photon models through solar neutrino-electron scattering in ton-scale dark matter detectors

The authors use solar neutrino-electron scattering data from PandaX-4T and XENONnT to constrain anomaly-free U(1)_X dark photon models with kinetic and mass mixing. They derive exclusion limits for six benchmark models and find that the stringency of the limits correlates with the leptonic charge assignments. It shows how dark-matter detectors double as neutrino-physics probes.
