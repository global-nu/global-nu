Experiments and results

IceCube Earth tomography

IceCube described using atmospheric neutrinos, produced when cosmic rays strike the atmosphere, as a way to probe the interior structure of the Earth, a target that seismic and gravitational measurements have not fully resolved. The neutrinos travel through the planet before reaching the detector, so their behaviour along the way carries information about the material they pass through.

Probing the Earth’s interior using atmospheric neutrinos — Read it
WavePID identification tool

IceCube introduced WavePID, a technique that reads the detailed shape and timing of the Cherenkov light produced when a neutrino interacts in the ice to identify the particle that produced it. The method operates at the nanosecond level, exploiting fine timing structure in the light pattern.

WavePID: A tool that identifies neutrinos at the nanosecond level — Read it
DUNE automated triggering

DUNE is developing AI-based systems to sift through the large data volumes the experiment will produce, aiming to identify neutrino interactions, flag possible signs of a stellar explosion, and spot problems inside the detector automatically. The work draws on Fermilab's existing machine-learning programme and is part of preparations for full data analysis.

US lab to hunt most elusive ‘ghost particles’ deep underground with automated triggers — Read itDUNE uses AI to transform the future of neutrino research — Read it
DUNE high-voltage test

A DUNE prototype detector was put through a stress test at 300,000 volts ahead of the full detector's installation deep underground in the United States. The trial is part of ongoing validation of the detector technology before construction proceeds.

300,000 volts: US team puts world’s largest neutrino detector through extreme stress test — Read it
NINJA emulsion measurement

The NINJA emulsion detector, exposed to the J-PARC muon-neutrino beam, produced a measurement of charged-particle multiplicities and the kinematic distributions of muons, protons and charged pions in charged-current interactions on water. The analysis, based on 82 selected events from a 3.9 kg fiducial water volume, compared the reconstructed distributions with Monte Carlo predictions.

Measurement of reconstructed final-state kinematics in charged-current interactions on … — arXiv
JUNO calibration background

A study of JUNO's calibration system examined the radioactive background contributed by hardware placed close to the detector's target volume, since several calibration components sit near the liquid scintillator used for the mass-ordering measurement. Material screening and selection were used to keep the combined background from radioactivity within the budget required for reactor antineutrino analysis.

The Radioactive Background of the JUNO Calibration System — arXiv

Theory highlights

recently published, with the links each record carries

  • A global analysis combines results from multiple neutrinoless double beta decay experiments with nuclear matrix elements computed from ab initio nuclear theory to set limits on the Majorana neutrino mass. The ab initio calculations suggest the current generation of neutrinoless double beta decay experiments has likely not yet reached the sensitivity needed to probe the mass range indicated by less rigorous nuclear models.

    Global ab initio neutrino mass limits from neutrinoless double-beta decay — arXiv · INSPIRE · DOI

  • A phenomenological study examines how sensitive the DUNE and NOvA long-baseline experiments would be to an invisible decay of the third neutrino mass state into a sterile particle, under the normal mass ordering. The true value of the mixing angle theta23 strongly affects the resulting constraint on the decay parameter, with a value in the higher octant improving the sensitivity.

    Constraining invisible neutrino decay at NOνA and DUNE — INSPIRE · DOI

  • A model links two open problems by embedding both neutrino mass generation and a secluded dark-matter sector in an extra U(1) gauge symmetry, with a Dirac dark-matter particle and three heavy neutrinos. The portal between the dark sector and the Higgs arises only at one loop, through the same Yukawa couplings that generate the light neutrino masses, tying the dark-Higgs mixing to the heavy neutrino masses.

    Neutrino mass and secluded dark matter puzzles solved together — INSPIRE · DOI

  • A minimal axion model, built by treating the Peccei-Quinn symmetry as a flavour symmetry and combining the KSVZ axion framework with a type-I seesaw, predicts a specific texture in the neutrino mass matrix. That texture, together with the observed value of theta13, favours an inverted neutrino mass ordering and a particular relation between the light neutrino mass eigenvalues.

    Neutrino mass hierarchy and axion–lepton couplings in a minimal flavored νKSVZ model — INSPIRE · DOI

  • Using the GENIE event generator, this study benchmarks a range of theoretical and empirical models of neutrino-argon scattering against recent MicroBooNE measurements of pionless charged-current interactions. The comparison finds strong separation between competing models in some channels, giving accelerator-based neutrino experiments a clearer basis for choosing interaction models in their simulations.

    Benchmarking state-of-the-art theory and empirical models of pionless neutrino-argon … — arXiv · INSPIRE · DOI

  • A lepto-hadronic model of accretion-disk winds in active galactic nuclei, calibrated against radio and gamma-ray data from nearby Seyfert galaxies, is revisited as a possible source of the diffuse cosmic neutrino and gamma-ray backgrounds. Cosmic rays accelerated at the wind's forward and reverse shocks produce both leptonic and hadronic emission in the model, feeding predictions for the background levels.

    Revisiting Disk Winds in Active Galactic Nuclei as an Origin of Cosmic Gamma-Ray and … — arXiv · INSPIRE · DOI
