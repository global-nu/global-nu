Experiments and results

DUNE construction and outreach

The Deep Underground Neutrino Experiment, led by Fermilab, was named among a set of the most ambitious international science projects, cited as an example of how modern science crosses borders. Fermilab also highlighted a Rice University undergraduate program, DUNE-TECH, that introduces students to the experiment's science, and described work on AI-based automated triggers intended to sort through data, identify neutrino interactions, spot signs of a stellar explosion and detect problems inside the detector.

IceCube: NGC 1068 revisited

IceCube has carried out a search for steady-state neutrino emission using its DeepCore sub-array, following on from its earlier evidence for high-energy neutrinos from NGC 1068, an active galactic nucleus with a central supermassive black hole.

Search for steady-state neutrino emission using IceCube DeepCore — Read it
IceCube: transient sources

IceCube continues to search for astrophysical neutrino sources among transient phenomena, extending its programme to tidal disruption events, which occur on timescales of weeks to months. This follows the collaboration's identification of two galaxies as steady neutrino sources and its detection of neutrinos from the Milky Way.

IceCube: extra dimensions

IceCube has searched its data for signatures of extra dimensions, one of the proposed explanations for the hierarchy problem between the weak force and gravity.

IceCube Upgrade sensitivities

The collaboration has published projected sensitivities for the planned IceCube Upgrade, building on IceCube's existing evidence that active galaxies are sources of high-energy neutrinos and likely sites of hadronic particle acceleration.

IceCube funding renewed

The US National Science Foundation has renewed its cooperative agreement with the University of Wisconsin–Madison to operate and maintain IceCube, a five-year, $53 million agreement covering 2026-2031.

Lake Vermilion detector

A Fermilab-led underwater neutrino detection experiment is set to begin testing this fall in Lake Superior and Lake Vermilion, continuing Minnesota's history of neutrino research.

Underwater neutrino testing in Lake Superior, Lake Vermilion to begin this fall — Read it
T2K ND280 upgrade

T2K has commissioned the Time-of-Flight detector for its ND280 Upgrade, comprising six panels of plastic-scintillator bars surrounding the SuperFGD and the High-Angle Time Projection Chambers, read out via silicon photomultipliers and SAMPIC electronics. The detector is intended to reduce systematic uncertainties in oscillation-parameter measurements and to improve sensitivity to the CP-violating phase δCP.

MicroBooNE cross sections

MicroBooNE has reported its first measurement of flux-integrated cross sections as a function of kinematic imbalance variables for electron neutrino interactions on argon, using its full dataset of 1.3×10^21 protons on target from the Booster Neutrino Beam. These variables are sensitive to Fermi motion, multinucleon processes and final-state interactions in neutrino-nucleus scattering.

Theory highlights

recently published, with the links each record carries

  • This paper presents the first combined oscillation analysis of atmospheric neutrino data from Super-Kamiokande, IceCube-DeepCore and KM3NeT/ORCA together with reactor data from Daya Bay, fitting 839,048 events across 1536 bins with 91 parameters. Such cross-experiment combinations had long been considered infeasible outside the collaborations themselves. The unified fit finds no significant tension between the datasets and prefers the normal over the inverted mass ordering at 3σ significance.

    Atmospheric neutrino oscillations: The full picture — arXiv · INSPIRE · DOI

  • This paper derives global limits on the Majorana neutrino mass by combining likelihood functions from several neutrinoless double-beta decay experiments with nuclear matrix elements computed from ab initio nuclear theory in the in-medium similarity renormalization group approach. In contrast to conventional nuclear models, the ab initio treatment indicates that current-generation 0νββ experiments have likely not yet reached the sensitivity needed to probe the target mass range.

    Global ab initio neutrino mass limits from neutrinoless double-beta decay — arXiv · INSPIRE · DOI

  • This review summarises the properties of KM3-230213A, the ultra-high-energy neutrino event detected by the KM3NeT telescope with an inferred parent-neutrino energy of order 10^17 eV. It discusses possible interpretations, including active galactic nuclei, transient sources and cosmogenic neutrinos, and notes that no unambiguous electromagnetic counterpart has been found despite extensive follow-up.

    KM3-230213A: The Highest-Energy Cosmic Neutrino — INSPIRE · DOI

  • This paper introduces TAMBO, a proposed mountain-based, deep-valley neutrino observatory aimed at helping to map the high-energy neutrino sky, an area where the field has so far struggled to achieve full coverage. The authors present it as a route to both improved neutrino astronomy and possible clues to new physics.

    Measuring the high-energy neutrino sky using the deep-valley neutrino observatory TAMBO — INSPIRE · DOI

  • This paper revisits accretion-disk winds driven by active galactic nuclei as a possible source of the cosmic neutrino and gamma-ray backgrounds, building a lepto-hadronic wind model calibrated against radio and GeV gamma-ray data from nearby Seyfert galaxies detected by Fermi-LAT. Cosmic rays accelerated at the wind's forward and reverse shocks are found to produce synchrotron, external-Compton and hadronic emission relevant to both backgrounds.

    Revisiting Disk Winds in Active Galactic Nuclei as an Origin of Cosmic Gamma-Ray and … — arXiv · INSPIRE · DOI

  • This paper proposes a minimal dark SU(2) gauge symmetry that yields a rank-two Dirac neutrino mass matrix and enforces one exactly massless neutrino while remaining consistent with oscillation data and cosmological constraints. Anomaly cancellation is shown to require a second dark fermion doublet, with a discrete symmetry forbidding Majorana masses allowing the two dark doublets to pair up vectorially.

    Minimal dark S U ( 2 ) origin of a massless Dirac neutrino — arXiv · INSPIRE · DOI
