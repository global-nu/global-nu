Current release

Preliminary Bari Release 2026.a is shown on this site as preliminary results. The Δχ² tables (1D, 2D and the full 6D χ²), the tables as PDF, the notebook and the release package will be published with the paper. Until then the values on this site are preliminary and may change.

The values of the previous release (the 2026 (1, 2)-sector update on top of the March 2025 full release) remain available: CSV · JSON.
