What changed

against the published values (2025 release with the 2026 (1, 2)-sector update)

  • δm² 1σ accuracy 1.3% → 1.0%*
  • sin²θ₁₂ 1σ accuracy 2.4% → 1.8%*
  • Δm² 1σ accuracy 0.8% → 0.6%*
  • sin²θ₁₃ 1σ accuracy 2.4% → 2.5%*
  • sin²θ₂₃ 1σ accuracy 5.1% → 4.4%*
  • δ/π 1σ accuracy 18% → 16.3%*
  • Ordering NO at 2.2σ → NO at 3.9σ (Δχ² +5.0 → +15.34)

1σ accuracy: one sixth of the 3σ range divided by the best fit, in percent, as in the tables of our papers.

* computed from the release ranges, with the definition of our papers' tables.
