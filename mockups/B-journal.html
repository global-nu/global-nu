What we know about the oscillation parameters

Twenty-five years of global three-flavour analyses from the Bari group: best fits and allowed ranges from solar, atmospheric, reactor and accelerator data, combined with cosmology — each release published, documented and downloadable.

Conventions used here

δm² = m₂² − m₁² > 0 and Δm² = m₃² − (m₁² + m₂²)/2.

Other groups quote Δm²₃₁ or Δm²₂₃: the numbers are not comparable at a glance. Every comparison on this site states the conversion it used.
