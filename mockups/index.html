Homepage mockups

Three visual directions for global-nu.org. Each carries a full light and dark palette (button “theme” to flip) and every number is a placeholder — nothing on these pages is an invented value.

A — Observatory Dark-first, instrument feel: hairline grid, luminous teal, data in a monospaced readout. B — Journal Light-first, serif display, indigo accent, classic academic tables. The data is the design. C — Spectrum Data-forward: the hero is a Δχ² figure, two accents for the two mass orderings, oversized numerals.
