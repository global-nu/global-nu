<p class="small muted">Selection and ranking are deterministic:
arXiv's API
is queried for the configured categories, every
record that names the field is admitted, and those are scored against the
fixed keyword list below. No model is involved in choosing what appears here. The split
between the two streams is by the preprint's primary arXiv category:
hep-ex, nucl-ex, physics.ins-det are read as experimental, everything else as
theory, so a phenomenology paper cross-listed to an experimental category
still appears under theory.</p>

A longer word starting with one of them counts (neutrinophilic, neutrino-argon).</p><p class="small"><b>Strong terms, counted double</b> — neutrino, oscillation, neutrinoless, double beta, leptogenesis, seesaw, sterile, mass ordering, mass hierarchy</p><p class="small"><b>Supporting terms</b> — flavour symmetry, flavor symmetry, lepton mixing, lepton flavour, lepton flavor, fermion mass, mass matrix, flavon, modular, discrete symmetry, grand unification, leptonic CP, non-standard interaction, reactor, long-baseline, supernova</p><p class="small muted">Among those admitted, a term found in the title counts 3 to the 1 it counts in the abstract, a strong term counts double a supporting one, and the scores add.</p></details>
