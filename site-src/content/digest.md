## What will appear here

Two streams, kept separate because they are read differently:

**Experimental.** New results, measurements and detector papers from the
collaborations — the material that eventually enters a global analysis.

**Theory.** Recent work on masses and mixing, flavour symmetries, non-standard
interactions, cosmology and the rest of the phenomenology.

Each item carries its arXiv identifier, its authors, a short summary and the
links needed to go further. An extended version of the day's digest sits
alongside the compact list on the home page.

## How it is produced

A job on a machine that stays on queries the arXiv API each morning, scores the
day's submissions against a stated set of interests, and asks a model to write
the summaries. The result is committed and published automatically. When the
run fails, the page keeps the last successful digest and says so with its
timestamp, rather than showing an empty or half-built list.
