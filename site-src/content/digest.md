<p class="small muted">Ranking is deterministic: arXiv's RSS feeds
are queried for the configured categories, and each
record is scored against the fixed keyword list below. No model is involved in choosing what appears here. The split
between the two streams is by the preprint's primary arXiv category:
hep-ex, nucl-ex, physics.ins-det are read as experimental, everything else as
theory, so a phenomenology paper cross-listed to an experimental category
still appears under theory.</p>

<details style="margin-top:.6rem"><summary class="small muted">The 19 words the score is computed from</summary><p class="small"><b>Strong terms, counted double</b> — neutrino, neutrinos, oscillation, neutrinoless, double beta, leptogenesis, seesaw, sterile, mass ordering, mass hierarchy</p><p class="small"><b>Supporting terms</b> — flavour symmetry, flavor symmetry, modular, grand unification, leptonic CP, non-standard interaction, reactor, long-baseline, supernova</p><p class="small muted">A term found in the title counts 3 to the 1 it counts in the abstract, a strong term counts double a supporting one, and the scores add.</p></details>
