<div class="section-head"><h2>Theory highlights</h2><p>recently published, with the links each record carries</p></div>
<ul class="list list--news">
<li><p>The paper presents what it describes as the first combined oscillation analysis of recent atmospheric datasets from Super-Kamiokande, IceCube-DeepCore and KM3NeT/ORCA, together with Daya Bay reactor data. A single physics model describes all datasets with no significant parameter tensions, fitting 839 048 events across 1536 bins with 91 parameters. The combination prefers the normal over the inverted mass ordering at 3σ, which is relevant to anyone following the ordering question.</p><span class="cites"><span class="cite">Atmospheric neutrino oscillations: The full picture — <a href="https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.09714">arXiv</a> · <a href="https://inspirehep.net/literature/3166344">INSPIRE</a> · <a href="https://doi.org/10.1103/v4hl-pbpn">DOI</a></span></span></li>
<li><p>The authors combine current neutrinoless double-beta decay results with ab initio nuclear matrix elements from chiral effective field theory in a Bayesian framework to derive global Majorana mass limits. Unlike results from nuclear models, the ab initio results indicate that the current generation of experiments has likely not yet reached the required sensitivity. The choice of matrix elements therefore affects how far current searches reach.</p><span class="cites"><span class="cite">Global ab initio neutrino mass limits from neutrinoless double-beta decay — <a href="https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.09288">arXiv</a> · <a href="https://inspirehep.net/literature/3166340">INSPIRE</a> · <a href="https://doi.org/10.1103/rsw5-57fz">DOI</a></span></span></li>
<li><p>The paper studies whether neutrinos from past Galactic core-collapse supernovae could be detected as nuclear recoil tracks in ancient minerals (paleodetectors). It varies neutron star masses, includes failed supernovae that form black holes, and considers the nuclear equation of state. Sensitivity is enhanced for models with larger neutrino emission.</p><span class="cites"><span class="cite">Paleodetectors for neutrino signals from diverse Galactic stellar collapses — <a href="https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.26826">arXiv</a> · <a href="https://inspirehep.net/literature/3184513">INSPIRE</a> · <a href="https://doi.org/10.1103/gys2-q6bk">DOI</a></span></span></li>
<li><p>The paper considers magnetic and oscillation effects on neutrinos emitted from a protoneutron star in a supernova. The hardness of the electron antineutrino spectrum increases significantly through oscillations in the outer layers of the star. The enhancement is most pronounced for the inverted mass ordering and is favourable for ground-based detection.</p><span class="cites"><span class="cite">Magnetorotational Supernova Neutrino Spectra Versus Mass Ordering — <a href="https://inspirehep.net/literature/3207686">INSPIRE</a> · <a href="https://doi.org/10.1134/S1063779626701182">DOI</a></span></span></li>
<li><p>The paper studies invisible decay of ν3 into a sterile state, assuming normal ordering, and asks how well NOvA and DUNE can constrain the decay parameter. The constraint depends significantly on the true value of θ23. This matters for assessing how much of the decay bound comes from the octant of θ23.</p><span class="cites"><span class="cite">Constraining invisible neutrino decay at NOνA and DUNE — <a href="https://inspirehep.net/literature/3188867">INSPIRE</a> · <a href="https://doi.org/10.1007/s12043-026-03167-8">DOI</a></span></span></li>
<li><p>The authors build composite neutrino models in which heavy neutrinos are bound states of a near-conformal strongly coupled sector, mixing with Standard Model neutrinos through an inverse seesaw. Matching onto the low-energy theory gives transition dipole couplings of order 10^-6 to 10^-8 GeV^-1, parametrically larger than loop-level predictions of minimal Dirac or Majorana models. They carry out an event-level simulation of the production-and-decay signature.</p><span class="cites"><span class="cite">Neutrino dipole moments and radiative signatures from partial compositeness — <a href="https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.20987">arXiv</a> · <a href="https://inspirehep.net/literature/3170873">INSPIRE</a> · <a href="https://doi.org/10.1007/JHEP09(2026)234">DOI</a></span></span></li>
</ul>
