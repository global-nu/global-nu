<div class="stamp stamp--no">Super-Kamiokande reactor antineutrinos</div>
<p>Super-Kamiokande has reported the first observation of electron antineutrinos from nuclear reactors in the detector. The analysis uses SK-Gd data, in which neutron capture on dissolved gadolinium improves sensitivity to low-energy electron antineutrinos, with a total exposure of 411.52 live days. The observed spectra and monthly rates are consistent with the oscillated reactor prediction after accounting for accidental, geoneutrino and spallation backgrounds. The no-reactor hypothesis is disfavoured at approximately 6σ.</p>

<div class="stamp stamp--no">PandaX-4T supernova background</div>
<p>This is the first search for the diffuse supernova neutrino background that uses coherent elastic neutrino-nucleus scattering, here in the PandaX-4T liquid-xenon detector at the China Jinping Underground Laboratory. With a total exposure of 1.54 tonne·year from the commissioning and first science runs, no significant event excess over the expected background was seen. The upper limit on the flux per flavour of ν_x, for neutrino energies above 19.3 MeV, is (2.27–2.43)×10^3 cm^-2 s^-1 at 90% confidence level.</p>
