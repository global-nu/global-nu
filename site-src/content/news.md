<li><p>Combining results from multiple neutrinoless double-beta decay experiments with ab initio nuclear matrix elements derived from chiral effective field theory, this analysis sets global Bayesian limits on the Majorana neutrino mass. <li><p>Working within R-parity-violating supersymmetry with comparatively light third-generation sleptons, this paper derives the neutrino non-standard interactions that arise from LLE-type couplings and checks the resulting parameter space against a broad set of experimental constraints.
