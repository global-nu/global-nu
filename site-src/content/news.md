<li><p>Combining results from several neutrinoless double-beta decay searches with nuclear matrix elements computed from ab initio nuclear theory, the authors derive global Bayesian limits on the Majorana neutrino mass. <li><p>The paper builds a secluded dark-matter model around an extra U(1) gauge symmetry, in which a Dirac dark-matter particle, three heavy neutrinos, and a Higgs-mixing singlet scalar are linked so that the same Yukawa structure generating active neutrino masses also fixes the Higgs-portal mixing angle at one loop. This ties dark-matter and neutrino-mass model building together in a single framework.</p><span class="cites"><span class="cite">Neutrino mass and secluded dark matter puzzles solved together — <a href="https://inspirehep.net/literature/3162132">INSPIRE</a> · <a href="https://doi.org/10.1103/np7k-2fck">DOI</a></span></span></li>
<li><p>Using the GENIE event generator, the authors benchmark a range of theoretical and empirical models of neutrino-argon scattering against recent MicroBooNE measurements of pionless charged-current interactions.
