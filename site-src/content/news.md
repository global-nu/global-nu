<div class="stamp stamp--no">Super-Kamiokande reactor antineutrinos</div>
<p>Super-Kamiokande reports a first observation of reactor electron antineutrinos, using SK-Gd data with a total exposure of 411.52 live days. The prompt energy spectra and monthly event rates were consistent with the oscillated reactor prediction after accounting for accidental, geoneutrino and spallation backgrounds. The no-reactor hypothesis is disfavoured at approximately 6σ.</p>

<div class="stamp stamp--no">SBND detector paper</div>
<p>The SBND Collaboration describes the as-built detector, a 112 tonne active-mass liquid argon time projection chamber in the Booster Neutrino Beam at Fermilab and part of the Short-Baseline Neutrino Program. SBND began operation in 2024 and has accumulated what the paper calls the world's largest sample of neutrino-argon interactions. All detector systems performed at or above design requirements. The electron lifetime exceeded 30 ms, ten times the design requirement, for more than 95% of Run 1.</p>

<div class="stamp stamp--no">PandaX-4T supernova background</div>
<p>PandaX-4T presents the first search for the diffuse supernova neutrino background using coherent elastic neutrino-nucleus scattering, at the China Jinping Underground Laboratory. With a total exposure of 1.54 tonne·year from the commissioning and first science runs, it observes no significant excess over the expected background. It sets an upper limit of (2.27–2.43)×10³ cm⁻²s⁻¹ per flavour at 90% confidence level on the ν_x flux for neutrino energies above 19.3 MeV.</p>

<div class="stamp stamp--no">Baikal-GVD point sources</div>
<p>Baikal-GVD is a cubic-kilometre-scale detector under construction in Lake Baikal, now 70% complete, and has taken data in partial configurations since 2016. A search for point-like astrophysical sources in a five-year track-like event sample from 2019–2024 found no significant source. Upper limits are computed for a selection of candidate objects and compared with published results from IceCube, ANTARES and KM3NeT.</p>
