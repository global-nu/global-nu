<p>JUNO reported an improved oscillation measurement and an indication of the neutrino mass ordering, using 207.2 live days and 8,294 inverse beta-decay candidates at an average reactor baseline of 52.5 km. <div class="stamp stamp--no">Super-Kamiokande reactor signal</div>
<p>Super-Kamiokande reported the first observation of reactor electron antineutrinos in the detector, using 411.52 live days from the SK-Gd phase, which began in 2020. The prompt energy spectra and monthly event rates are consistent with the oscillated reactor prediction after accounting for accidental, geoneutrino and spallation backgrounds. The no-reactor hypothesis is disfavoured at approximately 6σ.</p>

<div class="stamp stamp--no">MicroBooNE proton kinematics</div>
<p>MicroBooNE presented the first double-differential cross-section measurements for muon-neutrino charged-current interactions on argon with no mesons and one or more protons in the final state. The analysis uses the full Booster Neutrino Beam dataset, 1.30 × 10^21 protons on target. Cross sections are given as functions of proton multiplicity, proton momenta and muon-proton opening angles, up to the fourth-leading proton.</p>

<div class="stamp stamp--no">MINERvA superscaling</div>
<p>MINERvA reported the first measurement of the superscaling variable in neutrino-nucleus scattering. It used charged-current quasielastic-like muon-neutrino interactions on carbon in the medium-energy NuMI beam. The variable is reconstructed from visible final-state kinematics, and its peak position is sensitive to the invisible nuclear missing energy.</p>

<div class="stamp stamp--no">PandaX-4T supernova background</div>
<p>PandaX-4T presented the first search for the diffuse supernova neutrino background through coherent elastic neutrino-nucleus scattering. With an exposure of 1.54 tonne·year, it observed no significant excess over the expected background. It set a 90% confidence level upper limit of (2.27–2.43) ×10^3 cm^-2 s^-1 per flavour on the ν_x flux for neutrino energies above 19.3 MeV.</p>
