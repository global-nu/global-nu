<div class="stamp stamp--no">IceCube Earth tomography</div>
<p>IceCube described using atmospheric neutrinos, produced when cosmic rays strike the atmosphere, as a way to probe the interior structure of the Earth, a target that seismic and gravitational measurements have not fully resolved. <div class="stamp stamp--no">WavePID identification tool</div>
<p>IceCube introduced WavePID, a technique that reads the detailed shape and timing of the Cherenkov light produced when a neutrino interacts in the ice to identify the particle that produced it. The method operates at the nanosecond level, exploiting fine timing structure in the light pattern.</p>

<div class="stamp stamp--no">NINJA emulsion measurement</div>
<p>The NINJA emulsion detector, exposed to the J-PARC muon-neutrino beam, produced a measurement of charged-particle multiplicities and the kinematic distributions of muons, protons and charged pions in charged-current interactions on water. The analysis, based on 82 selected events from a 3.9 kg fiducial water volume, compared the reconstructed distributions with Monte Carlo predictions.</p>

<p>A study of JUNO's calibration system examined the radioactive background contributed by hardware placed close to the detector's target volume, since several calibration components sit near the liquid scintillator used for the mass-ordering measurement.
