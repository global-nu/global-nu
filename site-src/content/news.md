<div class="stamp stamp--no">T2K ND280 upgrade</div>
<p>T2K has commissioned the Time-of-Flight detector for its ND280 Upgrade, comprising six panels of plastic-scintillator bars surrounding the SuperFGD and the High-Angle Time Projection Chambers, read out via silicon photomultipliers and SAMPIC electronics. The detector is intended to reduce systematic uncertainties in oscillation-parameter measurements and to improve sensitivity to the CP-violating phase δCP.</p>

<p>MicroBooNE has reported its first measurement of flux-integrated cross sections as a function of kinematic imbalance variables for electron neutrino interactions on argon, using its full dataset of 1.3×10^21 protons on target from the Booster Neutrino Beam. <li><p>This paper presents the first combined oscillation analysis of atmospheric neutrino data from Super-Kamiokande, IceCube-DeepCore and KM3NeT/ORCA together with reactor data from Daya Bay, fitting 839,048 events across 1536 bins with 91 parameters. Such cross-experiment combinations had long been considered infeasible outside the collaborations themselves. The unified fit finds no significant tension between the datasets and prefers the normal over the inverted mass ordering at 3σ significance.</p><span class="cites"><span class="cite">Atmospheric neutrino oscillations: The full picture — <a href="https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.09714">arXiv</a> · <a href="https://inspirehep.net/literature/3166344">INSPIRE</a> · <a href="https://doi.org/10.1103/v4hl-pbpn">DOI</a></span></span></li>
<li><p>This paper derives global limits on the Majorana neutrino mass by combining likelihood functions from several neutrinoless double-beta decay experiments with nuclear matrix elements computed from ab initio nuclear theory in the in-medium similarity renormalization group approach. In contrast to conventional nuclear models, the ab initio treatment indicates that current-generation 0νββ experiments have likely not yet reached the sensitivity needed to probe the target mass range.</p><span class="cites"><span class="cite">Global ab initio neutrino mass limits from neutrinoless double-beta decay — <a href="https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.09288">arXiv</a> · <a href="https://inspirehep.net/literature/3166340">INSPIRE</a> · <a href="https://doi.org/10.1103/rsw5-57fz">DOI</a></span></span></li>
