<div class="stamp stamp--no">NOvA hydrogen cross-section</div>
<p>The collaboration reported a measurement of the muon-antineutrino charged-current quasi-elastic cross section on hydrogen using the near detector in the NuMI beam, drawn from a 1.2 × 10^21 proton-on-target exposure. A topological and kinematic selection yielded 35,509 signal events in the 10.8%-hydrogen-rich detector, the highest statistics of (anti)neutrino-hydrogen interactions measured to date, with backgrounds from heavier nuclei constrained using dedicated control samples.</p>
