<div class="stamp stamp--no">Supernova background limit</div>
<p>PandaX-4T reported what it calls the first search for the diffuse supernova neutrino background using coherent elastic neutrino-nucleus scattering. Using an exposure of 1.54 tonne·year from the commissioning and first science runs, the collaboration observed no significant excess over the expected background. It set a 90% confidence-level upper limit of (2.27–2.43)×10^3 cm^-2 s^-1 per flavour on the ν_x flux for neutrino energies above 19.3 MeV.</p>
