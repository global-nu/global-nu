<div class="stamp stamp--no">PandaX-4T DSNB search</div>
<p>PandaX-4T reported the first search for the diffuse supernova neutrino background using coherent elastic neutrino-nucleus scattering. With an exposure of 1.54 tonne·year from the commissioning and first science runs, it observed no significant event excess over the expected background. It set an upper limit of (2.27–2.43) × 10^3 cm^-2 s^-1 per flavour at 90% confidence level on the ν_x flux, for incident neutrino energies above 19.3 MeV.</p>
