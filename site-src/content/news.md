<div class="stamp stamp--no">IceCube extra dimensions</div>
<p>A new analysis of 10.7 years of upward-going muon-neutrino data sets constraints on low-scale-gravity scenarios invoked to address the hierarchy problem, including large extra dimensions, by searching for matter-induced spectral distortions between 0.5 and 100 TeV. No such distortion was found, tightening bounds on the compactification scale. The result extends earlier collaboration work motivated by the long-standing puzzle of why gravity is so much weaker than the other forces.</p>

<div class="stamp stamp--no">Super-K proton decay</div>
<p>Analysing the full 401 kiloton-year pure-water-phase exposure of Super-Kamiokande, a search for proton decay into a charged antilepton plus an invisible massless particle found no significant excess over the expected atmospheric-neutrino background. The null result sets new lower limits on the proton partial lifetime, improving on previous limits by factors of 2 for the positron channel and 1.5 for the muon channel.</p>

<p>A dedicated search combined optical, UV and X-ray selected samples of 89 tidal disruption events with time-dependent likelihood analysis, looking for coincident high-energy neutrino emission from individual sources and in a stacked sample. Tidal disruption events are theorised to produce neutrinos through photohadronic interactions between accelerated protons and multi-wavelength photons in the accretion disc and outflows.</p>
