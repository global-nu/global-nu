<div class="stamp stamp--no">SBND performance</div>
<p>The SBND Collaboration published a paper describing the as-built detector, a 112 tonne active-mass liquid argon time projection chamber in the Booster Neutrino Beam at Fermilab, and its first year of beam operation. All detector systems performed at or above design requirements. The electron lifetime exceeded 30 ms, ten times the design requirement, for more than 95% of Run 1.</p>

<div class="stamp stamp--no">PandaX-4T supernova search</div>
<p>PandaX-4T reported the first search for the diffuse supernova neutrino background using coherent elastic neutrino-nucleus scattering, which is sensitive to all flavours. With a total exposure of 1.54 tonne·year from the commissioning and first science runs, it observed no significant excess over the expected background. It set a 90% confidence-level upper limit of (2.27–2.43)×10^3 cm^-2 s^-1 per flavour on the ν_x flux for neutrino energies above 19.3 MeV.</p>

<div class="stamp stamp--no">Atmospheric neutrino combination</div>
<p>A preprint combines the latest publicly available atmospheric-neutrino data from Super-Kamiokande (all stages before the gadolinium phase), IceCube-DeepCore and the six-detection-unit configuration of KM3NeT/ORCA. <div class="stamp stamp--no">Baikal-GVD point sources</div>
<p>Baikal-GVD is a cubic-kilometre-scale detector under construction in Lake Baikal and is now 70% complete. A search for point-like astrophysical sources with a five-year track-like dataset from 2019–2024 found no significant source.
