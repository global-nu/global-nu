<li><p>A combined oscillation fit brings together atmospheric data from Super-Kamiokande, IceCube-DeepCore and KM3NeT/ORCA with reactor data from Daya Bay, showing that a single unified model can describe all four datasets without significant tension between them, a combination long considered infeasible outside the individual collaborations. <li><p>This work derives global limits on the Majorana neutrino mass by combining recent neutrinoless double-beta decay results with nuclear matrix elements computed from first-principles chiral effective field theory within a Bayesian framework. <li><p>This study asks whether ancient minerals could preserve nuclear recoil tracks left by neutrinos from past Galactic core-collapse supernovae, extending the paleodetector concept across a range of collapse outcomes, including varying neutron star masses and failed supernovae that form black holes.
