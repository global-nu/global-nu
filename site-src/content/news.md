<div class="stamp stamp--no">PandaX-4T supernova background</div>
<p>PandaX-4T, a liquid xenon detector at the China Jinping Underground Laboratory, made the first search for the diffuse supernova neutrino background that uses coherent elastic neutrino-nucleus scattering. With a total exposure of 1.54 tonne·year from the commissioning and first science runs, it observed no significant event excess over the expected background. It set an upper limit of (2.27 – 2.43) × 10^3 cm^-2 s^-1 per flavour at 90% confidence level on the nu_x flux, for incident energies above 19.3 MeV.</p>

<p>Baikal-GVD is a cubic-kilometre-scale detector under construction in Lake Baikal and is now 70% complete.
