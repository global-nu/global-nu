<p>The first measurement of the superscaling variable in neutrino–nucleus scattering was reported, using charged-current quasielastic-like muon-neutrino interactions on carbon in the NuMI medium-energy beam. <ul class="list list--news">
<li><p>The paper presents what it calls the first combined oscillation analysis of recent atmospheric datasets from Super-Kamiokande, IceCube-DeepCore and KM3NeT/ORCA, together with Daya Bay reactor data. Such combinations were long considered infeasible outside the collaborations. The author finds no significant parameter tensions and a 3σ preference for normal over inverted mass ordering.</p><span class="cites"><span class="cite">Atmospheric neutrino oscillations: The full picture — <a href="https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.09714">arXiv</a> · <a href="https://inspirehep.net/literature/3166344">INSPIRE</a> · <a href="https://doi.org/10.1103/v4hl-pbpn">DOI</a></span></span></li>
<li><p>Majorana neutrino mass limits are derived by combining neutrinoless double-beta decay results with nuclear matrix elements computed ab initio from chiral effective field theory. In contrast to nuclear models, the ab initio results indicate that the current generation of experiments has likely not yet reached the required sensitivity. This matters for how close present searches are to the relevant mass region.</p><span class="cites"><span class="cite">Global ab initio neutrino mass limits from neutrinoless double-beta decay — <a href="https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.09288">arXiv</a> · <a href="https://inspirehep.net/literature/3166340">INSPIRE</a> · <a href="https://doi.org/10.1103/rsw5-57fz">DOI</a></span></span></li>
<li><p>The authors calculate two-neutrino double-beta decay rates without decoupling nuclear structure from the lepton kinematics. They compare the resulting rates and electron spectra with closure, non-closure and Taylor-expansion approximations for ⁸²Se and ¹³⁶Xe. This is relevant because the electron spectrum of the two-neutrino mode is the background in neutrinoless searches.</p><span class="cites"><span class="cite">Direct calculation of the two-neutrino double-beta decay rate including the full … — <a href="https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.31107">arXiv</a> · <a href="https://inspirehep.net/literature/3174998">INSPIRE</a> · <a href="https://doi.org/10.1140/epjc/s10052-026-16356-x">DOI</a></span></span></li>
<li><p>The paper asks whether neutrinos from past Galactic core-collapse supernovae could be detected through nuclear recoil tracks in ancient minerals. It varies neutron star masses, includes failed supernovae that form black holes, and considers the nuclear equation of state. Sensitivity is quantified as larger for models with greater neutrino emission.</p><span class="cites"><span class="cite">Paleodetectors for neutrino signals from diverse Galactic stellar collapses — <a href="https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.26826">arXiv</a> · <a href="https://inspirehep.net/literature/3184513">INSPIRE</a> · <a href="https://doi.org/10.1103/gys2-q6bk">DOI</a></span></span></li>
<li><p>The authors study composite neutrino models in which heavy neutrinos arise from a near-conformal strongly coupled sector and mix with Standard Model neutrinos through an inverse seesaw. They find transition dipole operators, with couplings d_μN ~ 10⁻⁶–10⁻⁸ GeV⁻¹, parametrically larger than loop-level predictions of minimal Dirac or Majorana models. They also carry out an event-level simulation of the production-and-decay signature.</p><span class="cites"><span class="cite">Neutrino dipole moments and radiative signatures from partial compositeness — <a href="https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.20987">arXiv</a> · <a href="https://inspirehep.net/literature/3170873">INSPIRE</a> · <a href="https://doi.org/10.1007/JHEP09(2026)234">DOI</a></span></span></li>
<li><p>The paper considers how magnetic and oscillation effects shape neutrino emission from a protoneutron star in a magnetorotational supernova. It finds that oscillations in the outer stellar layers harden the electron antineutrino spectrum, most clearly for inverted ordering. That makes the spectrum a possible handle on mass ordering in a future Galactic supernova.</p><span class="cites"><span class="cite">Magnetorotational Supernova Neutrino Spectra Versus Mass Ordering — <a href="https://inspirehep.net/literature/3207686">INSPIRE</a> · <a href="https://doi.org/10.1134/S1063779626701182">DOI</a></span></span></li>
