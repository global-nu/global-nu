<li><p>TAMBO, a proposed mountain-based neutrino observatory, is presented as a route to filling gaps in the mapping of the high-energy neutrino sky that have persisted despite a decade of progress in neutrino astronomy. <li><p>Combining results from multiple neutrinoless double-beta decay searches with nuclear matrix elements computed ab initio via the in-medium similarity renormalization group, the authors derive global Bayesian limits on the Majorana neutrino mass. <li><p>The authors propose a minimal dark SU(2) gauge symmetry under which one right-handed neutrino-like fermion is charged, forbidding its Standard Model Yukawa coupling and so enforcing one exactly massless neutrino, consistent with oscillation data.
