<ul class="list list--news">
<li><p>This paper builds a minimal flavoured axion model that treats the Peccei-Quinn symmetry as a flavour symmetry, combining the KSVZ axion framework with the type-I seesaw mechanism. The resulting texture in the inverse Majorana neutrino mass matrix, together with the observed smallness of θ13, predicts an inverted neutrino mass ordering with the ratio m1/m3 constrained to lie between 17 and 45, linking axion model-building directly to a testable prediction for neutrino mass ordering.</p><span class="cites"><span class="cite">Neutrino mass hierarchy and axion–lepton couplings in a minimal flavored νKSVZ model — <a href="https://inspirehep.net/literature/3193022">INSPIRE</a> · <a href="https://doi.org/10.1016/j.nuclphysb.2026.117628">DOI</a></span></span></li>
<li><p>The authors study how well combined NOvA and DUNE data could constrain an invisible decay of the third neutrino mass state into a sterile state, assuming the normal mass ordering. They find that the achievable constraint on the decay parameter depends strongly on the true value of θ23, with a value in the higher octant improving the sensitivity.</p><span class="cites"><span class="cite">Constraining invisible neutrino decay at NOνA and DUNE — <a href="https://inspirehep.net/literature/3188867">INSPIRE</a> · <a href="https://doi.org/10.1007/s12043-026-03167-8">DOI</a></span></span></li>
<li><p>This work models three-flavour neutrino oscillations as a qutrit coupled to a classical random-telegraph-noise environment acting as pure dephasing in the mass basis. The authors show that the ratio between the noise switching rate and coupling strength determines whether flavour coherence decays monotonically or shows memory-induced revivals, offering a framework for non-Markovian environmental effects on oscillations.</p><span class="cites"><span class="cite">Non-Markovian Dephasing and Flavor Coherence in a Three-Flavor Neutrino Qutrit — <a href="https://inspirehep.net/literature/3188404">INSPIRE</a> · <a href="https://doi.org/10.1007/s10773-026-06429-8">DOI</a></span></span></li>

<li><p>Using effective Lagrangians, chiral perturbation theory and a hadronic molecular model, this paper calculates cross sections for antineutrino-proton scattering into hyperons, charmed baryons and candidate hadronic molecular states.
