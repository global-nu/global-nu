<div class="stamp stamp--no">IceCube DeepCore</div>
<p>Analysis of 11.1 years of DeepCore data delivered a competitive sub-TeV time-integrated neutrino search, covering active galactic nuclei with high intrinsic X-ray flux (including NGC 1068), Galactic objects identified by Fermi-LAT with a pion-decay-like spectral shape, and an all-sky point-source scan. The DeepCore subarray extends IceCube's sensitivity down to sub-TeV energies, which is especially useful for sources with soft spectra. Four years ago IceCube reported evidence for high-energy neutrinos from NGC 1068, an active galactic nucleus with a central supermassive black hole.</p>

<div class="stamp stamp--no">NOvA Test Beam</div>
<p>NOvA operated a dedicated Test Beam experiment at the Fermilab Test Beam Facility from April 2019 to July 2022, using a 30-ton segmented liquid scintillator detector functionally identical to the NOvA Near and Far Detectors. The programme analysed tagged particles from proton-copper collisions to sharpen understanding of the systematic uncertainties in detector response and calibration that underpin NOvA's oscillation, mass-ordering and CP-violation measurements.</p>

<div class="stamp stamp--no">PandaX-4T beta decay limit</div>
<p>Using 148.4 kg·yr of xenon-136 exposure collected during commissioning and the first science runs, PandaX-4T searched for neutrinoless quadruple beta decay of Xe-136, a process whose observation, in the absence of neutrinoless double beta decay, would point to Dirac neutrinos. No excess over background was seen, and the collaboration set a lower limit on the half-life of 6.01×10^24 years at 90% confidence, the most stringent constraint on this process in xenon to date.</p>

<div class="stamp stamp--no">JUNO information limits</div>
<p>A new study applies quantum and classical Fisher information to quantify how precisely JUNO can determine oscillation parameters from reactor antineutrino data, tracking how information carried by the neutrino state is lost as it moves through detection and analysis to the observed events. The authors establish an information ladder for JUNO and show that this loss of precision depends strongly on which oscillation parameter is being estimated.</p>

<li><p>Eller presents the first combined oscillation analysis of atmospheric neutrino data from Super-Kamiokande, IceCube-DeepCore and KM3NeT/ORCA together with reactor data from Daya Bay, fitting 839,048 events across 1536 bins with 91 parameters. The unified model describes all four datasets without significant tensions between them and prefers the normal over the inverted mass ordering at 3σ significance, a result of direct interest to anyone tracking the mass-ordering question.</p><span class="cites"><span class="cite">Atmospheric neutrino oscillations: The full picture — <a href="https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.09714">arXiv</a> · <a href="https://inspirehep.net/literature/3166344">INSPIRE</a> · <a href="https://doi.org/10.1103/v4hl-pbpn">DOI</a></span></span></li>
<li><p>This paper introduces TAMBO, a proposed mountain-based neutrino observatory designed to help fill the gap in mapping the high-energy neutrino sky. The valley-based detector concept targets tau neutrino air showers and is presented as a route to both new source identification and new physics.</p><span class="cites"><span class="cite">Measuring the high-energy neutrino sky using the deep-valley neutrino observatory TAMBO — <a href="https://inspirehep.net/literature/3185371">INSPIRE</a> · <a href="https://doi.org/10.1038/s41550-026-02916-4">DOI</a></span></span></li>
<li><p>Shickele and collaborators combine likelihoods from current neutrinoless double-beta decay searches with nuclear matrix elements computed ab initio, within the in-medium similarity renormalization group approach, to set global Bayesian limits on the Majorana neutrino mass.
