<div class="section-head"><h2>Theory highlights</h2><p>recently published, with the links each record carries</p></div>
<ul class="list list--news">
<li><p>A combined oscillation analysis unites recent atmospheric neutrino datasets from Super-Kamiokande, IceCube-DeepCore and KM3NeT/ORCA together with reactor data from Daya Bay, fitting 839,048 events across 1536 bins with 91 parameters. The analysis finds no significant tension between the datasets and prefers the normal over the inverted mass ordering at 3σ significance, a combination previously thought infeasible outside the individual experimental collaborations.</p><span class="cites"><span class="cite">Atmospheric neutrino oscillations: The full picture — <a href="https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.09714">arXiv</a> · <a href="https://inspirehep.net/literature/3166344">INSPIRE</a> · <a href="https://doi.org/10.1103/v4hl-pbpn">DOI</a></span></span></li>
<li><p>Combining likelihoods from a suite of neutrinoless double-beta decay experiments with nuclear matrix elements computed via ab initio chiral effective field theory, this study derives global Bayesian limits on the Majorana neutrino mass. Unlike conventional nuclear models, the ab initio results suggest current-generation 0νββ experiments have likely not yet reached the sensitivity required to probe the mass regime of interest.</p><span class="cites"><span class="cite">Global ab initio neutrino mass limits from neutrinoless double-beta decay — <a href="https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.09288">arXiv</a> · <a href="https://inspirehep.net/literature/3166340">INSPIRE</a> · <a href="https://doi.org/10.1103/rsw5-57fz">DOI</a></span></span></li>
<li><p>This review summarises the properties of KM3-230213A, the ultra-high-energy neutrino event detected by KM3NeT with an inferred parent-neutrino energy of order 10^17 eV, and surveys interpretations including active galactic nuclei, transient sources and cosmogenic neutrinos. Despite extensive follow-up, no unambiguous electromagnetic counterpart has been identified.</p><span class="cites"><span class="cite">KM3-230213A: The Highest-Energy Cosmic Neutrino — <a href="https://inspirehep.net/literature/3203436">INSPIRE</a> · <a href="https://doi.org/10.53941/pac.2026.100009">DOI</a></span></span></li>
<li><p>TAMBO, a mountain-based neutrino observatory sited in a deep valley, is presented as a project to help map the high-energy neutrino sky, a goal that has remained only partially achieved a decade into the field of neutrino astronomy. The authors note its potential to also turn up clues of new physics.</p><span class="cites"><span class="cite">Measuring the high-energy neutrino sky using the deep-valley neutrino observatory TAMBO — <a href="https://inspirehep.net/literature/3185371">INSPIRE</a> · <a href="https://doi.org/10.1038/s41550-026-02916-4">DOI</a></span></span></li>
<li><p>A full many-body calculation of neutrino helicity oscillations — the mixing between left- and right-handed states enabled by a nonzero neutrino rest mass — finds spin-flip probabilities that can exceed mean-field estimates by orders of magnitude in dense neutrino environments. The enhancement traces to many-body momentum exchange, an effect missed by calculations that keep only forward-scattering processes.</p><span class="cites"><span class="cite">Neutrino helicity oscillations in astrophysical environments: A many-body approach — <a href="https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.31242">arXiv</a> · <a href="https://inspirehep.net/literature/3163036">INSPIRE</a> · <a href="https://doi.org/10.1103/2p4r-f1gs">DOI</a></span></span></li>
<li><p>This study assesses how neutrinos from past Galactic core-collapse supernovae could be detected as nuclear recoil tracks preserved in ancient minerals, so-called paleodetectors. It accounts for diverse collapse outcomes, including variation in neutron star mass and failed supernovae forming black holes, and the nuclear equation of state, finding sensitivity enhanced for more compact remnants.</p><span class="cites"><span class="cite">Paleodetectors for neutrino signals from diverse Galactic stellar collapses — <a href="https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.26826">arXiv</a> · <a href="https://inspirehep.net/literature/3184513">INSPIRE</a> · <a href="https://doi.org/10.1103/gys2-q6bk">DOI</a></span></span></li>
