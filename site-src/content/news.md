<div class="stamp stamp--no">DUNE</div>
<p>DUNE, led by Fermilab, has been included in a line-up of the most ambitious international science projects. The collaboration is preparing for data analysis by developing AI-based automated triggers that sort through detector data, identify neutrino interactions, flag possible signs of a stellar explosion, and detect problems inside the detector. Rice University's DUNE-TECH programme is separately introducing undergraduates to the science behind the experiment.</p>

<p>The collaboration has commissioned and characterised the Time-of-Flight detector for the T2K ND280 Upgrade, comprising six panels of plastic-scintillator bars surrounding the Super Fine-Grained Detector and the High-Angle Time Projection Chambers. <div class="stamp stamp--no">MicroBooNE cross sections</div>
<p>MicroBooNE has reported the first measurement of flux-integrated differential cross sections in kinematic imbalance variables for electron neutrino interactions on argon, using its full dataset of 1.3×10^21 protons on target from the Booster Neutrino Beam. The variables are sensitive to Fermi motion, multinucleon processes and final-state interactions in neutrino-nucleus scattering.</p>

<li><p>The first combined oscillation analysis of atmospheric neutrino data from Super-Kamiokande, IceCube-DeepCore and KM3NeT/ORCA together with reactor data from Daya Bay fits over 839,000 events with a unified physics model and finds no significant tensions between datasets.
