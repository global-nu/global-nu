<div class="stamp stamp--no">Baikal-GVD point sources</div>
<p>Baikal-GVD is a cubic-kilometre-scale detector under construction in Lake Baikal and is now 70% complete. A search for point-like astrophysical sources used a 5-year track-like dataset collected in 2019-2024. No significant source was found, so upper limits were computed for selected candidate objects and compared with published IceCube, ANTARES and KM3NeT results.</p>

<div class="stamp stamp--no">T2K near detector upgrade</div>
<p>The ND280 upgrade of the T2K near detector was completed in 2024, and its leading role is to constrain neutrino-nucleus interaction modelling, a leading systematic uncertainty in T2K oscillation analyses. A new event selection targets muon-neutrino charged-current interactions with single positively charged pion production in the new modules. A separate paper proposes a neural network to distinguish electrons, photons, muons, pions and protons in the SuperFGD component.</p>
