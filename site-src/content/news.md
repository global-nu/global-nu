<div class="stamp stamp--no">T2K ToF detector</div>
<p>The T2K collaboration has reported the commissioning and performance of the new Time-of-Flight detector built as part of the ND280 upgrade. Comprising plastic-scintillator bars read out at both ends by silicon photomultipliers and digitised with SAMPIC waveform electronics, the detector is intended to reduce systematic uncertainties in oscillation measurements and improve sensitivity to the CP-violating phase δCP by providing timing and directional information that rejects backgrounds entering the tracker.</p>

<li><p>This work presents the first combined oscillation analysis of atmospheric neutrino data from Super-Kamiokande, IceCube-DeepCore and KM3NeT/ORCA together with reactor data from Daya Bay, a combination previously thought infeasible outside the individual collaborations.
