<div class="stamp stamp--no">PandaX-4T quadruple beta decay</div>
<p>PandaX-4T searched for neutrinoless quadruple beta decay of xenon-136 using 148.4 kg·yr of exposure collected during commissioning and the first science runs. No significant excess over background was seen, and the collaboration set a lower limit on the decay half-life of 6.01×10^24 years at 90% confidence, the most stringent constraint on this process in xenon to date.</p>
