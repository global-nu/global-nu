<p>The collaboration reported an improved measurement of oscillation parameters and an indication of the neutrino mass ordering. <div class="stamp stamp--no">Baikal-GVD point sources</div>
<p>Baikal-GVD is a cubic-kilometre-scale detector under construction in Lake Baikal and is now 70% complete. A search for point-like astrophysical sources used a 5-year track-like dataset from 2019-2024. No significant source was found, and upper limits were computed for a selection of candidate objects. <div class="stamp stamp--no">MicroBooNE proton cross sections</div>
<p>MicroBooNE presented the first double-differential cross sections for mesonless muon-neutrino charged-current interactions on argon as a function of proton multiplicity and kinematics. The analysis uses the full Booster Neutrino Beam dataset, corresponding to 1.30 × 10²¹ protons on target. Cross sections were measured for up to the fourth-leading proton, among other variables.</p>

<div class="stamp stamp--no">MINERvA superscaling</div>
<p>MINERvA reported the first measurement of the superscaling variable reconstructed from visible final-state kinematics in neutrino-nucleus scattering. The sample is charged-current quasielastic-like muon-neutrino interactions on carbon in the medium-energy NuMI beam. The variable is used as an externally calibrated quantity whose peak position is sensitive to the invisible nuclear missing energy.</p>

<li><p>This paper presents what it describes as the first combined oscillation analysis of recent atmospheric datasets from Super-Kamiokande, IceCube-DeepCore and KM3NeT/ORCA, together with Daya Bay reactor data. Such combinations were long considered infeasible outside the collaborations. A unified model fits all datasets without significant parameter tensions and prefers normal over inverted ordering at 3σ.</p><span class="cites"><span class="cite">Atmospheric neutrino oscillations: The full picture — <a href="https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.09714">arXiv</a> · <a href="https://inspirehep.net/literature/3166344">INSPIRE</a> · <a href="https://doi.org/10.1103/v4hl-pbpn">DOI</a></span></span></li>
<li><p>The paper derives global Bayesian limits on Majorana neutrino masses from neutrinoless double-beta decay searches, using nuclear matrix elements computed ab initio from chiral effective field theory. In contrast to nuclear models, the ab initio results indicate that current-generation experiments have likely not yet reached the sensitivities required to probe the mass region it considers. This matters for how existing 0νββ limits are translated into neutrino-mass constraints.</p><span class="cites"><span class="cite">Global ab initio neutrino mass limits from neutrinoless double-beta decay — <a href="https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.09288">arXiv</a> · <a href="https://inspirehep.net/literature/3166340">INSPIRE</a> · <a href="https://doi.org/10.1103/rsw5-57fz">DOI</a></span></span></li>
<li><p>The authors calculate two-neutrino double-beta decay rates without the usual approximations that decouple nuclear structure from lepton kinematics. They compare the resulting rates and electron spectra with closure, non-closure and Taylor-expansion methods for ⁸²Se and ¹³⁶Xe. A more realistic treatment of the 2νββ spectrum is relevant to the background modelling of searches for the neutrinoless mode.</p><span class="cites"><span class="cite">Direct calculation of the two-neutrino double-beta decay rate including the full … — <a href="https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.31107">arXiv</a> · <a href="https://inspirehep.net/literature/3174998">INSPIRE</a> · <a href="https://doi.org/10.1140/epjc/s10052-026-16356-x">DOI</a></span></span></li>
<li><p>The paper asks whether nuclear recoil tracks in ancient minerals could record neutrinos from past Galactic core-collapse supernovae. It varies neutron-star masses, includes failed supernovae that form black holes, and considers the role of the nuclear equation of state. Sensitivity is enhanced for models with larger neutrino emission, so paleodetectors depend on the collapse outcome.</p><span class="cites"><span class="cite">Paleodetectors for neutrino signals from diverse Galactic stellar collapses — <a href="https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.26826">arXiv</a> · <a href="https://inspirehep.net/literature/3184513">INSPIRE</a> · <a href="https://doi.org/10.1103/gys2-q6bk">DOI</a></span></span></li>

This differs from canonical seesaw-based leptogenesis because of the scalar quadruplet in the model.</p><span class="cites"><span class="cite">Thermal leptogenesis in the Babu-Nandi-Tavartkiladze model of neutrino mass — <a href="https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.01890">arXiv</a> · <a href="https://inspirehep.net/literature/3186500">INSPIRE</a> · <a href="https://doi.org/10.1103/lj7k-5c1q">DOI</a></span></span></li>
