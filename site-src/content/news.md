<div class="stamp stamp--no">IceCube decoherence search</div>
<p>A new IceCube analysis searches for quantum-gravity-induced decoherence of neutrino flavour oscillations. It uses 368,071 atmospheric neutrinos between 0.5 and 100 TeV, an order of magnitude higher in energy than any previous decoherence search. The collaboration found no evidence for decoherence and sets what it describes as the strongest constraints to date.</p>
<div class="cites"><span class="cite">Planck-Scale Constraints on Quantum Gravity-Induced Neutrino Decoherence with IceCube — <a href="https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.14144">arXiv</a></span></div>

<div class="stamp stamp--no">Combined atmospheric fit</div>
<p>A joint analysis combines public atmospheric data from Super-Kamiokande (all stages before the gadolinium phase), IceCube-DeepCore and the six-detection-unit configuration of KM3NeT/ORCA. All event samples are fitted simultaneously, with shared systematic uncertainties treated as fully correlated and detector-response uncertainties modelled independently per experiment. The paper is titled as evidence for normal neutrino ordering and also quotes constraints on the atmospheric oscillation parameters.</p>
<div class="cites"><span class="cite">Evidence for Normal Neutrino Ordering with Combined Atmospheric Neutrino Experiments — <a href="https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.12208">arXiv</a></span></div>

<div class="stamp stamp--no">Baikal-GVD point sources</div>
<p>Baikal-GVD is a cubic-kilometre-scale detector under construction in Lake Baikal and is now 70% complete. A search for point-like astrophysical sources in its 2019–2024 track-like dataset found no significant source, so upper limits are computed for selected candidate objects. These are compared with published results from IceCube, ANTARES and KM3NeT.</p>

<div class="stamp stamp--no">T2K SuperFGD identification</div>
<p>SuperFGD is a highly granular scintillator target at the heart of the upgraded ND280 near detector of T2K. A published paper proposes a deep neural network with long short-term memory and a permutation-invariant feature encoder to distinguish electrons, photons, muons, pions and protons in it. The stated motivation is that precise particle identification is needed to reconstruct neutrino interactions and reduce systematic uncertainties in T2K's oscillation measurements.</p>

<li><p>The paper computes the neutrino emissivity of Δ-direct-Urca processes in neutron-star cores, where Δ isobars may accompany nucleons and hyperons.
