<div class="stamp stamp--no">IceCube tests extra dimensions</div>
<p>IceCube analysed 10.7 years of upward-going muon neutrino data, from 0.5 to 100 TeV, looking for the spectral distortions that low-scale-gravity models proposed to solve the hierarchy problem would imprint as neutrinos cross the Earth. No such distortion was found, and the collaboration used the result to constrain large-extra-dimension scenarios, including the compactification scale.</p>

<div class="stamp stamp--no">Super-K proton decay limits</div>
<p>Using the full pure-water phase of Super-Kamiokande, a 401 kiloton-year exposure, the collaboration searched for proton decay to a charged lepton plus an invisible massless particle and saw no signal beyond the expected atmospheric neutrino background. The analysis set new lower limits on the proton partial lifetime of 1.72×10^33 years for decay to a positron and 0.61×10^33 years for decay to a muon, improving on prior limits by factors of 2 and 1.5 respectively.</p>

<li><p>The paper lays out nuSCOPE, a proposed short-baseline experiment at CERN building on the ENUBET and NuTAG monitored-beam concept, which tags and monitors neutrinos so the muon and electron neutrino flux is exceptionally well controlled and the neutrino energy can be reconstructed event by event.
