<div class="stamp stamp--no">SBND detector paper</div>
<p>The SBND Collaboration has released a paper describing the as-built detector and the performance of the first year of beam operation. SBND is a 112 tonne active-mass liquid argon time projection chamber in the Booster Neutrino Beam at Fermilab, and began operation in 2024. The collaboration reports that all detector systems performed at or above design requirements. The electron lifetime exceeded 30 ms, ten times the design requirement, for more than 95% of Run 1.</p>

<div class="stamp stamp--no">IceCube decoherence search</div>
<p>IceCube searched for quantum-gravity-induced decoherence of neutrino flavour oscillations using 368,071 atmospheric neutrinos between 0.5 and 100 TeV. The collaboration states this energy range is an order of magnitude higher than in any previous decoherence search. It finds no evidence for decoherence and sets what it describes as the strongest constraints to date on Planck-scale effects.</p>
<div class="cites"><span class="cite">Planck-Scale Constraints on Quantum Gravity-Induced Neutrino Decoherence with IceCube — <a href="https://arxiv.org/abs/2610.14144">arXiv</a></span></div>

<div class="stamp stamp--no">Joint atmospheric fit</div>
<p>A combined analysis uses public atmospheric-neutrino data from Super-Kamiokande (all stages before the gadolinium phase), IceCube-DeepCore and the six-detection-unit configuration of KM3NeT/ORCA. All event samples are fitted simultaneously, with shared systematic uncertainties treated as fully correlated and detector-response uncertainties modelled independently per experiment. The title of the paper states that the combination gives evidence for normal neutrino ordering.</p>

<div class="stamp stamp--no">Baikal-GVD point sources</div>
<p>Baikal-GVD is a cubic-kilometre-scale detector under construction in Lake Baikal. It has taken data in partial configurations since 2016 and is now 70% complete. A search for point-like astrophysical sources in a five-year track-like dataset from 2019-2024 found no significant source, so upper limits are given for selected candidate objects. These are compared with published results from IceCube, ANTARES and KM3NeT.</p>
