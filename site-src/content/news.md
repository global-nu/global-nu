<div class="stamp stamp--no">IceCube source searches</div>
<p>The collaboration has published a new search for steady-state neutrino emission using DeepCore data, revisiting NGC 1068, the active galaxy for which IceCube reported evidence of high-energy neutrino emission four years ago. <div class="stamp stamp--no">T2K ND280 upgrade</div>
<p>T2K has commissioned the Time-of-Flight detector of its ND280 upgrade, six scintillator panels totalling 118 EJ-200 plastic-scintillator bars read out by silicon photomultipliers around the SuperFGD and the High-Angle Time Projection Chambers. The detector provides timing and directional information for particle identification and background rejection, part of the effort to reduce systematic uncertainties on oscillation parameters including the CP-violating phase.</p>

<div class="stamp stamp--no">MicroBooNE cross sections</div>
<p>MicroBooNE has reported the first measurement of flux-integrated cross sections for electron-neutrino interactions on argon as a function of kinematic imbalance variables, using its full dataset of 1.3×10^21 protons on target from Fermilab's Booster Neutrino Beam. These variables are sensitive to nuclear effects such as Fermi motion, multinucleon processes and final-state interactions.</p>

<div class="stamp stamp--no">Solar neutrinos outlook</div>
<p>A review argues that reactor antineutrino measurements have now pinned down the oscillation parameters governing solar-neutrino flavour conversion so precisely that the propagation-related uncertainty is becoming negligible, opening the way to use solar neutrinos to probe the Sun itself. It notes that solar-neutrino detection still falls short, by roughly an order of magnitude, of the precision the Standard Solar Model already predicts for the dominant pp flux.</p>

<div class="stamp stamp--no">SHiP/NA67 detector design</div>
<p>The SHiP/NA67 collaboration described the detector systems planned for its experiment at CERN's HI-ECN3 beam facility, which will search for feebly interacting GeV-scale particles and perform all-flavour neutrino-physics measurements. The collaboration is currently optimising the initial configuration ahead of commissioning and first physics runs in 2032-2033.</p>

<li><p>A combined oscillation analysis unifies atmospheric-neutrino datasets from Super-Kamiokande, IceCube-DeepCore and KM3NeT/ORCA with reactor data from Daya Bay, fitting 839,048 events across 1536 bins with 91 parameters. The single physics model describes all datasets with no significant tensions and prefers the normal over the inverted mass ordering at 3σ significance, a combination previously considered infeasible outside individual collaborations.</p><span class="cites"><span class="cite">Atmospheric neutrino oscillations: The full picture — <a href="https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.09714">arXiv</a> · <a href="https://inspirehep.net/literature/3166344">INSPIRE</a> · <a href="https://doi.org/10.1103/v4hl-pbpn">DOI</a></span></span></li>
<li><p>By combining likelihood functions from a suite of neutrinoless double-beta decay experiments with nuclear matrix elements computed ab initio in the in-medium similarity renormalization group approach, this work derives global Bayesian limits on the Majorana neutrino mass.
