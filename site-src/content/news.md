<div class="stamp stamp--no">PandaX-4T quadruple beta decay</div>
<p>Using commissioning and first-science-run data from the PandaX-4T detector, an exposure of 148.4 kg·yr of xenon-136, the collaboration searched for neutrinoless quadruple beta decay and found no significant excess over background. The result sets a lower limit on the process's half-life of 6.01×10^24 years at 90% confidence, the most stringent constraint on this decay in xenon to date.</p>

<div class="stamp stamp--no">NOvA Test Beam</div>
<p>NOvA deployed a dedicated Test Beam experiment at the Fermilab Test Beam Facility, running from April 2019 to July 2022, using a 30-ton segmented liquid scintillator detector functionally identical to the NOvA near and far detectors. The measurements characterise systematic uncertainties in detector response and calibration that underpin NOvA's long-baseline oscillation measurements of mixing parameters, mass ordering and CP violation.</p>

<p>A new study quantifies how information about reactor antineutrino oscillation parameters flows from the neutrino state through to the events JUNO will observe, using quantum and classical Fisher information. The authors construct an information ladder for the JUNO configuration and find that the loss of precision at each stage depends strongly on which oscillation parameter is being measured.</p>

<li><p>This paper performs a combined oscillation fit using recent atmospheric neutrino data from Super-Kamiokande, IceCube-DeepCore and KM3NeT/ORCA together with reactor data from Daya Bay, a combination long considered infeasible outside the individual collaborations. Fitting 839,048 events across 1536 bins with 91 parameters, the unified model describes all four datasets with no significant tensions and prefers the normal over the inverted mass ordering at 3σ significance.</p><span class="cites"><span class="cite">Atmospheric neutrino oscillations: The full picture — <a href="https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.09714">arXiv</a> · <a href="https://inspirehep.net/literature/3166344">INSPIRE</a> · <a href="https://doi.org/10.1103/v4hl-pbpn">DOI</a></span></span></li>
<li><p>By combining likelihoods from a suite of neutrinoless double-beta decay experiments with nuclear matrix elements computed in an ab initio many-body approach using chiral effective field theory forces, this paper derives global Bayesian limits on the Majorana neutrino mass. <li><p>This paper introduces TAMBO, the Tau Air-shower Mountain-Based Observatory, a proposed deep-valley detector aimed at mapping the high-energy neutrino sky, an area where the field has struggled to make progress despite a decade of neutrino astronomy.
