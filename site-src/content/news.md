<div class="stamp stamp--no">Baikal-GVD point sources</div>
<p>Baikal-GVD is a cubic-kilometre-scale detector under construction in Lake Baikal and is now 70% complete, having taken data in partial configurations since 2016. A search for point-like astrophysical sources in its 2019-2024 track-like dataset found no significant source, so upper limits were computed for selected candidate objects. These are compared with published results from IceCube, ANTARES and KM3NeT.</p>

<div class="stamp stamp--no">T2K near detector upgrade</div>
<p>The leading systematic uncertainties in T2K's oscillation analysis come from modelling neutrino-nucleus interactions, which are largely constrained by measurements at the near detector ND280. The upgrade of ND280 was completed in 2024, and a new selection targets muon neutrino charged-current interactions with single positively charged pion production in the new modules. The authors present it as a step towards reducing the dominant systematic uncertainties.</p>

<li><p>The paper addresses the contribution of Δ-direct-Urca processes to neutrino emissivity in neutron stars, motivated by the possibility that massive neutron stars contain Δ isobars and hyperons.
