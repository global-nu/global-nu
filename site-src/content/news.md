<div class="stamp stamp--no">MINERvA superscaling</div>
<p>The MINERvA Collaboration reports the first measurement of the superscaling variable ψ′ in neutrino–nucleus scattering. It uses charged-current quasielastic-like νμ–carbon interactions in the medium-energy NuMI beam, with ψ′ reconstructed from visible final-state kinematics. The variable is used as an externally calibrated quantity whose peak position is sensitive to the invisible nuclear missing energy.</p>

<div class="section-head"><h2>Theory highlights</h2><p>recently published, with the links each record carries</p></div>
<ul class="list list--news">
<li><p>This paper presents what it calls the first combined oscillation analysis of recent atmospheric datasets from Super-Kamiokande, IceCube-DeepCore and KM3NeT/ORCA, together with Daya Bay reactor data. A single physics model describes all datasets with no significant parameter tensions, and the fit prefers normal over inverted ordering at 3σ. It matters because such combinations were long considered infeasible outside the collaborations.</p><span class="cites"><span class="cite">Atmospheric neutrino oscillations: The full picture — <a href="https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.09714">arXiv</a> · <a href="https://inspirehep.net/literature/3166344">INSPIRE</a> · <a href="https://doi.org/10.1103/v4hl-pbpn">DOI</a></span></span></li>
<li><p>The authors combine current neutrinoless double-beta decay results with nuclear matrix elements computed ab initio from chiral effective field theory, within a Bayesian framework. In contrast to nuclear models, the ab initio results indicate that the current generation of experiments has likely not yet reached the sensitivities required for the mass limits at stake. This bears on how the reach of running searches is interpreted.</p><span class="cites"><span class="cite">Global ab initio neutrino mass limits from neutrinoless double-beta decay — <a href="https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.09288">arXiv</a> · <a href="https://inspirehep.net/literature/3166340">INSPIRE</a> · <a href="https://doi.org/10.1103/rsw5-57fz">DOI</a></span></span></li>
<li><p>The paper studies invisible decay of ν3 into a sterile state under normal ordering, and the ability of DUNE and NOvA to constrain the decay parameter. The true value of θ23 affects the constraint significantly, and a higher-octant value improves it.</p><span class="cites"><span class="cite">Constraining invisible neutrino decay at NOνA and DUNE — <a href="https://inspirehep.net/literature/3188867">INSPIRE</a> · <a href="https://doi.org/10.1007/s12043-026-03167-8">DOI</a></span></span></li>
<li><p>The authors consider neutrinos from a protoneutron star, including nuclear scattering through the neutral Gamow-Teller current and oscillations in the outer stellar layers. They find the electron antineutrino spectrum hardens significantly, and that this is most pronounced for the inverted mass ordering. That makes the spectrum relevant to ordering-sensitive supernova neutrino detection.</p><span class="cites"><span class="cite">Magnetorotational Supernova Neutrino Spectra Versus Mass Ordering — <a href="https://inspirehep.net/literature/3207686">INSPIRE</a> · <a href="https://doi.org/10.1134/S1063779626701182">DOI</a></span></span></li>
<li><p>The paper investigates whether neutrinos from past Galactic core-collapse supernovae could leave nuclear recoil tracks in ancient minerals, known as paleodetectors. It varies neutron star masses, includes failed supernovae that form black holes, and considers the nuclear equation of state. Sensitivity is found to be enhanced for models with larger neutrino emission.</p><span class="cites"><span class="cite">Paleodetectors for neutrino signals from diverse Galactic stellar collapses — <a href="https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.26826">arXiv</a> · <a href="https://inspirehep.net/literature/3184513">INSPIRE</a> · <a href="https://doi.org/10.1103/gys2-q6bk">DOI</a></span></span></li>
<li><p>The authors study composite neutrino models in which heavy neutrinos are bound states of a near-conformal strongly coupled sector, mixing with Standard Model neutrinos through an inverse seesaw. They obtain transition dipole couplings of about 10⁻⁶–10⁻⁸ GeV⁻¹, parametrically larger than loop-level predictions in minimal Dirac or Majorana models. An event-level simulation of the production-and-decay signature is carried out.</p><span class="cites"><span class="cite">Neutrino dipole moments and radiative signatures from partial compositeness — <a href="https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.20987">arXiv</a> · <a href="https://inspirehep.net/literature/3170873">INSPIRE</a> · <a href="https://doi.org/10.1007/JHEP09(2026)234">DOI</a></span></span></li>
</ul>
