<div class="stamp stamp--no">XENONnT Solar Neutrinos</div>
<p>The XENON Collaboration reported the first measurement of solar neutrinos through elastic neutrino-electron scattering at the keV scale, using 2.46 tonne-years of exposure from XENONnT's first two science runs and covering electron recoil energies between 1 and 140 keV. <div class="stamp stamp--no">Super-K Proton Decay Search</div>
<p>Using the full 401 kiloton-year exposure of Super-Kamiokande's pure water phase, the collaboration searched for proton decay to a charged antilepton plus an invisible massless particle and found no significant signal above the atmospheric neutrino background. They set 90% confidence-level lower limits on the partial proton lifetime of 1.72 × 10^33 years for the positron channel and 0.61 × 10^33 years for the muon channel, improving on previous limits by factors of 2 and 1.5 respectively.</p>

<div class="stamp stamp--no">HAWC Anomalous Events</div>
<p>An independent analysis examined two track-like events reported by the HAWC Collaboration in a search for Earth-skimming neutrinos, pointing back to the Pico de Orizaba volcano, and found them kinematically inconsistent with all identified backgrounds using published HAWC information. The analysis places the in-tank energy deposit above roughly 190-210 GeV, or a worst case of 122-136 GeV, exceeding the 100 GeV maximum expected from backgrounds.</p>

<div class="stamp stamp--no">ARA Diffuse UHE Search</div>
<p>The Askaryan Radio Array presented its first array-wide diffuse search for ultra-high-energy neutrinos, using data collected from January 2013 to December 2023 across its five autonomous South Pole stations, the largest livetime of any in-ice radio array. The analysis incorporates improved detector characterization, data-driven noise and electronics models, and updated interaction and lepton-propagation modelling.</p>

<div class="stamp stamp--no">IceCube Extra Dimensions</div>
<p>Using 10.7 years of upward-going muon neutrino data between 0.5 and 100 TeV, IceCube searched for the spectral distortions that low-scale gravity theories addressing the hierarchy problem predict from matter effects as neutrinos cross the Earth. The analysis placed constraints on the compactification scale in large extra dimension models.</p>
