<div class="stamp stamp--no">Super-Kamiokande proton decay limit</div>
<p>Using the full 401 kiloton-year exposure of its pure-water phase, Super-Kamiokande searched for proton decay into a charged antilepton plus an invisible massless particle and found no significant signal above the expected atmospheric-neutrino background. The resulting 90% confidence lower limits on the proton partial lifetime, 1.72×10^33 years for the positron channel and 0.61×10^33 years for the muon channel, improve on previous limits by factors of 2 and 1.5 respectively.</p>

<div class="stamp stamp--no">ARA diffuse UHE search</div>
<p>The Askaryan Radio Array reported its first array-wide diffuse search for ultra-high-energy neutrinos, combining data from all five autonomous South Pole stations taken between January 2013 and December 2023, the largest livetime accumulated by any in-ice radio array. The analysis used improved detector characterisation and updated neutrino interaction and propagation modelling.</p>
