<li><p>Combines results from a suite of neutrinoless double-beta decay experiments with nuclear matrix elements computed ab initio from chiral effective field theory in a Bayesian framework to set global limits on the Majorana neutrino mass. In contrast to phenomenological nuclear models, the ab initio treatment indicates that the current generation of 0νββ experiments has likely not yet reached the sensitivity needed to probe the mass region implied by oscillation data. This is a caution for comparing double-beta decay limits derived from different nuclear-structure inputs.</p><span class="cites"><span class="cite">Global ab initio neutrino mass limits from neutrinoless double-beta decay — <a href="https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.09288">arXiv</a> · <a href="https://inspirehep.net/literature/3166340">INSPIRE</a> · <a href="https://doi.org/10.1103/rsw5-57fz">DOI</a></span></span></li>
<li><p>Describes the Tau Air-shower Mountain-Based Observatory (TAMBO), a mountain-based instrument designed to help map the high-energy neutrino sky, an area the field has struggled to complete despite a decade of progress in neutrino astronomy. The authors present TAMBO as a route to filling in that map and to searching for signs of new physics.</p><span class="cites"><span class="cite">Measuring the high-energy neutrino sky using the deep-valley neutrino observatory TAMBO — <a href="https://inspirehep.net/literature/3185371">INSPIRE</a> · <a href="https://doi.org/10.1038/s41550-026-02916-4">DOI</a></span></span></li>
<li><p>Uses the GENIE event generator to test a range of pionless charged-current interaction models, including different nucleon form factors, quasielastic cross-section treatments and nuclear-ground-state prescriptions, against recent MicroBooNE measurements on argon. The comparison finds strong separation between competing models in some cases, giving neutrino experiments a clearer basis for choosing an interaction model.</p><span class="cites"><span class="cite">Benchmarking state-of-the-art theory and empirical models of pionless neutrino-argon … — <a href="https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.14196">arXiv</a> · <a href="https://inspirehep.net/literature/3155411">INSPIRE</a> · <a href="https://doi.org/10.1016/j.physletb.2026.140750">DOI</a></span></span></li>
<li><p>Revisits accretion-disk winds from active galactic nuclei as a possible source of the cosmic neutrino and gamma-ray backgrounds, building a lepto-hadronic wind model calibrated against radio and GeV gamma-ray data from nearby Seyfert galaxies detected by Fermi-LAT. Cosmic rays accelerated at the wind-driven shocks are shown to produce hadronic neutrino emission alongside the multiwavelength electromagnetic signal, offering a testable astrophysical origin for part of the diffuse neutrino flux.</p><span class="cites"><span class="cite">Revisiting Disk Winds in Active Galactic Nuclei as an Origin of Cosmic Gamma-Ray and … — <a href="https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.17490">arXiv</a> · <a href="https://inspirehep.net/literature/3169314">INSPIRE</a> · <a href="https://doi.org/10.3847/1538-4357/ae7d28">DOI</a></span></span></li>
