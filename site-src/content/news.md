<div class="stamp stamp--no">IceCube Earth Probe</div>
<p>A new study examines how the IceCube Upgrade, a densely instrumented central region deployed during the 2025-26 polar season, could use atmospheric neutrino oscillations to probe Earth matter effects and constrain features of the Preliminary Reference Earth Model. Lowering the detector's energy threshold for multi-GeV atmospheric neutrinos sharpens this sensitivity to the planet's interior structure.
