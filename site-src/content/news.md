<p>The analysis uses 207.2 live days and 8,294 inverse beta-decay candidates at an average reactor baseline of 52.5 km. <div class="stamp stamp--no">Super-Kamiokande reactor antineutrinos</div>
<p>Super-Kamiokande entered the SK-Gd phase in 2020, which improves its sensitivity to low-energy electron antineutrinos through neutron capture on gadolinium. Using 411.52 live days, the collaboration found prompt spectra and monthly rates consistent with the oscillated reactor prediction after accounting for accidental, geoneutrino and spallation backgrounds. The no-reactor hypothesis is disfavoured with a signal significance of approximately 6σ, which the paper presents as the first observation of reactor antineutrinos at the detector.</p>

<div class="stamp stamp--no">MicroBooNE proton kinematics</div>
<p>The collaboration presented the first double-differential cross sections for muon-neutrino charged-current interactions on argon with no mesons and one or more protons.
