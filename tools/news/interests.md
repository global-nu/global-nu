# What this site's digest is looking for

Ranked by the keyword scoring in `config.yaml`; this file is the prose version
of the same intent, and is what a human should read before changing the lists.

**Core.** Three-flavour oscillations and global analyses; the oscillation
parameters and their determination; mass ordering; the CP phase; the θ₂₃
octant.

**Experiments.** Reactor (JUNO, Daya Bay, RENO, Double Chooz, SNO+),
accelerator (DUNE, Hyper-K, T2K, NOvA), atmospheric and astrophysical
(Super-K, IceCube, KM3NeT), solar (Borexino), absolute mass (KATRIN) and
neutrinoless double beta decay (LEGEND, nEXO, KamLAND-Zen).

**Theory.** Neutrino masses and mixing, seesaw mechanisms, flavour and modular
symmetries, leptogenesis, non-standard interactions, sterile neutrinos,
neutrinos in cosmology.

**Not this site's subject.** Collider physics without a neutrino connection,
dark matter unless it bears on neutrino observables, instrumentation papers
with no physics result.
